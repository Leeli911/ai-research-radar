## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Toward Calibrated Mixture-of-Experts Under Distribution Shift
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20544
Year / Venue: 2026, ICML 2026 / arXiv
Source: official arXiv record
Research line: calibrated conditional routing under distribution shift
Research function: Explanation / Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor and Recommended for paper card

#### English Summary

- Problem: mixture-of-experts models can improve specialization, but hard routing can become unreliable under distribution shift because router confidence and expert assignment may be miscalibrated.
- Method: the paper introduces calibrated MoE variants that replace brittle hard routing with calibrated soft routing and uncertainty-aware expert combination, aiming to make specialization more reliable when the test distribution differs from training.
- Evaluation: the paper evaluates in-distribution and shifted settings, comparing standard MoE routing with calibrated variants using accuracy and calibration-focused metrics.
- Findings: calibrated routing improves reliability under shift by reducing overconfident expert assignment. The central insight is that conditional specialization must be evaluated as a calibration and routing-risk problem, not only as an accuracy improvement.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 conditional routing 在 distribution shift 下容易失准的问题。MoE 看起来像是更灵活的模型，但如果 router 在新分布上过度自信，就会把样本送到不合适的 expert。
- 为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 中 gender-conditional cascaded model 很接近。我的 cascade 不是 MoE，但同样依赖先做一个条件判断，再进入不同预测路径；一旦上游 route 不稳，下游 age estimation 会被放大影响。
- 技术直觉: 关键不是简单增加 expert，而是让 routing 本身可校准、可不确定、可平滑。对我的研究来说，这提示我把 cascade fragility 看成 routing calibration under historical shift，而不是只看最终 MAE。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: a MoE can have good average accuracy while a shifted subset is routed to the wrong expert with high confidence. This mirrors my thesis finding that global MAE can hide elderly-group degradation.
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits may be routed incorrectly or handled by a path that has weak elderly support. The paper suggests checking whether elderly failures are concentrated in high-confidence route assignments.
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing may change expert or route exposure without fixing router calibration. This helps reinterpret why data balancing in my thesis could fail or backfire for elderly samples.
- Cascade fragility: this is the strongest connection. My gender-conditional cascade can be reframed as a hard-routed expert system whose reliability depends on upstream route calibration under historical shift.
- Historical domain shift: archive, era, image quality, and demographic composition can shift router behavior. A calibrated routing analysis could test whether historical portraits trigger unstable conditional paths.

#### Limitation

The paper studies MoE-style classification settings rather than facial age regression. I would need to translate its routing-calibration idea to regression heads, gender-conditioned age routes, and subgroup residual diagnostics.

#### Follow-up

Reanalyze my cascaded model by measuring gender-router confidence, route disagreement, elderly MAE by route-confidence bin, and residual skew under archive or era splits.

### Paper 2

Title: Multicenter validation and updating of the ELDER-ICU model for severity assessment in elderly critical illness
Link: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-02472-1
Year / Venue: 2026, npj Digital Medicine
Source: official Springer Nature article page
Research line: elderly-cohort clinical risk validation under temporal and geographic shift
Research function: Diagnosis / Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference with strong proposal citation value

#### English Summary

- Problem: severity assessment models for older ICU patients must remain reliable across hospitals, time periods, and care environments; average internal performance is not enough for elderly critical illness decision support.
- Method: the paper externally validates and updates the ELDER-ICU severity model across multicenter elderly ICU cohorts, comparing model performance to existing ICU severity scores and using updating/recalibration to improve transportability.
- Evaluation: the study uses multicenter retrospective data for patients aged 65 and older, with temporal and geographic validation cohorts. It evaluates discrimination, calibration, clinical usefulness, and model updating under real deployment-style shift.
- Findings: ELDER-ICU shows strong external performance relative to conventional severity scores, but validation and updating remain necessary because elderly critical-care populations differ across sites and time periods.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 elderly ICU severity model 能否跨医院、跨时间、跨地理环境稳定使用的问题。它不是只在一个内部数据集上报高分，而是强调 external validation 和 updating。
- 为什么重要: 我的 thesis 中最脆弱的群体正是 elderly group。这个 paper 提供了一个直接的 elderly reliability 场景，说明老年群体模型不能只看整体指标，也不能假设一个开发集结果可以迁移到所有部署环境。
- 技术直觉: 模型可靠性要经过 temporal/geographic validation，再根据目标环境做 recalibration 或 updating。对我的历史人像研究来说，archive period 和 source 就像不同医院和时间段，需要单独检查 elderly reliability。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: internal or pooled performance can hide site-specific or time-specific degradation. This parallels global MAE hiding elderly residual patterns in my thesis.
- Elderly-group degradation: the whole paper treats elderly patients as the target reliability population. It strengthens my framing that elderly-group performance deserves primary evaluation rather than an appendix table.
- Balancing trade-offs: model updating is a more targeted intervention than naive balancing. This helps me distinguish between changing sample counts and correcting target-domain calibration or transportability.
- Cascade fragility: severity scores and updated models show that risk pipelines need validation after each modeling step. My cascaded model should similarly be evaluated route by route and updated only when the target split supports it.
- Historical domain shift: temporal and geographic ICU validation maps naturally to historical portrait period/source validation. I can treat archive splits as deployment environments rather than random folds.

#### Limitation

The task is clinical severity classification/risk assessment, not visual regression. Its value for my work is the deployment-validation design and elderly-specific reliability framing, not the exact ICU features.

#### Follow-up

Adapt its validation logic to my thesis: compare random validation, source-held-out validation, period-held-out validation, and elderly-specific recalibration for facial age estimation.

### Paper 3

Title: Fair and robust early readmission risk prediction in primary care using MedFair diffusion-based data augmentation and causal-invariant domain generalization
Link: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-50146-5
Year / Venue: 2026, Scientific Reports
Source: official Springer Nature article page
Research line: fairness-aware data augmentation and causal-invariant prediction under cross-racial domain shift
Research function: Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference

#### English Summary

- Problem: readmission risk models can perform unevenly across racial groups and deployment contexts, especially when training data imbalance and domain-specific correlations shape the learned risk signal.
- Method: the paper combines MedFair diffusion-based data augmentation with causal-invariant domain generalization, aiming to improve subgroup fairness and robustness by generating better minority-support data while encouraging invariant risk features.
- Evaluation: the study evaluates early readmission prediction in primary care with attention to fairness, robustness, calibration, and cross-racial generalization.
- Findings: the paper reports improved fair and robust readmission prediction when data augmentation is paired with causal-invariant representation learning. The main value is the combined intervention logic: synthetic balancing alone is not enough unless the model also learns stable causal features.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文研究 readmission prediction 在不同 race/domain 下的公平性和鲁棒性问题。它不只是补数据，而是把 diffusion-based augmentation 和 causal-invariant learning 结合起来。
- 为什么重要: 这正好对应我的 balancing trade-off 观察。我的 thesis 里 balancing 有时让 elderly group 更差，说明补齐数量本身不一定解决隐藏的 reliability 问题。
- 技术直觉: 如果生成数据只是改变表面分布，模型仍可能学到 domain-specific shortcut。把 augmentation 和 causal-invariant representation 结合，等于同时处理 sample support 和 stable signal，这对 historical portraits 中 source/era artifact 很有启发。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: overall readmission performance can improve while fairness or calibration across racial groups remains weak. This mirrors average MAE hiding elderly degradation.
- Elderly-group degradation: race in the paper is not age, but the same logic applies to any vulnerable subgroup whose training support is limited or confounded. Elderly portraits may need support-aware intervention plus invariant feature checks.
- Balancing trade-offs: this is the key connection. The paper suggests that balancing or augmentation should be paired with a mechanism that discourages shortcut learning; otherwise, extra samples may not improve vulnerable-group reliability.
- Cascade fragility: if generated or reweighted data changes subgroup distributions, a conditional model may shift route behavior in unstable ways. This reinforces the need to evaluate cascade paths after data interventions.
- Historical domain shift: causal-invariant learning can be adapted to separate age-relevant facial cues from era/source/image-quality artifacts in historical portraits.

#### Limitation

The diffusion component may be heavier than my immediate thesis-extension needs. For my proposal, the transferable idea is not the specific generator but the claim that data augmentation must be audited together with invariant representation and subgroup calibration.

#### Follow-up

Use this as a warning reference when discussing synthetic augmentation for historical portraits: generated elderly samples should be evaluated by source-held-out elderly MAE, calibration, residual skew, and representation shortcut tests.

## 3. Two Research Questions

Each question is specific, testable, technically plausible, and grounded in my thesis observations.

### Question 1

English:

- Research question: Under historical portrait source and period shift, does route calibration explain why a gender-conditional cascaded age model degrades more for elderly samples than a simpler regression model?
- Why it matters: my thesis found cascade fragility and elderly-group degradation, but the failure mechanism was not yet isolated.
- Possible data: thesis portrait images, age labels, gender labels, model outputs, gender-router confidence, age-head predictions, residuals, archive/source metadata, period labels, and image-quality proxies.
- Possible method: compare simple regression, multitask, and cascaded models across random, source-held-out, and period-held-out splits; stratify elderly residuals by router confidence and route disagreement; evaluate whether calibration-aware soft routing reduces elderly error.
- Evaluation metric: global MAE, elderly MAE, worst age-gender-source MAE, route ECE, route-confidence error curves, elderly underestimation rate, residual variance, and bootstrap confidence intervals.
- Thesis connection: directly tests aggregate metric masking, elderly degradation, cascade fragility, balancing trade-offs through route exposure, and historical domain shift.
- Risk or limitation: gender labels and route confidence may not fully explain elderly errors if image quality or age-label noise is the dominant source of degradation.

中文:

- 研究问题: 在 historical portrait source 和 period shift 下，route calibration 是否能解释为什么 gender-conditional cascaded age model 比 simple regression model 更容易在 elderly samples 上退化？
- 为什么重要: 我的 thesis 已经观察到 cascade fragility 和 elderly-group degradation，但还没有把失败机制明确拆出来。
- 可能数据: thesis portrait images、age/gender labels、gender-router confidence、age-head predictions、residuals、archive/source metadata、period labels 和 image-quality proxies。
- 可能方法: 比较 simple regression、multitask 和 cascaded models，在 random/source-held-out/period-held-out splits 上评估；按 router confidence 和 route disagreement 分层检查 elderly residuals；测试 calibration-aware soft routing 是否降低 elderly error。
- 评估指标: global MAE、elderly MAE、worst age-gender-source MAE、route ECE、route-confidence error curves、elderly underestimation rate、residual variance 和 bootstrap confidence intervals。
- Thesis 连接: 这个问题直接连接 aggregate metric masking、elderly degradation、cascade fragility、balancing trade-offs 和 historical domain shift。
- 风险: 如果 image quality 或 age-label noise 才是主要原因，gender route calibration 可能只能解释一部分 elderly failure。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: Can source-aware recalibration or invariant representation learning improve elderly-group age estimation under historical archive shift more reliably than demographic balancing alone?
- Why it matters: my thesis showed that balancing sometimes worsened elderly performance, so a better intervention must distinguish sample support from stable signal.
- Possible data: thesis portraits, age/gender labels, source or period metadata, image-quality measures, balanced and unbalanced training variants, embeddings, prediction intervals, and residual diagnostics.
- Possible method: compare age balancing, source-aware recalibration, adversarial source-invariant representation learning, and a combined balancing-plus-invariance intervention; evaluate source-held-out and period-held-out elderly performance.
- Evaluation metric: elderly MAE, worst-group MAE, calibration error by age-source cell, prediction interval coverage, underestimation-rate gap, representation source-predictability, and intervention harm rate for non-elderly groups.
- Thesis connection: tests aggregate masking, elderly degradation, balancing trade-offs, historical domain shift, and whether simpler regression remains more stable than conditional or heavily augmented models.
- Risk or limitation: removing source information may also remove real age cues if historical style is partially age-informative, so invariance pressure needs sensitivity analysis.

中文:

- 研究问题: source-aware recalibration 或 invariant representation learning 能否比 demographic balancing alone 更可靠地改善 historical archive shift 下的 elderly-group age estimation？
- 为什么重要: 我的 thesis 发现 balancing 有时会恶化 elderly performance，所以更好的 intervention 必须区分 sample support 和 stable signal。
- 可能数据: thesis portraits、age/gender labels、source/period metadata、image-quality measures、balanced/unbalanced training variants、embeddings、prediction intervals 和 residual diagnostics。
- 可能方法: 比较 age balancing、source-aware recalibration、adversarial source-invariant representation learning，以及 balancing-plus-invariance；在 source-held-out 和 period-held-out elderly performance 上评估。
- 评估指标: elderly MAE、worst-group MAE、age-source calibration error、prediction interval coverage、underestimation-rate gap、representation source-predictability，以及 non-elderly groups 的 intervention harm rate。
- Thesis 连接: 这个问题检验 aggregate masking、elderly degradation、balancing trade-offs、historical domain shift，以及 simple regression 是否仍比 conditional/augmented models 更稳定。
- 风险: source information 可能也包含真实 age cues，所以 invariance 不能过强，需要 sensitivity analysis。
