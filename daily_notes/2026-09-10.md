## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Conditional Coverage Diagnostics for Conformal Prediction
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11779
Year / Venue: 2025 arXiv preprint; ICML 2026
Source: arXiv primary page and arXiv HTML
Research line: conformal prediction, conditional coverage, local reliability diagnostics, uncertainty-aware evaluation
Research function: Diagnosis, Monitoring, and Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 5; proposal citation value 5; feasibility 5; strategic long-term fit 4.
Usefulness: method reference with high strategic value

#### English Summary

- Problem: conformal prediction gives strong marginal coverage, but marginal coverage can hide poor coverage on local regions of the feature space or on unobserved subgroups. The paper asks how to diagnose these conditional failures without pretending that exact conditional coverage is easy to guarantee in finite samples.
- Method: the paper introduces a diagnostic framework based on the Excess Risk of Target Coverage (ERT), which compares local conditional coverage behavior against the desired target coverage. Instead of reporting only average coverage, it estimates where prediction sets are too narrow or unreliable.
- Evaluation: the authors evaluate the diagnostic on regression and classification settings where standard conformal procedures can satisfy marginal validity while missing local conditional failures. The experiments compare diagnostic sensitivity under different conformal methods and data-generating structures.
- Findings: marginal conformal validity is not enough for local reliability. ERT-style diagnostics can expose conditional under-coverage patterns that aggregate coverage hides, making conformal prediction more useful as an audit tool rather than only a global guarantee.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: conformal prediction 的 overall coverage 看起来达标, 但这并不说明每个 subgroup、每个局部 feature region 或每个 shifted slice 都被覆盖得足够好。它不是直接承诺完美 conditional coverage, 而是提出一种诊断思路: 去测量哪些局部区域的 coverage 风险超过目标水平。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 中 global MAE 掩盖 elderly error。这篇论文把同样的问题推进到 uncertainty evaluation: 一个 age estimator 的 overall interval coverage 可能合格, 但 elderly portraits、某个 archive source、低画质图像或 cascade route 的 interval 仍然可能 under-cover。

技术直觉: conformal prediction 不能只看 "90% coverage 是否达成"。我需要问 coverage error 是否集中在特定区域。ERT 的价值是把 hidden conditional failure 转成可比较的 local diagnostic signal, 这样我可以检查模型哪里看似有 uncertainty guarantee, 实际上却没有足够 local validity。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: marginal coverage can hide conditional failure just as global MAE hid elderly-group degradation in my thesis.
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits can be treated as a target conditional region where interval coverage, residual tails, and underestimation risk are audited separately.
- Balancing trade-offs: if balancing changes residual distributions unevenly, ERT-like diagnostics can show whether the intervention improves global coverage while worsening local elderly coverage.
- Cascade fragility: the gender-conditional cascade creates route-specific prediction regions. Conditional coverage diagnostics can test whether a route has enough local validity before being trusted.
- Historical domain shift: archive period, scan quality, restoration style, and portrait source can define shifted regions where marginal conformal validity may fail.

#### Limitation

The paper is mainly a diagnostic contribution. It helps identify conditional coverage failure, but it does not by itself solve the intervention question of how to repair every failing subgroup under small sample sizes.

#### Follow-up

Use the diagnostic idea on thesis outputs: compare marginal conformal residual intervals with age-group, source-period, and route-conditioned coverage diagnostics, then test whether elderly under-coverage appears before global MAE or global coverage changes.

### Paper 2

Title: False Confidence: Automated Labels Confound Fairness Audits in Cervical Spine Segmentation
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07852
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint
Source: arXiv primary page and arXiv HTML
Research line: medical-image segmentation, fairness auditing, label provenance, subgroup reliability
Research function: Diagnosis, Explanation, Monitoring, and Evaluation
Screening Score: 25/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 5; proposal citation value 5; feasibility 5; strategic long-term fit 5.
Usefulness: proposal anchor and Recommended for paper card

#### English Summary

- Problem: fairness audits in medical segmentation often rely on automated or proxy reference labels. If those labels encode subgroup-specific errors, the audit can report false fairness or false unfairness even when model robustness looks stable on average.
- Method: the paper compares fairness and robustness conclusions under two reference-label sources: automated SCT outputs and expert radiologist labels. It evaluates cervical-spine MRI segmentation across subgroup axes including sex and age, and it studies whether aggregation changes the fairness signal.
- Evaluation: the authors test nnU-Net segmentation on a multi-site cervical-spine MRI setting and compare Dice and Hausdorff-style robustness metrics under automated versus expert labels. They explicitly examine subgroup fairness under the two label sources.
- Findings: automated labels can confound fairness audits. Robustness measurements may stay relatively stable across label sources, but subgroup fairness conclusions can change substantially when expert labels replace automated references. Aggregating labels can mitigate some confounding but cannot remove the need to audit label provenance.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: fairness audit 的结果不仅取决于模型, 也取决于 "ground truth" 是谁给的。如果 reference label 本身来自自动模型, 而这个自动模型对某些 subgroup 更不准, 那么后续 fairness audit 就可能被污染。模型看起来公平或不公平, 可能只是因为尺子本身有偏差。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 使用 historical portrait age labels。历史数据中的年龄、性别、身份、拍摄年代和图像质量都可能带有 annotation uncertainty。对于 elderly group, label noise 或 metadata bias 可能和真实模型 error 混在一起。如果不区分 model failure 和 label-source failure, 我可能会误判 balancing、cascade 或 regression baseline 的可靠性。

技术直觉: 评价模型之前要先评价评价标准。自动标签在整体 Dice 或 robustness 指标上可能看起来稳定, 但 fairness gap 对 reference label 更敏感。换句话说, aggregate robustness 不等于 subgroup fairness audit 稳定。我的研究也需要检查 age labels、archive metadata 和 derived labels 是否会改变 subgroup conclusions。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: stable average robustness can coexist with unstable subgroup fairness conclusions, mirroring how global MAE hid elderly error.
- Elderly-group degradation: the paper directly studies age-related audit sensitivity. It warns that elderly-group failure can be exaggerated, hidden, or redirected by label-source bias.
- Balancing trade-offs: if balancing uses labels or subgroup assignments with provenance bias, it may optimize toward a distorted failure signal and worsen the real vulnerable group.
- Cascade fragility: a cascaded model can propagate upstream label or subgroup errors. If gender or age labels are noisy, route-level evaluation may become fragile before the age predictor is even assessed.
- Historical domain shift: archival labels are not neutral ground truth. Historical portrait metadata may be incomplete, socially constructed, or derived from later records, so source-aware label audit is part of distribution-shift evaluation.

#### Limitation

The domain is cervical-spine MRI segmentation rather than historical face analysis. I should transfer the audit principle, not the segmentation-specific metrics directly.

#### Follow-up

Create a label-provenance checklist for my thesis-style data: original age label source, derived age bins, missingness patterns, source archive, historical period, and whether subgroup conclusions change under alternative label assumptions.

### Paper 3

Title: Temporal Generalization: A Reality Check
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23487
Year / Venue: 2025 arXiv preprint; ICLR 2026
Source: arXiv primary page and arXiv HTML
Research line: temporal distribution shift, historical visual data, model extrapolation, evaluation baselines
Research function: Explanation, Monitoring, and Evaluation
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 4; methodological novelty 4; proposal citation value 5; feasibility 5; strategic long-term fit 4.
Usefulness: method reference for historical domain shift evaluation

#### English Summary

- Problem: many temporal generalization studies claim future-domain robustness, but evaluation protocols can overstate progress when they use weak baselines, random splits, or unrealistic assumptions about model training time.
- Method: the paper evaluates temporal shift with explicit time-ordered training and testing. It compares common temporal adaptation ideas against simple but strong baselines, including models trained on the latest available data and interpolation-based strategies.
- Evaluation: the benchmark spans domains including language, satellite imagery, and historical yearbook photo prediction. The historical image setting is especially relevant because it asks whether visual models trained on earlier periods generalize to later periods.
- Findings: temporal extrapolation is difficult, and complex temporal methods often fail to beat simple latest-data or interpolation baselines. The paper argues that realistic temporal evaluation needs stronger baselines and careful split design before claiming robustness under time shift.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: temporal generalization 论文有时看起来提升了 future performance, 但可能只是因为 baseline 太弱或 split 不够真实。它强调真正的 temporal shift evaluation 应该按时间训练和测试, 并且要和 latest available data 这样的强 baseline 比较。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 本质上就是 historical visual domain shift。历史人像随年代、摄影技术、社会身份表达、扫描质量和 archive source 改变。这个 paper 提醒我, 不能只说 "domain shift 存在", 还要设计合理的 temporal evaluation: 模型是否真的能跨时期泛化, 是否只是被随机 split 或弱 baseline 高估。

技术直觉: 时间不是普通随机噪声。未来或历史时期的分布变化可能具有方向性, 但并不保证复杂 temporal method 会更好。最简单的 recent-data baseline 或 interpolation baseline 可能比看起来更强。这与我的 thesis 中 simpler regression models sometimes generalized better 的发现直接呼应。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: random or pooled evaluation can hide period-specific degradation, just as global MAE hid elderly-specific degradation.
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits may be unevenly distributed across historical periods, so temporal shift can interact with age-group reliability.
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing across time periods or age groups may remove useful temporal structure or overweight noisy minority periods, which could explain backfire.
- Cascade fragility: temporal shift can alter upstream gender cues and downstream age residuals differently, making conditional routing brittle across eras.
- Historical domain shift: this is the most direct bridge. The paper includes historical visual prediction and gives a protocol vocabulary for evaluating period-to-period generalization.

#### Limitation

The paper is broader than subgroup reliability. Its main value for my radar is the temporal evaluation discipline and historical visual setting, not a new subgroup fairness method.

#### Follow-up

Recast my thesis experiments as temporal generalization tests: train on earlier portrait periods, validate on an intermediate period, test on later periods, and compare simple regression, balanced models, cascades, latest-period baselines, and interpolation-style baselines.

### Question 1

English:

- Research question: Can conditional coverage diagnostics detect elderly under-coverage in historical face age estimation before global MAE or marginal coverage shows degradation?
- Why it matters: my thesis found elderly underestimation and high residual variance, but it did not test whether uncertainty intervals remain valid for elderly portraits under archive and period shift.
- Possible data: thesis predictions, residuals, age labels, age groups, gender labels, source archive, historical period, model variant, cascade route, confidence scores, and image-quality proxies.
- Possible method: build split-conformal residual intervals for simple regression, balanced regression, multi-task, and cascaded models; compute marginal coverage and ERT-style conditional coverage diagnostics for elderly, source-period, and route-defined slices.
- Evaluation metric: elderly coverage deficit, worst-slice coverage deficit, interval width, global MAE, elderly MAE, residual skew, underestimation rate, and local diagnostic lead time before global degradation.
- Thesis connection: this directly extends aggregate metric masking, elderly-group degradation, cascade fragility, and historical domain shift into uncertainty-aware monitoring.
- Risk or limitation: elderly-source-period intersections may be small, so the first experiment should use prespecified coarse strata and then explore finer hidden regions cautiously.

- 重要性: 我的 thesis 发现 elderly underestimation 和 residual variance 较高, 但还没有检验 uncertainty intervals 在 archive/period shift 下是否对 elderly portraits 有效。
- 可能数据: thesis predictions、residuals、age labels、age groups、gender labels、source archive、historical period、model variant、cascade route、confidence scores 和 image-quality proxies。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: How sensitive are elderly-age reliability conclusions to label provenance and temporal split design in historical portrait data?
- Why it matters: if age labels or archive metadata are noisy in subgroup-specific ways, a model may look unfair or reliable for the wrong reason; if temporal splits are unrealistic, domain-shift conclusions may be overstated.
- Possible data: original age metadata, derived age bins, alternative age-label sources if available, source archive, capture or publication period, model predictions, residuals, and subgroup assignments.
- Possible method: repeat subgroup reliability audits under multiple label assumptions and multiple temporal split protocols: random split, source-held-out split, early-to-late split, and intermediate-period validation. Compare whether elderly degradation, balancing gains, and cascade performance rankings remain stable.
- Evaluation metric: change in elderly MAE ranking, change in worst-group residual skew, subgroup coverage gap, label-provenance sensitivity, temporal generalization gap, and whether simple regression remains the strongest baseline.
- Thesis connection: this tests whether my thesis findings are robust audit findings rather than artifacts of one label source or one evaluation split.
- Risk or limitation: alternative expert labels may not exist for historical portraits, so the first version may need sensitivity analysis with plausible label-noise models rather than full relabeling.

- 研究问题: historical portrait data 中 elderly-age reliability conclusions 对 label provenance 和 temporal split design 有多敏感?
