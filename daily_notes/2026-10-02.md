## Paper 1 — How Much Can Reliability Drift Under a Fixed Confidence Distribution?

Wenhao Liang et al. — submitted 2026-09-30. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38917v1) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38917v1).

**Research function:** Diagnosis → Monitoring.

### English Summary

- **Problem:** Confidence histograms can remain unchanged while conditional correctness deteriorates.
- **Method:** A fragility profile maximizes reliability movement under score-preserving, divergence-constrained covariate reweighting; estimation uses learned readouts and separated label roles.
- **Evaluation:** ImageNet classifiers, controlled reweightings, and natural-shift diagnostics.
- **Findings:** Controlled-shift results support the diagnostic, but the source profile does not bound total natural drift. A plug-in profile is distinct from the separately constructed lower confidence bound. [Evidence and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38917v1).

### 中文解释

论文解释“置信度分布没变，正确率为何仍会变”：同一分数下可以混合难易不同的样本，混合比例改变就会影响可靠性。它适合提醒我检查监控盲点；自然漂移的全部风险不能由该指标保证。[原文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38917v1)

### Connection to My Thesis

**Explicit Conceptual Bridge — my interpretation:** My thesis showed that global MAE concealed elderly failure. I now have a reason to audit a second aggregation axis: equal-confidence examples may conceal unequal conditional error. This classification result does not directly establish a regression guarantee.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection |
|---|---|
| Aggregate metric masking | I will check error heterogeneity within uncertainty bins as well as between demographic groups. |
| Elderly-group degradation | I will test whether older portraits have larger errors than younger portraits at similar estimated uncertainty. |
| Balancing trade-offs | I will ask whether rebalancing changes the age composition within uncertainty bins even when the marginal uncertainty histogram barely moves. |
| Cascade fragility | I will audit route-specific age errors within similar gate-confidence bands; gate confidence is not a guarantee about downstream age error. |
| Historical domain shift | I will compare within-bin group composition and within-cell error changes across archives; these are different failure mechanisms. |

**Methodological Relevance:** I can begin with fixed uncertainty bins, saved embeddings, and independent error auditing. A regression adaptation would be my own method, requiring validation.

**Reinterpretation of My Thesis Findings:** Stable confidence may coexist with elderly underestimation, so a confidence-only monitoring panel could be insufficient even before global MAE becomes visibly worse.

**中文连接：** 我把 thesis 中的平均指标盲点延伸到不确定性分箱：在相近分数下，老年样本仍可能有更大误差。平衡可能改变箱内年龄组成；级联需要分别检查门控与年龄预测；历史域变化需要区分组成变化和同类样本本身变难。这些是我的研究假设，不是论文已经验证的年龄估计结论。

**Needs full-paper verification:** Proof conditions, readout sensitivity, and whether enough independent elderly labels exist for a useful bound; I have not reproduced the estimator.

## Paper 2 — Learning the Robustness Mechanism with Bilevel Optimization

Yiyang Shen, Qihang Lin, Weiran Wang — submitted 2026-09-28. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35541v1) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35541v1).

**Research function:** Intervention → Evaluation.

### English Summary

- **Problem:** Robustness must handle both changing group proportions and changing distributions inside groups.
- **Method:** Bilevel minimax learning uses held-out data to learn robustness parameters, with variants for known and inferred training groups.
- **Evaluation:** Shifted CMNIST, CelebA, and CivilComments; average and worst-group accuracy over three runs.
- **Findings:** Table 1 shows a trade-off: on CivilComments, learnable-radius Bi-HDRO raises worst-group accuracy from GDRO's 34.8 to 59.5, while average accuracy falls from 87.8 to 78.5. The inferred-group variant still uses group-labeled validation data. [Methods and results](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35541v1).

### 中文解释

技术直觉是让验证数据决定保护强度，而不只手工设定群体权重。论文同时检验组间与组内变化；最差组改善可能伴随平均性能下降。“训练组标签缺失”也不意味着完全无需群体标注。[原文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35541v1)

### Connection to My Thesis

**Explicit Conceptual Bridge — my interpretation:** My balancing experiments changed representation counts, but counts alone need not capture differences among elderly portraits. I can separate who is present from how difficult their images are.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection |
|---|---|
| Aggregate metric masking | I will report global and worst-age-group error together when comparing intervention strengths. |
| Elderly-group degradation | I will cross age groups with image quality or archive source to expose elderly within-group variation. |
| Balancing trade-offs | I will compare fixed resampling with validation-selected weights, explicitly retaining aggregate–elderly trade-offs. |
| Cascade fragility | I will treat routing as a separate experimental factor; robust weighting alone does not establish that route errors are repaired. |
| Historical domain shift | I will separately manipulate age composition and within-age visual corruption before evaluating a held-out archive. |

**Methodological Relevance:** A modest first experiment uses frozen visual features and a small weighting grid. Full bilevel optimization is a later comparator, not a prerequisite for my question.

**Reinterpretation of My Thesis Findings:** Balancing backfire may reflect insufficient within-group coverage or unsuitable intervention strength. These remain competing hypotheses rather than a retrospective causal explanation.

**中文连接：** 我的平衡实验不能只问“老年样本够不够”，还要问“增加的是哪类老年人像”。我会同时报告整体与老年组误差，在年龄组内部按来源或图像质量细分，把路由结构作为独立因素，在历史留出域验证权重选择。论文没有直接解释我的级联失败。

**Needs full-paper verification:** Generalization assumptions, validation representativeness, and optimization restrictions before any end-to-end regression implementation; three runs are limited evidence of training stability.

## Paper 3 — Accountable and uncertainty-aware evaluation of sensor-based AI under distribution shift: devices, subjects, and nearly three years underground

Benny Platte, Rico Thomanek, Christian Roschke, Marc Ritter — submitted 2026-09-08. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09257v1) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09257v1).

**Research function:** Evaluation.

### English Summary

- **Problem:** Random splits and mean scores can obscure deployment instability.
- **Method:** Staged device, subject, and time holdouts, explicit chance references, repeated-training quantiles, and an out-of-present-scope error rate.
- **Evaluation:** Smartphone geomagnetic localization in two mines, including a 34-month revisit.
- **Findings:** A bimodal training configuration passes a mean-based assessment despite a poor lower tail. The evidence concerns training stochasticity; sparse people and walks restrict population inference. [Protocol and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09257v1).

### 中文解释

论文把“能否部署”写成有参考水平和不确定性的评估问题。多次训练的低分位表现能暴露均值掩盖的不稳定，但它不是人口子组保证。我的借鉴点是分层留出与明确评估单位，而不是矿井定位系统。[原文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09257v1)

### Connection to My Thesis

**Explicit Conceptual Bridge — my interpretation:** My conclusion that simpler regression generalized better should be checked across seeds and archives, not only by comparing one mean per architecture.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection |
|---|---|
| Aggregate metric masking | I will separate averages over people, groups, archives, and training seeds instead of calling them all mean performance. |
| Elderly-group degradation | I will keep elderly MAE distinct from the upper tail of elderly MAE across training runs. |
| Balancing trade-offs | I will test whether balancing changes both expected elderly error and run-to-run instability. |
| Cascade fragility | I will compare seed sensitivity of cascades and simple regression using matched splits and budgets. |
| Historical domain shift | I will hold out identities and archives or documented eras, rather than randomly mixing portraits from all sources. |

**Methodological Relevance:** I can reuse my evaluation panels with repeated small-head training, explicit split definitions, and separate person-sampling versus seed uncertainty.

**Reinterpretation of My Thesis Findings:** An apparently strong cascade may be unreliable because of its mean error, its unstable training, or both. I should measure these separately before selecting an explanation.

**中文连接：** 我的简单回归优势需要在不同随机种子和档案上复查。整体均值、老年组均值、以及老年组误差跨训练次数的高分位数是不同对象；平衡与级联可能分别影响它们。对误差这种越低越好的指标，我应关注高分位风险，不能机械照搬精确率的低分位规则。

**Needs full-paper verification:** Transferability of the decision rule and required run count. A small seed pilot cannot support a precise extreme-quantile claim or broad population guarantee.

## Two Research Questions / 两个研究问题

### RQ1 — Hidden error inside stable uncertainty bins

- **Research question:** Can within-uncertainty-bin age-composition changes identify archive shifts with increased elderly MAE that a marginal uncertainty monitor misses? / 当边际不确定性监控未报警时，箱内年龄组成变化能否识别老年组 MAE 上升的档案变化？
- **Why it matters:** I need to distinguish confidence stability from error stability. / 我需要区分“置信度稳定”和“误差稳定”。
- **Possible data:** My thesis portraits and saved predictions, subject to access; independent identity splits and archive metadata. True age is available in the offline audit only. / 使用可访问的 thesis 人像、预测与档案信息，按身份隔离；真实年龄仅供离线审核。
- **Possible method:** Fit a small absolute-error predictor on frozen embeddings using a separate labeled calibration split; freeze its uncertainty bins. Compare a marginal histogram monitor with a bin-by-age composition diagnostic under controlled reweighting and real archive holdouts. Fit all weights without evaluation residuals. This is my regression adaptation, not the paper's theorem. / 独立校准集拟合轻量误差预测器并固定分箱；比较边际直方图与箱内年龄组成诊断，在受控重加权和真实档案留出上验证，不能用测试残差挑权重。
- **Evaluation metric:** Detection sensitivity at a source-calibrated 5% false-alert target; elderly MAE increase, signed underestimation, effective sample size, and bootstrap intervals clustered by identity. Define degradation as at least one additional MAE year, with sensitivity analysis. / 固定源域误报目标为 5%，评估检测率、老年 MAE 增量、低估、有效样本量及身份聚类区间；预设增加一岁为退化阈值并做敏感性分析。
- **Thesis connection:** Directly tests aggregate masking and elderly underestimation. / 直接检验平均掩盖与老年低估。
- **Risk or limitation:** True age is unavailable in unlabeled deployment; predicted age groups introduce routing error. I will report oracle and predicted-group variants separately. An observational diagnostic is not a label-free guarantee or causal explanation. / 部署时真实年龄不可得；预测分组会出错，因此分别报告真实分组与预测分组结果，不宣称无标签保证或因果解释。

### RQ2 — Does balancing survive compound shift and repeated training?

- **Research question:** Does validation-selected partial balancing reduce elderly MAE more consistently than full balancing when age-composition and within-age image-quality shifts occur together? / 当年龄组成与组内图像质量同时变化时，验证集选择的部分平衡是否比完全平衡更稳定地降低老年组误差？
- **Why it matters:** My balancing backfire needs a controlled diagnosis before a more complex intervention. / 我的平衡反效果需要先做受控诊断。
- **Possible data:** Thesis portraits with age and source labels; fixed identity-disjoint splits; documented quality perturbations as a stress test, plus one untouched historical archive. / 使用带年龄与来源的人像、身份隔离划分、明确的质量扰动及未参与选择的历史档案。
- **Possible method:** Freeze the backbone; compare unweighted, fully balanced, and a preregistered five-level partial-weight grid. Select weights only on validation worst-age-group MAE with a global-MAE constraint. Cross composition-only, quality-only, and combined shifts; compare simple and conditional heads under matched seeds. Start with ten paired seeds as a variance pilot. / 冻结骨干，比较不加权、完全平衡和预设五档部分权重；仅用验证集最差年龄组误差与整体约束选权重；交叉三种漂移，并对简单头与条件头使用相同随机种子，先做十次配对试验。
- **Evaluation metric:** Paired elderly/global MAE changes, worst-group MAE, underestimation rate, and between-seed spread; report the Pareto trade-off. Avoid estimating a precise 95th percentile from ten seeds. / 比较配对老年与整体 MAE、最差组误差、低估率及种子间波动，报告权衡，不用十次训练声称精确的极端分位数。
- **Thesis connection:** Tests balancing trade-offs and cascade fragility while retaining simpler regression as a baseline. / 检验平衡代价和级联脆弱性，保留简单回归基线。
- **Risk or limitation:** Synthetic degradation is not historical change; limited elderly labels can make weight selection noisy. I will keep the archive test untouched and use identity-level intervals. / 人工扰动不等于真实历史变化，老年标签稀少也会让选权重不稳定；档案测试集保持独立，使用身份级区间。
