## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Fairness Beyond Demographics: Optimizing Performance Across Appearance-Based Hidden Cohorts in Medical Imaging
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29827
Year / Venue: 2026, arXiv
Source: arXiv official abstract
Research line: hidden-cohort fairness and latent subgroup reliability in medical imaging
Research function: Diagnosis / Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 25/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor

#### English Summary

- Problem: medical imaging models can show subgroup disparities, but fairness methods that focus only on visible attributes such as age or sex may miss appearance-based latent cohorts where model errors concentrate.
- Method: the paper proposes label-free hidden-cohort fairness training. It clusters images into appearance-based cohorts and optimizes fairness across those latent groups instead of relying only on demographic labels.
- Evaluation: the authors introduce HIDFairBench and evaluate whether hidden-cohort fairness improves both single-attribute and multi-attribute fairness without using demographic labels during training.
- Findings: optimizing over image-derived hidden cohorts improves fairness across visible demographic attributes and reduces the sparsity problem that appears when many demographic intersections are used directly.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 demographic subgroup 不是唯一可靠性单位的问题。模型可能不是简单地对 age 或 sex 不公平，而是在某些 visual appearance cohort 上失败。
- 为什么重要: 这直接挑战了只按可见人口属性做 fairness evaluation 的习惯。对于我的研究，elderly group 的失败可能只是表面现象，真正机制可能来自 image quality、era、pose、texture 或 archive style。
- 技术直觉: 先用图像表征聚类发现 latent cohorts，再把这些 cohorts 当作 fairness optimization 的单位。这样可以避免 demographic intersection 太稀疏的问题，也能发现 demographic label 看不到的 hidden reliability degradation。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 这篇论文说明 overall performance 和单一 demographic fairness report 都可能掩盖 hidden cohorts。我的 global MAE 掩盖 elderly-group failure 可以进一步扩展为: global MAE 也可能掩盖 appearance-based historical slices。
- Elderly-group degradation: 我的 elderly group 可能不是单独的年龄问题，而是 elderly portraits 与历史画质、面部姿态、服饰、光照、扫描质量共同形成的 hidden cohort 问题。
- Balancing trade-offs: 这解释了为什么 age balancing 有时可能伤害 elderly performance。如果错误机制在 appearance cohort，而我按 age group 平衡，干预对象就不一定对应真实失败来源。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded gender-conditional model 的 routing 依赖 visible attribute，但如果真实 failure cohort 是 latent appearance group，conditional routing 可能把错误结构固化到不同 heads 里。
- Historical domain shift: hidden-cohort discovery 很适合 historical portraits，因为 historical shift 往往不是单一 demographic shift，而是 era、archive、image degradation 和 representation change 的组合。

#### Limitation

The method depends on the quality and interpretability of the learned image representation used for clustering. In historical portraits, cohort discovery would need careful validation so that clusters do not become opaque artifacts.

#### Follow-up

Use this paper as a proposal anchor for a small study: discover appearance-based cohorts in historical face embeddings and test whether elderly-group MAE degradation is concentrated in a few latent cohorts.

### Paper 2

Title: Worst-Group Equalized Odds Regularization for Multi-Attribute Fair Medical Image Classification
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19214
Year / Venue: 2026, arXiv
Source: arXiv official abstract and indexed summary
Research line: operating-point subgroup fairness for multi-attribute medical image classification
Research function: Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference with high strategic value

#### English Summary

- Problem: subgroup AUC can hide clinically important disparities because at a fixed decision threshold some groups may be over-diagnosed while others are under-diagnosed.
- Method: the paper proposes a worst-group equalized-odds margin regularizer. During training, it identifies demographic groups with the largest true-positive and false-positive margin deviations and penalizes those worst-group deviations.
- Evaluation: the method is tested on two medical imaging datasets in realistic multi-label classification settings, with attention to age, sex, race, equalized odds, equal opportunity, and AUC preservation.
- Findings: the method reduces equalized-odds and equal-opportunity disparities with minimal AUC loss, showing that subgroup fairness needs to be evaluated at decision operating points rather than only through aggregate ranking metrics.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文处理的是 subgroup AUC 也可能不够的问题。两个 subgroup 的 AUC 看起来相近，但在实际 threshold 下，一个 group 可能 false positives 更高，另一个 group 可能 true positives 更低。
- 为什么重要: medical AI 的风险来自具体决策点，而不是抽象排序能力。对于我的 thesis，MAE 之外也需要看 underestimation、overestimation 和 decision threshold 下的 subgroup risk。
- 技术直觉: 方法每次关注表现最差的 subgroup margin deviation，把 fairness optimization 放在 worst group 和实际 operating point 上，而不是只优化平均 AUC 或整体 loss。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 这篇论文的核心就是 aggregate AUC 和 subgroup AUC 会掩盖 threshold-level harm。我的 thesis 中 global MAE 好看但 elderly residual pattern 很差，是同类 masking。
- Elderly-group degradation: age 是论文讨论的 explicit demographic attribute。我的 elderly-group underestimation 可以被看成 operating-point failure: 在某些决策阈值下，elderly portraits 可能系统性被低估。
- Balancing trade-offs: 只做 group balancing 不一定修复 equalized odds，因为问题可能来自 false-positive and false-negative margins。我的 balancing 实验也可能没有对准真正的 error type。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded conditional model 不仅要看整体 MAE，还要看每个 routing path 在不同 threshold 或 clinical decision point 下是否形成 worst-group deviation。
- Historical domain shift: historical face estimation 可以把 age bins、gender、era 和 archive source 当作 multiple attributes，检查 underestimation 和 overestimation 是否在某些 groups at operating points 中集中。

#### Limitation

The method assumes explicit demographic attributes for training-time regularization. My historical portrait setting may have incomplete or noisy metadata, so the idea would need adaptation to uncertain subgroup labels or discovered cohorts.

#### Follow-up

Translate the equalized-odds operating-point idea into age-estimation evaluation by defining decision-relevant age thresholds and measuring subgroup-specific underestimation and overestimation around those thresholds.

### Paper 3

Title: Patient-Conditioned Adaptive Offsets for Reliable Diagnosis across Subgroups
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13094
Year / Venue: 2026, arXiv
Source: arXiv official abstract
Research line: patient-context adaptation for subgroup-reliable medical image diagnosis
Research function: Intervention / Explanation
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference

#### English Summary

- Problem: medical diagnostic models can perform unevenly across patient populations because disease prevalence, appearance, and risk profiles differ by patient context. Suppressing sensitive attributes may remove clinically useful signal.
- Method: the paper introduces HyperAdapt, a patient-conditioned adaptation framework. Age and sex are encoded into a compact embedding that conditions a hypernetwork-style module, generating small residual modulation parameters for a shared backbone.
- Evaluation: experiments across multiple public medical imaging benchmarks compare subgroup-level performance, recall, F1, and overall accuracy against fairness and adaptation baselines.
- Findings: patient-conditioned offsets improve subgroup-level reliability while preserving overall performance, with larger gains for underrepresented patient populations.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文讨论的是 subgroup fairness 中一个很现实的矛盾: 在医疗场景里，age 和 sex 不一定只是 bias source，也可能是诊断所需的 clinical context。
- 为什么重要: 这和我的 cascaded gender-conditional model 很相关。问题不是是否使用 sensitive attribute，而是如何使用它，避免把 subgroup routing 做成脆弱的硬分支。
- 技术直觉: 它保留一个共享诊断 backbone，但用 patient context 生成小的 residual offsets。这样模型既能共享通用视觉知识，又能对不同 patient subgroup 做轻量调整。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 论文关注 subgroup-level reliability 而不是只看 overall accuracy。我的 thesis 也说明 average MAE 不能判断模型是否对 elderly group 可靠。
- Elderly-group degradation: age-conditioned adaptation 可以直接对应我的 elderly-group problem。它提示我可以把 age context 用作轻量 model modulation，而不是只作为 evaluation label。
- Balancing trade-offs: 如果 patient context 本身携带有效信号，简单 suppression 或 naive balancing 可能伤害可靠性。这解释了为什么我的 balancing 实验可能让 elderly group 更差。
- Cascade fragility: HyperAdapt 比 hard cascaded routing 更柔和。它启发我重新思考 gender-conditional age model: 是否可以用 low-rank residual modulation 代替 hard routing。
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits 的 context 不只包括 age and gender，也可以包括 era、archive source 和 image quality。条件化 offsets 可能成为处理 historical domain shift 的轻量方法。

#### Limitation

The method depends on patient-context metadata and medical benchmarks where context variables are available and clinically meaningful. For historical portraits, context variables may be noisy, missing, or socially constructed differently across eras.

#### Follow-up

Use this paper as a method contrast: compare hard cascaded routing, shared regression, and lightweight context-conditioned residual adapters for historical facial age estimation.
