## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Fairness and Robustness of CLIP-Based Models for Chest X-rays
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21291
Year / Venue: 2025 / arXiv preprint
Research function: Evaluation / Explanation
Research line: age-specific reliability, shortcut learning, and calibration in multimodal medical models
Screening score: 24/25 = relevance 5 + novelty 4 + citation value 5 + feasibility 5 + long-term fit 5
Usefulness: proposal anchor and evaluation reference

#### English Summary

- **Problem:** Strong overall zero-shot performance from medical CLIP models may conceal demographic performance gaps, shortcut dependence, and unreliable probability estimates. The paper asks whether chest X-ray vision-language models are equally reliable across age, sex, and race and whether apparent disease recognition partly depends on treatment artefacts.
- **Method:** The authors evaluate six pretrained CLIP-style models—MedCLIP, BioViL, BioViL-T, MedImageInsight, CheXzero, and CXR-CLIP. They compare disease performance across demographic groups, probe whether sensitive attributes can be decoded from image embeddings, contrast PCA visualization with supervised probes, and test pneumothorax recognition with versus without chest drains.
- **Evaluation:** Zero-shot classification is evaluated on six conditions in the MIMIC-CXR test set, using AUC and imbalance-adjusted AUPRC with bootstrap confidence intervals. NIH-CXR14 and NEATX support the chest-drain shortcut analysis. Linear probes, k-NN, and a shallow MLP test whether age, sex, and race are encoded in the embeddings; calibration curves assess probability reliability.
- **Findings:** Overall scores hide recurring age-group gaps, whereas sex and race gaps are generally smaller but uncertain for sparsely represented groups. Sensitive attributes are difficult to see in two-dimensional PCA plots but remain decodable from the embeddings. All models except CXR-CLIP achieve adjusted-AUPRC gains of roughly 0.09-0.30 on pneumothorax images with chest drains, indicating shortcut reliance. The models are also miscalibrated and generally overconfident.
- **Technical intuition:** A model can rank cases well enough to obtain acceptable AUC while its probability scale, subgroup behavior, and causal evidence remain unreliable. Low-dimensional visualization is also a weak audit: protected information can be distributed across many embedding dimensions and remain usable by downstream models without forming visible clusters.

#### 中文解释

- **解决的问题:** 这篇论文检验 medical CLIP model 的整体 zero-shot score 是否掩盖 age-specific degradation、shortcut learning 和 probability miscalibration。
- **为什么重要:** 一个模型可以有不错的 overall AUC，但对某些年龄组明显更差；它也可能依赖 chest drain 这类治疗痕迹，而不是疾病本身。这样的模型在真实决策中并不可靠。
- **技术直觉:** AUC 主要反映排序能力，不保证 probability 可解释，也不保证 subgroup performance 稳定。PCA 只展示少数方向；敏感属性可能分散编码在高维 representation 中，因此“图上看不出 cluster”并不代表模型没有编码 age、sex 或 race。
- **核心发现:** 六个模型普遍存在 age-related performance gap；多数模型在有 chest drain 的 pneumothorax 图像上表现更好；敏感属性可以从 embedding 中被 probe 预测；模型整体存在过度自信和 calibration failure。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- **Aggregate metric masking:** 论文直接说明 overall AUC/AUPRC 会掩盖 age-group gaps、drain-defined shortcut strata 和 calibration failure。这与我的 global MAE 掩盖 elderly error 完全同构。
- **Elderly-group degradation:** 论文显式比较不同年龄组，并分析 80+ 与 18-25 岁 patient embedding 的差异。我可以把 elderly underestimation、residual variance 和 calibration 与年龄 representation 一起检查，而不是只报告 elderly mean MAE。
- **Balancing trade-offs:** 论文没有直接提出 balancing intervention，但稀有 age/race cells 的宽 confidence interval 说明只平衡 marginal counts 不够。我需要保留 age × image condition × source 的联合结构，否则 balancing 可能改变病例难度并再次伤害 elderly group。
- **Cascade fragility:** CLIP zero-shot prediction本身是一条 image encoder → text similarity → softmax probability 的多阶段链。其 discrimination 尚可但 probability 失真，说明一个 stage 的合理输出不能保证整条 decision chain 可靠。这支持我对 gender-routing cascade 分阶段检查 routing error、head error 和最终 calibration。
- **Historical domain shift:** Chest drain 是非因果视觉 shortcut；历史人像中的 archive mark、background、frame、photographic process 或 restoration artefact 可能扮演相同角色。我可以按 archive period 与 artefact presence 分层测试 elderly performance 是否依赖这些历史域线索。

#### Limitation

The study evaluates one sensitive attribute at a time and does not establish intersectional guarantees. Its shortcut analysis is specific to pneumothorax and partly relies on automatically generated drain labels for negative cases. The results show that demographic information is encoded, but encoding alone does not prove that the model causally uses that information for every disease prediction.

#### Follow-up

I should reproduce the paper's three-part audit—subgroup discrimination, representation probing, and calibration/shortcut testing—on my historical portrait models before testing new architectures.

### Paper 2

Title: Diagnosing Generalization Failures from Representational Geometry Markers
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01879
Year / Venue: 2026 / arXiv preprint
Research function: Diagnosis / Monitoring
Research line: representation-based prognostics for out-of-distribution model selection
Screening score: 23/25 = relevance 5 + novelty 5 + citation value 5 + feasibility 4 + long-term fit 4
Usefulness: monitoring method and mechanism-boundary reference

#### English Summary

- **Problem:** Models with nearly identical in-distribution accuracy can generalize very differently to unseen classes or transfer tasks. Standard validation accuracy therefore provides a weak basis for anticipating some deployment failures.
- **Method:** The paper treats internal measurements as model-level biomarkers. It compares accuracy, logit statistics, low-order feature statistics, and class-manifold geometry computed only from in-distribution penultimate-layer representations. The strongest markers are task-relevant effective manifold dimensionality and utility from the GLUE geometry framework; reductions in these markers indicate feature overspecialization.
- **Evaluation:** The controlled study trains multiple ResNet, VGG, MobileNet, EfficientNet, and DenseNet variants with SGD or AdamW over learning-rate and weight-decay grids. It measures in-distribution markers on CIFAR-10 or ImageNet subsets and evaluates transfer to disjoint-class datasets with frozen-backbone linear probes. A realistic model-selection study compares v1/v2 weights for 20 ImageNet-pretrained architectures across nine out-of-distribution datasets. CIFAR-10-C is used to test corruption shifts separately.
- **Findings:** Representation geometry predicts class-level transfer performance when in-distribution accuracy is nearly tied. For the 20-architecture study, the selected geometry-marker pair predicts the better OOD weight version with 73.02% accuracy, versus 37.22% when using ID test accuracy. However, ID accuracy is more predictive for corruption-style shifts, so the geometric markers are not a universal drift detector.
- **Technical intuition:** A representation can become highly optimized for the source classes while discarding directions needed for future tasks. Effective dimension and utility measure whether class manifolds retain task-relevant degrees of freedom. Higher source accuracy can therefore coexist with a more overspecialized and less transferable representation.

#### 中文解释

- **解决的问题:** 当两个模型的 validation accuracy 几乎相同时，如何只用 source-domain representation 提前判断哪个模型在 unseen class 或 transfer task 上更容易失败。
- **为什么重要:** 如果 model selection 只看整体 validation score，就可能选择一个 source performance 更高、但 representation 更过度压缩的模型。这个风险与我的 thesis 中“复杂模型 validation 表现不错但 historical shift 下更脆弱”高度一致。
- **技术直觉:** 论文把 effective manifold dimensionality 和 utility 当作 model biomarker。Representation 对 source class 过度专门化时，ID accuracy 仍然可能提高，但可支持新任务的几何方向减少，因此 OOD transfer 变差。
- **核心发现:** 几何 marker 在 class-level transfer shift 下明显优于 ID accuracy 进行 model ranking；但在 corruption shift 下，ID accuracy 更有效。Monitoring signal 必须和 shift mechanism 匹配。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- **Aggregate metric masking:** 相同或更高的 ID accuracy 可能掩盖 OOD transfer failure。这为我的 global validation MAE 无法预测 elderly historical performance 提供了 representation-level explanation。
- **Elderly-group degradation:** 论文目前计算全体 class manifolds；我的扩展应分别计算 elderly 与 non-elderly representation geometry，检验 elderly manifold dimensionality 或 utility 的下降是否先于 elderly MAE、underestimation 和 variance 恶化。
- **Balancing trade-offs:** Balancing 可能提高 marginal validation score，却同时压缩 elderly representation 的有效维度。这个框架提供了一个解释 balancing 为何出现非单调效果的候选机制，而不只是把结果归因于 sample count。
- **Cascade fragility:** 我可以分别测量 gender router 前后的 representation geometry，以及每个 conditional age head 的 age-manifold geometry。如果某个 route 的 representation 过度专门化，就可能解释 cascade 在 historical shift 下的 error amplification。
- **Historical domain shift:** 论文的主要证据来自 class-level transfer，不等同于 archive-period covariate shift。对我的任务而言，需要在 source period 上计算 marker，再检验它是否能预测 later-period elderly error；CIFAR-10-C 的反例提醒我不能直接假设 marker 对所有 historical artefact shift 都有效。

#### Limitation

The strongest evidence concerns class-level transfer and frozen-feature linear probing, not subgroup-specific temporal covariate shift. The markers also require enough labeled in-distribution samples to estimate class manifolds. Their usefulness for continuous age regression, small elderly groups, and evolving archival image conditions remains unverified.

#### Follow-up

I should first test inexpensive geometry summaries on existing thesis checkpoints and only implement the full GLUE marker family if they add predictive value beyond validation MAE and residual diagnostics.

### Paper 3

Title: Fair Dataset Distillation via Cross-Group Barycenter Alignment
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00185
Year / Venue: 2026 / arXiv preprint; submitted to ICML
Research function: Intervention / Evaluation
Research line: data-centric fairness under subgroup imbalance and representational separation
Screening score: 23/25 = relevance 5 + novelty 5 + citation value 5 + feasibility 3 + long-term fit 5
Usefulness: balancing-mechanism and data-intervention reference

#### English Summary

- **Problem:** Dataset distillation compresses a large training set into a small synthetic set by matching aggregate representations. This can preserve average accuracy while amplifying subgroup disparity. Correcting group counts alone is insufficient when groups also follow different predictive representation patterns.
- **Method:** COBRA computes a class-conditional, group-imbalance-agnostic barycenter of subgroup representations and distills synthetic samples toward that common target. With a squared Q-norm discrepancy, the barycenter becomes a uniform subgroup mean. The objective can be added to gradient-matching, distribution-matching, feature-alignment, and trajectory-matching distillation pipelines.
- **Evaluation:** The study uses controlled spurious-correlation benchmarks (Colored-MNIST, Colored-Fashion-MNIST, and CIFAR10-S) and real datasets (UTKFace and BFFHQ). It evaluates images-per-class budgets of 10, 50, and 100, reports equalized-odds difference and accuracy over ten runs, varies subgroup imbalance and representational separation independently, and tests transfer across ConvNet, AlexNet, VGG11, and ResNet18.
- **Findings:** Vanilla distillation increasingly amplifies fairness gaps as imbalance, representation separation, or compression severity grows. COBRA consistently reduces disparity while retaining competitive or higher accuracy. With distribution matching at 100 images per class, it reduces CIFAR10-S equalized-odds difference from 82.87 to 9.37 while raising accuracy from 45.4 to 62.4; on BFFHQ it reduces the gap from 63.47 to 7.87 while raising accuracy from 65.8 to 74.2. The gains persist across evaluation architectures.
- **Technical intuition:** Uniformly sampling groups changes their frequency but still lets geometrically similar groups dominate the aggregate target. COBRA changes the target itself: every class-specific subgroup contributes equally to the representation that the synthetic set must preserve. This addresses the interaction between sample imbalance and subgroup-specific predictive structure.

#### 中文解释

- **解决的问题:** Dataset distillation 可能保留 overall accuracy，却把 minority subgroup 的 predictive pattern 从 compressed data 中抹掉。单纯做 uniform group sampling 不能解决 subgroup representation separation。
- **为什么重要:** 这篇论文给 balancing trade-off 一个更具体的机制：失败不只来自 subgroup 数量不足，还来自不同 subgroup 的 representation pattern 被错误聚合。
- **技术直觉:** COBRA 不只是重新加权 loss，而是在每个 class 内计算各 subgroup 等权的 representation barycenter，再让 distilled data 匹配这个目标。这样 majority group 不能仅凭 sample size 控制 synthetic representation。
- **核心发现:** 在多个 synthetic 和 real-world benchmark 上，Vanilla DD 会随 imbalance、group separation 和压缩强度增加而放大 EOD；COBRA 在多种 distillation backbone 和 evaluation architecture 上显著降低 disparity，并通常保持或提高 accuracy。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- **Aggregate metric masking:** Dataset distillation 可以保持甚至提高 aggregate accuracy，同时显著恶化 EOD。这直接说明数据处理 pipeline 也需要 subgroup-aware evaluation，不能只看压缩后的平均性能。
- **Elderly-group degradation:** Elderly portraits 可能不仅数量少，其 age cues、image quality 和 archive-source representation 也与其他年龄组不同。COBRA 提示我应保护 age-conditioned predictive structure，而不是假设 count balancing 足够。
- **Balancing trade-offs:** 这是三篇中对我的 balancing 结果解释最直接的一篇。我的 elderly degradation 可能来自 group imbalance 与 representation separation 的交互；uniform resampling 改变了数量，却没有修复 elderly feature geometry，甚至可能让新的 aggregate target 更偏离 elderly pattern。
- **Cascade fragility:** 如果 condensed 或 rebalanced data 已丢失某个 gender-age route 的 predictive structure，conditional cascade 会在后续 stage 放大这个缺口。Barycentric alignment 可以按 route 检查或保护 representation，但也必须防止硬 route label 本身在 shift 下出错。
- **Historical domain shift:** Archive source、period 和 photographic process 可以定义 domain groups，age 或 gender 可以定义 vulnerable groups。我可以构造 age × archive cells，测试 barycenter-aware condensation 是否比 uniform balancing 更能保留跨时期的 elderly cues；论文尚未验证 distillation-to-deployment shift，因此这是关键扩展。

#### Limitation

COBRA assumes usable group labels during distillation and is evaluated mainly on classification fairness with equalized-odds difference. Adapting it to continuous age regression, multiple intersecting attributes, uncertain historical metadata, and source-to-target temporal shift is nontrivial. Dataset distillation also adds optimization complexity beyond a simple sampling intervention.

#### Follow-up

I should prototype the barycenter idea first as feature-space coreset selection on frozen portrait embeddings, which is more feasible than full image-level distillation and directly tests the proposed imbalance-separation mechanism.

## 2. Emerging Trend

### English

Today's papers point to a reliability chain rather than a single fairness metric. Evaluation must first expose age-specific performance, shortcut strata, and calibration failure. Representation diagnostics can then test whether a model has become overspecialized before a relevant class-level shift, but the signal must be matched to the shift mechanism. Finally, data interventions must preserve subgroup-specific predictive structure rather than only equalizing sample counts. The common theme is that aggregation can fail at three levels: aggregate test scores, aggregate representations, and aggregate data-compression targets.

### 中文

今天的趋势不是寻找一个统一的 fairness metric，而是建立一条 **reliability chain**。第一步用 age-specific performance、shortcut strata 和 calibration 暴露隐藏失败；第二步用 representation marker 检查模型是否对 source domain 过度专门化，但必须区分 class-level shift 与 corruption/temporal shift；第三步的数据干预不能只平衡 sample count，还要保留 subgroup-specific predictive structure。三篇论文共同说明，aggregation 可以在三个层次失败：aggregate test score、aggregate representation，以及 aggregate data-compression target。

## 3. Two Research Questions

### Question 1

#### English

- **Research question:** Can age-conditioned representation-geometry markers computed on source-period portraits predict elderly-group MAE, underestimation rate, and residual variance under archive-source or later-period shift more accurately than global validation MAE?
- **Why it matters:** This tests whether representation overspecialization can provide an earlier model-selection signal for the elderly failure that global MAE hid in my thesis.
- **Possible data:** My historical portrait dataset, partitioned by archive source and time period, with age groups used only for retrospective subgroup evaluation.
- **Possible method:** Extract penultimate features from simple regression, multi-task, and gender-conditional cascade checkpoints. Compute participation ratio, within-group covariance, effective manifold dimension, and utility globally and within the elderly group. Fit a simple source-only predictor of target elderly degradation and compare it with validation-MAE ranking.
- **Evaluation metric:** Correlation and absolute error between predicted and observed elderly MAE; accuracy in ranking model pairs; elderly underestimation rate; residual variance; worst archive-age cell MAE; and bootstrap confidence intervals.
- **Thesis connection:** The design directly tests aggregate masking, elderly degradation, cascade-specific representation failure, and historical domain shift while checking whether balancing changes geometry in a harmful direction.
- **Risk or limitation:** Elderly samples may be too sparse for stable manifold estimation, and the paper's markers may not transfer from class-level classification to continuous age regression. Shrinkage estimates and simpler geometry baselines are necessary.

#### 中文

- **研究问题:** 只使用 source-period portraits 计算 age-conditioned representation-geometry marker，能否比 global validation MAE 更准确地预测 archive-source 或 later-period shift 下的 elderly-group MAE、underestimation rate 和 residual variance？
- **重要性:** 这个问题把 thesis 中被 global MAE 掩盖的 elderly failure 转化为一个 source-only model selection 与 early-warning 问题。
- **可能数据:** 我的历史人像数据，按 archive source 与 time period 划分；age group 用于 retrospective subgroup evaluation。
- **可能方法:** 对 simple regression、multi-task 和 gender-conditional cascade checkpoint 提取 penultimate feature；全局和 elderly group 内分别计算 participation ratio、within-group covariance、effective manifold dimension 和 utility；比较 geometry-based predictor 与 validation-MAE ranking。
- **评估指标:** 预测与真实 elderly MAE 的 correlation/absolute error、model-pair ranking accuracy、elderly underestimation rate、residual variance、worst archive-age cell MAE 和 bootstrap confidence interval。
- **Thesis 连接:** 同时检验 aggregate masking、elderly degradation、cascade representation failure、balancing-induced geometry change 和 historical domain shift。
- **风险:** Elderly sample 可能不足以稳定估计 manifold；classification marker 也可能不适用于 continuous regression，需要 shrinkage estimate 和简单 geometry baseline。

### Question 2

#### English

- **Research question:** At a fixed training-data budget, does age-and-archive-aware barycentric coreset selection preserve elderly predictive structure and reduce worst archive-age cell error better than uniform age balancing or loss reweighting?
- **Why it matters:** This directly tests whether my harmful balancing result was caused by sample imbalance alone or by the interaction between imbalance and subgroup representation separation.
- **Possible data:** Frozen embeddings and labels from my historical portrait dataset, with age group, gender, archive source, period, and image-quality metadata where available.
- **Possible method:** Build three equal-budget training subsets: uniform age balancing, loss-influence or uncertainty reweighting, and a COBRA-inspired feature-space coreset selected to approximate class/age-bin-specific subgroup barycenters. Train the same simple regression backbone on each subset across repeated source-target period splits.
- **Evaluation metric:** Global and elderly MAE; elderly 90th-percentile absolute error; underestimation rate; worst archive-age cell MAE; subgroup representation distance; calibration error; and accuracy-retention versus subset size.
- **Thesis connection:** The experiment isolates balancing trade-offs, protects elderly cues, tests historical shift, and uses the simpler regressor as a stable reference against the conditional cascade.
- **Risk or limitation:** Historical metadata may be incomplete, and a barycenter can erase legitimate subgroup-specific age cues if alignment is too strong. A strength sweep and no-alignment ablation are required.

#### 中文

- **研究问题:** 在固定 training-data budget 下，age-and-archive-aware barycentric coreset selection 是否比 uniform age balancing 或 loss reweighting 更能保留 elderly predictive structure，并降低 worst archive-age cell error？
- **重要性:** 这个问题直接检验我的 harmful balancing result 是只由 sample imbalance 引起，还是由 imbalance 与 subgroup representation separation 的交互引起。
- **可能数据:** 历史人像的 frozen embedding 与 label，并使用可获得的 age group、gender、archive source、period 和 image-quality metadata。
- **可能方法:** 构建三个等预算训练子集：uniform age balancing、loss-influence/uncertainty reweighting，以及 COBRA-inspired feature-space coreset；后者近似 class/age-bin-specific subgroup barycenter。使用相同 simple regression backbone，在 repeated source-target period split 上训练。
- **评估指标:** global/elderly MAE、elderly 90th-percentile error、underestimation rate、worst archive-age cell MAE、subgroup representation distance、calibration error，以及不同 subset size 下的 accuracy retention。
- **Thesis 连接:** 实验直接隔离 balancing trade-off，检验 elderly cue preservation 与 historical shift，并用 simple regressor 作为 conditional cascade 的稳定参照。
- **风险:** Historical metadata 可能不完整；过强 barycentric alignment 可能抹去真实的 subgroup-specific age cue，因此需要 alignment-strength sweep 和 no-alignment ablation。

## 4. Personal Reflection

### English

The clearest advance today is a shift from reporting subgroup failure to explaining where the failure enters the pipeline. The chest X-ray study is closest to my thesis: overall discrimination can look acceptable while age gaps, shortcut dependence, and overconfidence remain hidden.
