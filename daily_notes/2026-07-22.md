## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: The Label Complexity of Class-Conditional Coverage under Distribution Shift
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18088
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint
Source: official arXiv record, verified 2026-07-22
Research line: conformal prediction, class-conditional coverage, distribution shift, uncertainty-aware subgroup reliability
Research function: Diagnosis / Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor and Recommended for paper card

#### English Summary

- Problem: conformal prediction can provide marginal coverage while individual classes or rare groups remain undercovered after distribution shift. The paper asks how many target-domain labels are needed to guarantee class-conditional coverage.
- Method: the paper studies label-complexity bounds for class-conditional coverage under distribution shift, analyzing when target labels are necessary and when structure in the shift can reduce the required labeling burden.
- Evaluation: the paper combines theory with empirical studies on skeleton-based action recognition and natural-image corruption shifts, using source-only calibration, source-plus-target calibration, and per-class target calibration comparisons.
- Findings: marginal 90% conformal coverage can coexist with much lower worst-class coverage under shift. The paper shows that a small number of target labels per class can substantially improve class-conditional coverage, while formal label requirements scale with the desired accuracy of the conditional guarantee.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 conformal prediction 在 distribution shift 下的 hidden coverage failure。整体 coverage 看起来达标，不代表每个 class 或 subgroup 都被覆盖。
- 为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 中 global MAE 掩盖 elderly-group error 是同一种问题，只是指标从 prediction error 换成 uncertainty coverage。如果一个模型对 elderly portraits 的 prediction interval 覆盖不足，但整体 coverage 仍然合格，deployment risk 仍然会被隐藏。
- 技术直觉: marginal conformal coverage 把所有样本混在一起保证平均覆盖；class-conditional coverage 要求每个类别或 subgroup 都可靠。distribution shift 后，某些 subgroup 的 nonconformity score distribution 会改变，因此需要额外 target labels 或结构假设来恢复条件覆盖。
- 核心价值: 这篇论文让我看到，uncertainty-aware monitoring 不能只报告一个 global coverage number，而要问 subgroup coverage claim 需要多少 evidence 才成立。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: the paper directly parallels my thesis observation that global MAE can hide elderly failure. Here, marginal coverage can hide class- or subgroup-specific undercoverage.
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits can be treated as a vulnerable conditional group. The paper suggests that elderly-group uncertainty reliability may require target-period calibration labels rather than relying on pooled calibration.
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing may change class or subgroup proportions, but conditional coverage still depends on the subgroup score distribution. This helps explain why rebalancing in my thesis could fail if it changed counts without stabilizing residual behavior.
- Cascade fragility: a cascaded gender-conditional age model creates route-conditioned score distributions. If each route has different calibration error under historical shift, a marginal conformal guarantee may miss route-specific elderly undercoverage.
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits naturally create target-domain uncertainty because age cues, image quality, and archive sources change over time. The paper gives a formal way to ask how much target-domain evidence is needed before I can claim elderly-group coverage.

#### Limitation

The paper is more theoretical than applied, so I should use it to strengthen the logic of subgroup-valid monitoring rather than as a plug-and-play experimental method for facial age estimation.

#### Follow-up

Adapt the label-complexity question to age-regression intervals: how many labeled historical elderly portraits are needed before elderly-group conformal age coverage can be reported with confidence?

### Paper 2

Title: Benchmarking the Robustness of Foundation Models for Mammography under Domain Shift
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10358
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint
Source: official arXiv record, verified 2026-07-22
Research line: medical imaging robustness, foundation models, mammography, external domain shift, subgroup evaluation
Research function: Diagnosis / Evaluation
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for visual domain-shift evaluation

#### English Summary

- Problem: medical imaging foundation models may perform well on familiar data while degrading under scanner, site, dataset, or acquisition shifts. The paper asks whether mammography foundation models remain robust across realistic domain shifts.
- Method: the paper benchmarks 15 vision and vision-language foundation-model backbones with frozen-backbone linear probing, standardized preprocessing, and label harmonization across mammography datasets.
- Evaluation: the benchmark trains on three public mammography datasets and tests on 12 held-out OOD datasets, with subgroup analyses by breast density, BI-RADS category, and cancer status.
- Findings: foundation-model performance is not uniformly robust. Source performance does not reliably predict OOD robustness, while subgroup difficulty and dataset-specific shifts can change model ranking and clinically relevant reliability.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 medical imaging foundation model 在 domain shift 下是否真的可靠的问题。一个模型在某个 mammography dataset 上表现好，不代表跨医院、设备、采集协议或患者构成变化后仍然可靠。
- 为什么重要: 这与我的 historical portrait age estimation 很接近。历史人像中的 shift 来自拍摄年代、扫描质量、修复方式和档案来源；mammography 中的 shift 来自设备、站点和数据集。两者都说明视觉模型的平均表现容易掩盖 domain-specific failure。
- 技术直觉: foundation model 的 representation 可能很强，但不等于所有 domain 都稳定。真正的 evaluation 需要跨数据源、跨临床 subgroup、跨 acquisition condition 检查性能和 calibration，而不是只看一个 pooled AUROC。
- 核心价值: 这篇论文给我一个视觉领域的 evaluation template: 用 external datasets 模拟真实 shift，并检查每个 domain 和 subgroup 中的 reliability gap。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: a pooled mammography score can hide dataset- or subgroup-specific degradation, just as global MAE hid elderly-group error in my thesis.
- Elderly-group degradation: although the paper is not about age estimation, clinically meaningful mammography subgroups play the same role as elderly portraits: they are groups where average performance may not reflect actual reliability.
- Balancing trade-offs: if one dataset or subgroup dominates training, simple rebalancing may improve pooled performance while weakening another domain. This mirrors my thesis result where balancing did not automatically protect elderly-group performance.
- Cascade fragility: foundation-model pipelines often combine representation extraction, task-specific heads, and sometimes language or metadata components. Under domain shift, these stages can fail unevenly, similar to how my gender-conditional cascade could amplify upstream instability.
- Historical domain shift: the paper's cross-dataset mammography protocol can be translated into archive-source or historical-period splits for portrait data. I can treat each source or period as a domain and evaluate whether representation quality and age residuals remain stable.

#### Limitation

The paper focuses on mammography classification rather than facial age regression. I need to translate classification metrics into regression reliability metrics such as subgroup MAE, residual skew, underestimation rate, calibration error, and age-interval coverage.

#### Follow-up

Use this paper as a template for a historical portrait robustness benchmark: compare simple regression, multitask, and cascade models across archive source, time period, image quality, and age group.

### Paper 3

Title: Improving Survival Models in Healthcare by Balancing Data Distributions: A Novel Approach to Addressing Imbalanced Cohorts
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02137
Year / Venue: 2025 arXiv preprint
Source: official arXiv record, verified 2026-07-22
Research line: healthcare survival modeling, cohort imbalance, risk-stratified balancing, subgroup reliability
Research function: Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 21/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference and balancing-trade-off warning

#### English Summary

- Problem: healthcare survival datasets often contain imbalanced cohorts, where high-risk or low-risk strata are underrepresented. Standard Cox models can then optimize average performance while missing clinically important risk groups.
- Method: the paper proposes clustering-based risk stratification followed by matching within risk strata, so cohort balancing is tied to baseline prognostic risk rather than only demographic counts.
- Evaluation: the paper evaluates Cox proportional-hazards models on colorectal liver metastases cohorts, including internal and external validation settings.
- Findings: risk-stratified balancing improves discrimination and calibration compared with unbalanced training and several balancing baselines, especially in underrepresented risk groups.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文研究 survival models 在 healthcare cohort imbalance 下如何失败。高风险或低风险人群样本少时，模型可能整体 C-index 或 average loss 不差，但对关键 subgroup 的风险预测不可靠。
- 为什么重要: 这直接对应我的 thesis 中 balancing 的矛盾结果。我的数据平衡实验没有稳定改善 elderly-group performance，有时反而让 elderly error 变差。这个 paper 提醒我，balancing 是否有用取决于它是否匹配真正的 risk structure，而不只是把样本数量拉平。
- 技术直觉: survival task 的 subgroup 不是简单 demographic group，而是 risk-defined region。一个好的 balancing 方法应该在相似 baseline risk 内做 matching，让训练分布更覆盖 decision-relevant risk strata，而不是盲目复制少数样本或改变总体比例。
- 核心价值: 这篇论文帮助我把 balancing 从一个 preprocessing trick 重新解释为 intervention evaluation: balancing 本身需要被 subgroup-wise and decision-wise 审计。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: global survival metrics can hide failures in high-risk or low-risk strata, just as global MAE hid elderly-group degradation in my age-estimation task.
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits in my thesis can be viewed as a vulnerable risk region: underrepresented, visually heterogeneous, and decision-relevant. The survival paper gives a way to think about subgroup reliability as risk-region reliability.
- Balancing trade-offs: this is the strongest thesis connection. The paper supports the idea that balancing must be evaluated by subgroup outcome, not assumed beneficial. It helps me explain why my balancing intervention could worsen elderly performance if it distorted age-related visual structure.
- Cascade fragility: if a survival model or age model first stratifies samples into risk or demographic paths, balancing errors can propagate into downstream predictions. This mirrors cascade fragility in my gender-conditioned model.
- Historical domain shift: historical data are likely imbalanced across age, source, and period. Risk-stratified balancing can be adapted to age-regression by balancing residual-risk regions or elderly visual modes, then testing whether the target historical domain actually benefits.

#### Limitation

The paper is about survival modeling, not historical computer vision. I should treat it as a balancing intervention reference rather than direct evidence that risk-stratified matching will help facial age estimation.

#### Follow-up

Design a balancing audit for my thesis data: compare demographic balancing, residual-risk balancing, and representation-region balancing, then evaluate elderly MAE, underestimation rate, calibration error, and worst-group reliability under historical source shift.

## 3. Two Research Questions

### Question 1

English:

- Research question: Under historical portrait domain shift, how many labeled elderly target-domain samples are needed before conformal age intervals can guarantee elderly-group coverage rather than only marginal coverage?
- Why it matters: my thesis showed that global MAE can hide elderly failure; the same problem may occur for uncertainty intervals if coverage is reported only marginally.
- Possible data: thesis portrait images, predicted ages, true age labels, age groups, gender labels, archive source, historical period, image-quality proxies, and natural versus balanced training variants.
- Possible method: train simple regression and cascaded age-estimation models, construct pooled conformal intervals, compare them with age-group or source-aware conformal intervals, and run label-budget simulations over increasing elderly target labels.
- Evaluation metric: marginal coverage, elderly-group coverage, worst-group coverage gap, interval width, elderly underestimation rate, confidence interval width for coverage estimates, and required elderly label count for stable reporting.
- Thesis connection: directly extends aggregate metric masking, elderly degradation, cascade fragility, balancing trade-offs, and historical domain shift into uncertainty-aware monitoring.
- Risk or limitation: elderly target labels may be sparse or noisy, so the experiment should report uncertainty and label-budget sensitivity instead of claiming exact finite-sample guarantees beyond the supported setting.

中文:

- 研究问题: 在 historical portrait domain shift 下，需要多少 labeled elderly target-domain samples，才能让 conformal age intervals 保证 elderly-group coverage，而不只是 marginal coverage？
- 为什么重要: 我的 thesis 已经看到 global MAE 掩盖 elderly failure；同样的问题也可能发生在 uncertainty intervals 上，如果我只报告 overall coverage。
- 可能数据: 我的历史人像数据、预测年龄、真实年龄标签、age group、gender、archive source、historical period、image-quality proxies，以及 natural/balanced training variants。
- 可能方法: 训练 simple regression 和 cascaded age-estimation models；比较 pooled conformal interval、age-group conformal interval 和 source-aware conformal interval；用逐步增加的 elderly target labels 做 label-budget simulation。
- 评估指标: marginal coverage、elderly-group coverage、worst-group coverage gap、interval width、elderly underestimation rate、coverage estimate 的 confidence interval，以及稳定报告所需的 elderly label count。
- Thesis 连接: 这个问题把 aggregate masking、elderly degradation、cascade fragility、balancing trade-off 和 historical domain shift 直接推进到 uncertainty-aware monitoring。
- 风险: elderly target labels 可能少且 noisy，所以应该报告 uncertainty 和 label-budget sensitivity，而不是过度声称强保证。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: Does representation-region balancing reduce elderly-group age-estimation error under archive-source shift more reliably than demographic count balancing?
- Why it matters: my thesis found that balancing sometimes worsened elderly performance, which suggests that count balancing may not target the real reliability mechanism.
- Possible data: thesis portraits, visual embeddings from the shared backbone, age/gender labels, source and period metadata, image-quality proxies, original and balanced training splits.
- Possible method: cluster or bin embeddings into representation regions, identify elderly-heavy high-error regions, compare demographic balancing with representation-region balancing and residual-risk balancing, then evaluate all variants under held-out source or period shift.
- Evaluation metric: global MAE, elderly MAE, worst-region MAE, residual skew, underestimation rate, subgroup calibration error, representation drift distance, and performance difference between simple regression and cascaded models.
- Thesis connection: tests whether balancing failed because it changed demographic counts without repairing representation instability or source-specific elderly visual modes.
- Risk or limitation: representation clusters can be unstable across backbones, so the method should include cluster-stability checks and avoid interpreting every cluster as a meaningful subgroup.

中文:

- 研究问题: 在 archive-source shift 下，representation-region balancing 是否比 demographic count balancing 更稳定地降低 elderly-group age-estimation error？
- 为什么重要: 我的 thesis 中 balancing 有时让 elderly performance 变差，这说明单纯按人数平衡可能没有击中真正的 reliability mechanism。
- 可能数据: 我的历史人像、shared backbone visual embeddings、age/gender labels、source/period metadata、image-quality proxies，以及原始和平衡训练集。
- 可能方法: 对 embeddings 做 cluster 或 binning，找出 elderly-heavy high-error regions；比较 demographic balancing、representation-region balancing 和 residual-risk balancing；在 held-out source 或 period shift 下评估。
- 评估指标: global MAE、elderly MAE、worst-region MAE、residual skew、underestimation rate、subgroup calibration error、representation drift distance，以及 simple regression 与 cascaded model 的性能差异。
- Thesis 连接: 这个问题检验 balancing 失败是否因为它只改变 demographic counts，而没有修复 representation instability 或 source-specific elderly visual modes。
