## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: FRAME: separating sampling variation from representational cause in medical imaging fairness
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25981
Year / Venue: 2026 / arXiv
Source: arXiv primary page and HTML full text
Research line: medical imaging fairness, fair-model reference, representation mechanism analysis, subgroup disparity interpretation
Research function: Diagnosis, Explanation, Intervention, and Evaluation
Screening Score: 25/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 5; proposal citation value 5; feasibility 5; strategic long-term fit 5.
Usefulness: proposal anchor and Recommended for paper card

#### English Summary

- Problem: subgroup performance gaps in medical imaging are often interpreted as direct evidence of fairness bias, but part of the observed gap may be explainable by subgroup sample sizes and ordinary sampling variation.
- Method: FRAME builds a fair-model reference distribution for the observed subgroup counts, then evaluates the remaining gap with representation-space operators designed to distinguish decodability of demographic information from mechanism-level entanglement with the disease direction.
- Evaluation: the paper evaluates 702,206 images and 36 encoders, audits subgroup claims from 9 published studies across 6 imaging modalities, and reports bootstrap uncertainty, fair-reference simulations, permutation tests, and FDR-controlled exceedance tests.
- Findings: a large share of reported race and age differences can be explained by the fair-reference baseline at current cohort sizes. Injecting demographic decodability alone does not change the remainder, while entangling the group direction with the disease direction increases the residual difference. Tested interventions often change operating-point gaps less than random seed variation, while image-text pretraining improves worst-group performance.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: 医学影像中的 subgroup performance gap 不一定都等于模型产生了机制性 bias。某些差异可能只是 subgroup sample size 和 sampling variation 带来的表面差异。FRAME 的价值在于先建立一个 "如果模型公平, 在当前 subgroup 数量下本来可能出现多大差异" 的 reference, 再分析剩余差异是否真的需要机制解释。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 看到 elderly-group MAE 明显更差, 但下一步不能只停留在 "elderly group 失败了"。我需要区分这是样本少、历史图像质量差、representation entanglement, 还是 age signal 本身在 elderly 区间不稳定。FRAME 正好提供了这种诊断顺序。

技术直觉: 先不要把所有 subgroup gap 都当作同一种 failure。第一步问这个 gap 是否超过 fair-model reference; 第二步再问 representation 里 group 信息只是可被解码, 还是已经和任务方向纠缠在一起。这个区别可以防止我把表面差异误解释成真正的机制性退化。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: FRAME和我的 thesis 一样反对只看全局指标, 但它进一步提醒我: subgroup gap 本身也需要统计 reference, 否则小样本 subgroup 的 gap 可能被过度解释。
- Elderly-group degradation: 我 thesis 中 elderly group 的高误差可以用 FRAME 的思路重新检查: 先估计在 elderly 样本量下公平模型可能自然产生的误差差异, 再判断剩余差异是否来自 age representation 或图像质量机制。
- Balancing trade-offs: 如果一个 gap 部分来自 sampling variation, 直接 balancing 可能只是在追逐不稳定估计; 如果剩余 gap 来自 representation entanglement, balancing 又可能不够。这能解释为什么我的 balancing 有时反而伤害 elderly performance。
- Cascade fragility: Cascaded gender-conditional age model 的问题可能不是 "gender 信息可被模型编码" 本身, 而是 gender routing 与 age disease/task direction 类似地发生了机制性纠缠。FRAME 提供了区分 decodability 和 harmful entanglement 的语言。
- Historical domain shift: 历史人像的 archive、period、image quality 和 age distribution 都会改变 subgroup counts。FRAME 支持我在 historical shift 下先做 fair-reference baseline, 再谈是否有真正的 temporal subgroup failure。

#### Limitation

The paper is centered on medical imaging classification, not age regression. For my proposal, I should adapt the reference idea to continuous residuals, MAE, calibration, and underestimation rate rather than copying the exact AUROC/rate-difference tests.

#### Follow-up

Deep-read this paper for a paper card. The most useful extraction is a thesis-extension protocol: build a fair-reference distribution for elderly residual gaps, then test whether the remaining gap is linked to representation drift, image quality, or source-period shift.

### Paper 2

Title: CIFA: Contextual-Intersectional Fairness Auditing for Hidden Subgroup Discovery in Face Analysis
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09669
Year / Venue: 2026 / arXiv
Source: arXiv primary page and HTML full text
Research line: face analysis fairness, contextual-intersectional auditing, hidden subgroup discovery, audit-mitigate-reaudit
Research function: Diagnosis, Intervention, and Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 5; proposal citation value 5; feasibility 5; strategic long-term fit 4.
Usefulness: directly transferable audit design for historical facial age estimation

#### English Summary

- Problem: fairness evaluation in computer vision often checks aggregate accuracy or demographic subgroups, but visual conditions such as illumination, blur, image quality, accessories, and appearance attributes can interact with demographics and create hidden vulnerable subgroups.
- Method: CIFA audits models across demographic groups, contextual groups, and contextual-intersectional groups, then ranks worst groups and uses an audit-mitigate-reaudit protocol with the same subgroup definitions.
- Evaluation: the paper evaluates gender classification with ResNet-50 and ViT-B/16 across FairFace, CelebA, and UTKFace. It reports aggregate accuracy, worst-group accuracy, overall-to-worst-group gap, and group-level variance.
- Findings: high overall accuracy still hides large contextual-intersectional failures. The largest reported gap is 26.43 percentage points on UTKFace with ResNet-50. Mitigation methods can reduce some gaps, but no single strategy consistently dominates across datasets and architectures.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: 只看 demographic subgroup 仍然不够。比如一个模型在 gender 或 race 上看起来还可以, 但当 race、age、illumination、image quality、facial accessories 同时组合时, 某些 hidden subgroup 会突然失败。

为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 非常接近。我的 historical portrait 数据不是普通人脸数据, 它天然有 period、archive、拍摄风格、画质、年龄段和性别交互。CIFA 提醒我, elderly group failure 可能不是单纯由 age 导致, 而是 age 和 historical context 的交互造成的。

技术直觉: CIFA 的重点不是提出一个新模型, 而是定义更合理的 failure unit。先检查 demographic, 再检查 contextual, 最后检查 contextual-intersectional subgroup。Mitigation 之后还要用同一套 subgroup 定义 re-audit, 避免 aggregate accuracy 提升但 hidden subgroup 仍然失败。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: CIFA 直接证明 high aggregate accuracy 和 demographic-only fairness 都可能掩盖 contextual-intersectional failure, 这扩展了我 thesis 中 global MAE 掩盖 elderly failure 的观察。
- Elderly-group degradation: Elderly degradation 在我的数据中可能是 `age group x gender x archive x image quality x period` 的交互结果, 而不是 age group 单独的效应。
- Balancing trade-offs: CIFA 的 re-audit 结果显示 balanced sampling、context augmentation、weighted loss 和 Group DRO 的效果随 dataset 和 architecture 变化。这个发现能解释我的 balancing 为什么不稳定: intervention 需要对准真实 hidden subgroup, 不能只按单轴分布平衡。
- Cascade fragility: Cascaded conditional model 把 gender 当作显式 routing 变量, 但如果真正的 failure unit 是 age-context-image quality 的交互, gender routing 会错过关键 failure mechanism, 甚至放大某些路径上的误差。
- Historical domain shift: CIFA 的 contextual attributes 可以自然迁移到 historical portraits: illumination、blur、facial accessory、source archive、portrait period、annotation uncertainty 和 preservation quality 都可以成为 shift-aware audit dimensions。

#### Limitation

CIFA evaluates gender classification, not age regression. It also depends on available contextual annotations or proxies. For my use, the difficult part is defining reliable historical-context variables without overfitting small subgroup cells.

#### Follow-up

Use CIFA as the template for a historical portrait audit table: aggregate MAE, age-only MAE, context-only MAE, and contextual-intersectional worst-group MAE, followed by mitigation and re-audit under the same group definitions.

### Paper 3

Title: Subgroup performance analysis of adaptation strategies for chest X-ray foundation models
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19078
Year / Venue: 2026 / MICCAI Workshop on Fairness of AI in Medical Imaging (FAIMI 2026)
Source: arXiv primary page and HTML full text
Research line: medical foundation models, subgroup fairness, adapter choice, representation probing
Research function: Explanation and Evaluation
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 4; proposal citation value 4; feasibility 4; strategic long-term fit 5.
Usefulness: method caution for representation adaptation and fairness-utility trade-offs

#### English Summary

- Problem: foundation models are often adapted with lightweight heads or adapters, but the subgroup fairness effect of the adaptation strategy is not well understood.
- Method: the paper freezes Rad-DINO and compares three adaptation strategies: linear heads on the raw CLS token, an MLP, and attention pooling over multi-layer patch features. It also probes how strongly adapters encode protected attributes.
- Evaluation: the study uses MIMIC-CXR, evaluates eight pathology classification tasks, and measures performance across race, sex, and imaging-view subgroups on a prevalence-preserving, demographically balanced test set.
- Findings: attention pooling improves overall discriminative performance and strongly encodes race, but better overall AUROC does not consistently reduce subgroup disparities. The relationship between layer choice, protected-attribute encoding, and subgroup fairness is task-dependent and unpredictable.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: foundation model adaptation 不能只看 overall downstream performance。一个更 expressive 的 adapter 可能提高整体 AUROC, 但 subgroup disparity 可能没有同步下降, 有时还会在某些 pathology 或 view/race subgroup 上变得更复杂。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 中更复杂的 cascaded conditional model 并没有比简单 regression 更稳。这篇论文给了一个更现代的对应例子: 更丰富的 representation 或 adapter 不等于更可靠的 subgroup behavior。Subgroup fairness 必须直接评估, 不能从 representation strength 或整体性能间接推断。

技术直觉: Adapter choice 改变了模型从 frozen encoder 中读取信息的方式。Attention pooling 可以利用多层 patch tokens, 所以整体性能更强, 但它也可能改变不同 subgroup 的证据路径。Protected attribute encoding 强不强, 和 subgroup gap 大不大之间没有简单线性关系。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 论文显示 overall AUROC 的提高不能说明 subgroup disparity 减少, 这对应我的 global MAE 无法代表 elderly-group reliability。
- Elderly-group degradation: 在我的 historical portrait 任务中, elderly group 可能对 backbone 层级、face texture、pose 和 image quality 更敏感。类似地, adapter 或 pooling strategy 可能改变 elderly residual distribution。
- Balancing trade-offs: 论文使用 prevalence-preserving and demographically balanced test set, 但 subgroup disparity 仍然需要逐任务检查。这提醒我, balanced evaluation set 不等于 intervention 已经解决 subgroup reliability。
- Cascade fragility: Adapter selection不是 cascaded routing, 但它同样是一种 conditional evidence extraction。我的 cascaded model fragile 可以被重新解释为: 更复杂的信息路径在 shift 下产生更难预测的 subgroup error。
- Historical domain shift: Historical portraits can be treated as a shifted visual domain where final-layer features, multi-layer features, and simple regression heads may behave differently across age, gender, period, and source. This paper supports direct layer/adapter-level subgroup auditing before assuming richer representation is safer.

#### Limitation

The paper does not center on temporal deployment shift or online monitoring. Its value for my research is mainly diagnostic: it helps frame why representation adaptation and fairness cannot be inferred from aggregate performance.

#### Follow-up

Use this paper to compare simple regression heads, multi-task heads, and cascaded heads in my own thesis setting through the lens of representation access: which architecture reads stable age evidence, and which one increases subgroup-specific residual variance?

## 2. Emerging Trend

English:

Today's trend is a shift from "subgroup gap reporting" toward "subgroup gap interpretation." FRAME asks whether a gap exceeds what fair sampling variation could produce. CIFA asks whether the chosen subgroup definition is too coarse because context and demographics interact. The chest X-ray adapter paper asks whether model adaptation changes subgroup reliability in a task-dependent way that aggregate accuracy cannot predict. For my research direction, this means a useful PhD proposal should not only detect elderly-group degradation; it should explain whether the degradation comes from sample support, contextual-intersectional shift, representation entanglement, or architecture-specific evidence routing.

## 3. Two Research Questions

### Question 1

English:

- Research question: Under historical portrait domain shift, how much of the elderly-group MAE gap remains after accounting for subgroup sample size, image quality, archive source, and time-period composition?
- Why it matters: My thesis found elderly degradation, but FRAME suggests that the next step is to separate apparent subgroup gaps from residual mechanism-level failure.
- Possible data: my historical portrait dataset with age group, gender, source archive, period, image-quality proxy, and model residuals; optionally modern-to-historical transfer splits.
- Possible method: construct a fair-reference residual distribution for subgroup MAE gaps, then compare observed elderly gaps against this reference and model the residual gap with representation-distance and image-quality covariates.
- Evaluation metric: elderly MAE gap, fair-reference exceedance probability, residual underestimation rate, subgroup calibration error, representation drift score, and confidence intervals under bootstrap resampling.
- Thesis connection: this directly extends aggregate metric masking and elderly-group degradation into a statistically disciplined failure-diagnosis protocol.
- Risk or limitation: subgroup sample sizes may be too small after adding archive and context intersections, so the method needs minimum-support rules and uncertainty reporting.

中文:

- 研究问题: 在 historical portrait domain shift 下, 控制 subgroup sample size、image quality、archive source 和 time-period composition 后, elderly-group MAE gap 还剩多少?
- 重要性: 我的 thesis 发现 elderly degradation, 但 FRAME 提醒我下一步要区分表面 gap 和真正的 mechanism-level failure。
- 可能数据: 我的 historical portrait 数据, 包含 age group、gender、source archive、period、image-quality proxy 和 model residuals; 也可以加入 modern-to-historical transfer split。
- 可能方法: 为 subgroup MAE gap 构造 fair-reference residual distribution, 再把 observed elderly gap 与 reference 比较, 并用 representation-distance 和 image-quality covariates 解释剩余 gap。
- 评估指标: elderly MAE gap、fair-reference exceedance probability、residual underestimation rate、subgroup calibration error、representation drift score 和 bootstrap confidence interval。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: Do contextual-intersectional audit groups explain why simple age regression generalized better than multi-task or cascaded age-gender models in historical facial age estimation?
- Why it matters: My thesis observed that simpler regression models were more stable, but did not fully explain whether the instability came from architecture, context, subgroup routing, or representation access.
- Possible data: predictions and embeddings from my regression, classification, multi-task, and cascaded models, joined with age group, gender, source archive, time period, and visual-quality/context proxies.
- Possible method: apply a CIFA-style audit to compare age-only, context-only, and contextual-intersectional residual gaps across architectures, then test whether richer heads or cascaded routing increase worst-group residual variance.

- Thesis connection: this directly links cascade fragility, balancing trade-offs, and historical domain shift to a testable audit design.
- Risk or limitation: contextual labels may be noisy or proxy-derived, so sensitivity analysis is needed before interpreting one hidden subgroup as a stable mechanism.
