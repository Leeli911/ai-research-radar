## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: KAISEN: Reproducible Subgroup Fairness Auditing for Clinical Risk Models
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28608
Year / Venue: 2026 / arXiv
Source: arXiv primary page
Research line: clinical risk auditing, subgroup fairness, mechanism diagnostics, drift monitoring
Research function: Diagnosis, Intervention, Monitoring, and Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 5; proposal citation value 5; feasibility 4; strategic long-term fit 5.
Usefulness: proposal anchor and Recommended for paper card

#### English Summary

- Problem: clinical risk models can show strong aggregate performance while producing materially different error rates across patient subgroups, but audit pipelines themselves are rarely stress-tested to see which components remain trustworthy.
- Method: KAISEN defines a five-phase audit pipeline: subgroup stratification, disparity measurement, mechanism diagnostics, post-hoc mitigation, and drift monitoring. It evaluates the pipeline to failure on a synthetic benchmark with 16 disease tasks, 15 social-determinant axes from Healthy People 2030, and three prespecified intersections.
- Evaluation: the paper reports equalized-odds differences, minimum detectable effects, per-group threshold optimization, group-wise Platt scaling, mechanism-diagnostic recovery, and CUSUM drift-monitor false alarms/misses across held-out runs and cohort seeds.
- Findings: threshold optimization consistently reduces EOD in the synthetic held-out runs, while Platt scaling improves calibration but behaves inconsistently on EOD. Mechanism diagnostics fail silently under proxy misspecification, and CUSUM monitoring thresholds do not transfer cleanly across cohort realizations. The key warning is that audit averages can be less informative than run-to-run variance and failure modes.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: 临床风险模型即使 aggregate performance 很强, 也可能在不同 patient subgroup 上产生明显不同的 error rate。更重要的是, 它不是只审计模型, 而是审计 audit pipeline 本身是否可靠。

为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 很贴近。我的 thesis 看到整体 MAE 可以掩盖 elderly-group failure, 而 KAISEN 进一步说明, 即使用了 subgroup audit, audit 的某些组件也可能在 cohort shift 或 proxy misspecification 下失效。

技术直觉: 一个可信的 fairness/reliability audit 不应该只报告平均 disparity 是否下降, 而要拆开看 stratification、metric sensitivity、mechanism diagnosis、post-hoc mitigation 和 drift monitoring 每一环是否稳定。KAISEN 的价值在于把 audit pipeline 推到 failure point, 暴露哪些结论只是特定 cohort seed 或特定阈值下成立。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: KAISEN starts from the same issue as my thesis: strong aggregate performance can hide subgroup-specific error. It also extends the warning to audit summaries, where mean mitigation effect can hide high variance across runs.
- Elderly-group degradation: Elderly portraits in my thesis can be treated as one vulnerable subgroup, but KAISEN reminds me to ask whether age is only one axis among intersections with source, image quality, gender, and period.
- Balancing trade-offs: The paper's comparison between threshold optimization and calibration-based mitigation helps explain why an intervention can improve one fairness metric while leaving another reliability objective unstable. This matches my balancing result where elderly performance worsened instead of uniformly improving.
- Cascade fragility: KAISEN's mechanism-diagnostic failure under proxy misspecification is relevant to cascaded conditional models: a pipeline can look structured and interpretable while silently routing or diagnosing the wrong mechanism.
- Historical domain shift: The CUSUM finding is directly useful for historical portraits. A monitoring threshold tuned on one archive or period may not transfer to another archive realization, so monitoring needs source-aware validation rather than a single global alarm rule.

#### Limitation

The benchmark is synthetic with known ground truth and does not establish clinical validity. I should use it as an audit-design and failure-analysis reference, not as evidence that a specific clinical mitigation method is deployment-ready.

#### Follow-up

Deep-read this paper for the exact audit phases and failure cases. It can help me design a thesis-extension audit where subgroup metrics, residual diagnostics, mechanism labels, and drift thresholds are each stress-tested.

### Paper 2

Title: Fair and Calibrated Toxicity Detection with Robust Training and Abstention
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14074
Year / Venue: 2026 / arXiv
Source: arXiv primary page
Research line: subgroup calibration, fairness intervention trade-offs, abstention reliability
Research function: Explanation, Intervention, Monitoring, and Evaluation
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 4; proposal citation value 4; feasibility 5; strategic long-term fit 5.
Usefulness: method caution for balancing, calibration, and abstention under subgroup disparity

#### English Summary

- Problem: toxicity classifiers can look fair or calibrated under aggregate metrics while identity subgroups experience non-uniform calibration error, ranking gaps, and unequal benefit from abstention.
- Method: the paper compares ERM, instance-level reweighting, and Group DRO, then combines them with temperature scaling, confidence-based abstention, and per-identity threshold optimization.
- Evaluation: it evaluates subgroup AUC, BPSN/BNSP AUC, error gaps, per-subgroup ECE, bootstrap confidence intervals, and risk-coverage behavior.
- Findings: aggregate calibration can hide large identity-subgroup miscalibration. Reweighting improves ranking but can worsen calibration-fairness gaps; Group DRO removes calibration disparity by becoming globally miscalibrated; temperature scaling fails when miscalibration is non-uniform; and abstention can help background content more than identity-mentioning content.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: fairness 不能只看 ranking, calibration 也不能只看 aggregate ECE, abstention 也不能默认是安全机制。一个模型可能整体 calibration 很好, 但每个 identity subgroup 都明显 miscalibrated。

为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 中 "整体 MAE 好看但 elderly group 失败" 的逻辑非常一致。它把隐藏失败从 error level 扩展到 calibration 和 abstention level: 模型不只是会错, 还会对某些 subgroup 错得更自信, 或者让某些 subgroup 更少受益于 abstention。

技术直觉: training-time intervention 和 post-hoc safety mechanism 不是独立的。Reweighting、Group DRO、temperature scaling、abstention 都会继承前一阶段留下的 subgroup failure mode。如果 failure 是 non-uniform calibration, 一个全局温度或全局 deferral rule 可能只修好平均表现, 却扩大 subgroup-level harm。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: Near-perfect aggregate calibration in this paper still hides large identity-subgroup ECE gaps, mirroring how global MAE hid elderly-group degradation in my thesis.
- Elderly-group degradation: The identity subgroup role can transfer to elderly portraits: elderly samples may have wider residuals, worse calibration, and weaker abstention protection even if the full model looks reliable.
- Balancing trade-offs: The reweighting result is a direct caution for my balancing experiments. A data intervention can improve one axis, such as ranking, while worsening calibration or subgroup reliability.
- Cascade fragility: The paper shows that post-hoc methods inherit training failure modes. This is analogous to a cascaded gender-conditioned age model where downstream age heads inherit upstream routing and representation errors.
- Historical domain shift: In historical portraits, a global calibration or abstention threshold may not transfer across age, source, and period. This paper supports subgroup-conditioned calibration and risk-coverage evaluation rather than a single confidence rule.

#### Limitation

The task is toxicity classification, not visual age regression. I should use it as a technically grounded fairness-calibration analogue, while avoiding a generic NLP framing.

#### Follow-up

Adapt the paper's multi-axis evaluation idea to historical facial age estimation: subgroup MAE, subgroup calibration, residual risk-coverage curves, and abstention benefit by age group and source.

### Paper 3

Title: Drift-to-Action Controllers: Budgeted Interventions with Online Risk Certificates
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08578
Year / Venue: 2026 / CAO Workshop at ICLR 2026
Source: arXiv primary page
Research line: drift monitoring, online risk certification, intervention selection under budget
Research function: Monitoring and Intervention
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 5; proposal citation value 4; feasibility 4; strategic long-term fit 4.
Usefulness: method reference for decision-relevant monitoring under shift

#### English Summary

- Problem: deployed ML systems face distribution drift, but many monitoring pipelines stop at alarms and do not specify what action should be taken under label delay, compute cost, latency constraints, and safety requirements.
- Method: Drift2Act combines an unlabeled sensing layer, a belief over drift types, and an active risk certificate that queries a small set of delayed labels from a recent window to produce an anytime-valid upper bound on current risk. The certificate gates low-cost actions, abstention/handoff, rollback, or retraining.
- Evaluation: the paper uses a realistic streaming protocol with label delay and explicit intervention costs on WILDS Camelyon17, DomainNet, and a controlled synthetic drift stream.
- Findings: online risk certification reduces safety violations and enables faster recovery at moderate cost compared with alarm-only monitoring, adapt-always adaptation, scheduled retraining, selective prediction alone, and a non-certified ablation.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: drift detection 不能停在 "alarm"。真实部署中还要决定什么时候 recalibration、什么时候 test-time adaptation、什么时候 abstain/handoff, 什么时候 rollback 或 retrain, 而且这些动作都有 label delay、compute 和 intervention cost。

为什么重要: 我的 research direction 里 monitoring 不能只是画 dashboard。它必须和 decision-relevant action 连接。Drift2Act 把 monitoring 变成 constrained decision-making, 用少量延迟标签给当前 risk 一个上界, 再决定是否允许低成本修复或升级到更强干预。

技术直觉: 不同 drift 类型不应该触发同一种动作。先用 unlabeled signal 形成 drift belief, 再用少量近期标签验证风险上界。如果风险还在阈值内, 可以用轻量 adaptation; 如果风险超过阈值, 就应该 abstain、handoff、rollback 或 retrain。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: Drift2Act focuses on current risk certificates rather than retrospective average scores, which fits my concern that global MAE can remain acceptable while specific reliability regions fail.
- Elderly-group degradation: A thesis extension could compute risk certificates separately for elderly or high-uncertainty historical portraits, not only for the full test stream.
- Balancing trade-offs: If balancing or adaptation is treated as a low-cost action, Drift2Act's gate suggests that it should only be applied when certified risk remains acceptable. This avoids assuming balancing is always beneficial.
- Cascade fragility: The action gate is relevant to cascaded models because a fragile routing stage should trigger handoff or rollback when subgroup risk rises, rather than silently passing samples downstream.
- Historical domain shift: Historical archives naturally form temporal streams. Drift2Act gives me a way to translate archive-period shift into a monitoring-and-action protocol with label delay and intervention budgets.

#### Limitation

The method is more deployment-protocol oriented than subgroup-audit oriented. For my proposal, its value is strongest when adapted to subgroup-conditioned certificates rather than used as a general monitoring citation.

#### Follow-up

Use this paper to define a practical monitoring question: when elderly-source risk exceeds a certified bound, should the system widen intervals, abstain, recalibrate, or retrain?
