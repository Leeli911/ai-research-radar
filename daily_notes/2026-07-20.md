## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Deployment-readiness audit of calibration, clinical utility, and fairness in perioperative infection prediction
Link: https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.15.26355656v1
Year / Venue: 2026 medRxiv preprint
Source: official medRxiv record, verified 2026-07-20
Research line: deployment-readiness evaluation, subgroup calibration, clinical utility, fairness-aware recalibration
Research function: Diagnosis / Intervention / Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor and Recommended for paper card

#### English Summary

- Problem: perioperative infection prediction can look strong under aggregate discrimination metrics, while deployment-relevant properties such as calibration, net clinical benefit, and subgroup fairness remain unstable.
- Method: the paper audits out-of-fold model predictions with AUROC/AUPRC, threshold error-rate gaps, calibration curves, calibration intercept and slope, expected calibration error, integrated calibration index, decision-curve net benefit, and bootstrap uncertainty.
- Evaluation: the audit analyzes 10,719 surgical procedures with 504 postoperative bacterial infection events. The prespecified subgroup axes are recorded sex, age strata, and an EHR-derived physiological-reserve proxy.
- Findings: strong average discrimination and reassuring sex-marginal metrics are not enough. The paper identifies physiologic-reserve-stratified miscalibration that changes clinical utility at the monitoring threshold, while AUROC also falls at age extremes and in pre-frail patients.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: 一个临床预测模型即使整体 AUROC 很高，也不代表已经 deployment-ready。真正部署时，模型需要在不同 age group、recorded sex 和 physiological-reserve subgroup 中保持 calibration、clinical utility 和 fairness。

为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 非常接近。我的 thesis 不是只发现 global MAE 的问题，而是发现 elderly group 的 residual、underestimation 和 variance pattern 被整体指标掩盖。这个 paper 把同样问题放到临床部署语境中: aggregate performance 不能替代 subgroup decision utility。

技术直觉: 论文的关键不是提出一个更复杂模型，而是把 evaluation panel 做得更接近真实决策。AUROC 只说明排序能力，calibration 说明概率是否可信，decision curve 说明阈值下是否有临床净收益，subgroup fairness gap 说明某些人群是否承担更高风险。对我的研究来说，这相当于把 age-estimation 的 global MAE 扩展为 subgroup residual + calibration + decision-threshold utility panel。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 这篇论文直接支持我的核心观察: global AUROC 或 global MAE 都可能掩盖 subgroup-level failure。我的 thesis 中 overall MAE 不能说明 elderly portraits 是否可靠；这里 overall discrimination 不能说明 perioperative subgroups 是否 deployment-ready。
- Elderly-group degradation: paper 中 age and functional-reserve subgroup audit 可以类比我的 elderly group。它提醒我，elderly degradation 不只是 error 大，也可能表现为 calibration slope shift、threshold utility loss 或 false reassurance。
- Balancing trade-offs: paper 的 audit 发生在使用 SMOTE 的 training pipeline 之后，但 deployment-relevant subgroup miscalibration 仍然存在。这个结果可以帮助我解释为什么 thesis 中 balancing sometimes worsened elderly performance: intervention 可能改善样本结构或 aggregate behavior，却没有保证 vulnerable subgroup 的 decision-relevant reliability。
- Cascade fragility: paper 的 threshold-based deployment decision and subgroup-aware recalibration framing 提醒我，多阶段或条件化决策会把 upstream score instability 转化为 downstream utility gaps。我的 gender-conditional cascade 也可能在 route-level calibration 不稳时放大 elderly error。
- Historical domain shift: paper 的 deployment-readiness logic 可以迁移到 historical portrait 的 source/period shift。我的研究可以把 clinical deployment strata 改写成 modern/historical or archive-source splits，然后检查 calibration and utility 是否随 domain shift 发生 subgroup-specific degradation。

#### Limitation

The paper is a clinical prediction audit, not a computer-vision age-regression paper. I need to translate clinical utility into age-estimation decision contexts, such as demographic reconstruction thresholds, elderly detection reliability, or abstention/review decisions for high-risk historical portraits.

#### Follow-up

Build a deployment-readiness panel for thesis models: global MAE, elderly MAE, residual skew, calibration error, underestimation rate, thresholded elderly miss rate, and subgroup-specific net utility for historical demographic decisions.

### Paper 2

Title: Locally Certified Worst-Group Reliability under Covariate Shift: When Subgroup Alarms Are Evidence
Link: https://zenodo.org/records/19708008
Year / Venue: 2026 Zenodo preprint
Source: official Zenodo record, verified 2026-07-20
Research line: worst-group reliability, covariate shift, subgroup alarm evidence, local certification
Research function: Diagnosis / Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for statistically disciplined subgroup monitoring

#### English Summary

- Problem: subgroup monitoring under covariate shift can produce many apparent alarms, but small local sample support makes it hard to know which alarms reflect real worst-group reliability degradation.
- Method: the paper proposes local confidence certificates for subgroup reliability under covariate shift, using ideas such as local support and effective sample size to decide when a subgroup alarm is evidence rather than noise.
- Evaluation: the paper studies subgroup reliability claims under shifted covariate distributions, focusing on when worst-group estimates are statistically supportable.
- Findings: the central message is that worst-group monitoring needs evidence accounting. A subgroup failure signal should be interpreted together with local support, otherwise the monitoring system may overreact to unstable slices or ignore true failures with weak aggregate visibility.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: subgroup monitoring 不能只看哪个 subgroup 的指标最差。很多 worst-group alarm 可能来自样本太少、covariate shift 后 local support 不足，或者估计方差太大。真正的问题是: 这个 alarm 是否有足够 evidence。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 中 elderly group error 明显较高，但如果进一步拆到 elderly + gender + source + image quality，某些 slice 可能非常小。这个 paper 提醒我，不能把所有高误差 slice 都直接当成稳定结论，需要同时报告 local support and uncertainty。

技术直觉: 论文的技术直觉是把 reliability alarm 和 evidence strength 绑定。under covariate shift, target subgroup 的 effective sample size 可能很低，所以 worst-group metric 需要 certificate。对我的研究来说，这可以转化为: 每个 elderly/high-residual slice 不只报告 MAE，也报告 support score、confidence interval、sample-weight stability 和 alarm credibility。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: global MAE hides subgroup failure, but this paper adds another issue: subgroup metric 本身也可能不可靠。我的 evaluation 需要同时避免 global masking 和 noisy subgroup overclaim。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly degradation 是我最重要的 thesis finding，但 deeper elderly slices 可能样本不足。local certificate 可以帮助我区分 stable elderly failure 与 evidence-weak slice artifact。
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing changes subgroup sample support and weight structure. 如果 balancing 后 elderly error 上升，我需要判断这是 stable degradation 还是由 local support/variance 改变造成的 alarm instability。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded gender-conditional model 会把样本分到不同 branches，降低某些 branch-subgroup 的 effective sample size。local certification 可以解释为什么 cascade route 下的 elderly estimates 更脆弱。
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits often have uneven period/source coverage. Under archive-source shift, some subgroup alarms may be real but hard to certify because target support is sparse.

#### Limitation

The paper is more statistical and certification-oriented than my current thesis pipeline. I need to keep the adaptation lightweight: confidence intervals, effective sample size, bootstrap stability, and slice-support diagnostics may be more realistic than implementing the full theory immediately.

#### Follow-up

Add an evidence layer to subgroup reliability reporting: each age/gender/source slice should include metric value, confidence interval, effective sample size, source-support score, and alarm status.

### Paper 3

Title: Beyond the Training Distribution: Evaluating Predictions Under Distribution Shift and Selection Bias
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14506
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint
Source: official arXiv record, verified 2026-07-20
Research line: target-risk estimation, covariate shift, selection bias, high-stakes model evaluation
Research function: Diagnosis / Evaluation
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for target-population evaluation under incomplete deployment labels

#### English Summary

- Problem: model evaluation usually assumes labeled validation data represent deployment, but high-stakes settings often have both distribution shift and selection bias, where labels are observed only for selected cases.
- Method: the paper develops statistically efficient target-risk estimators using modern causal inference and debiased machine learning ideas. It targets the risk the model would have in the deployment population, not just the observed validation sample.
- Evaluation: the paper uses simulations and an eICU mortality prediction case study to compare estimators under covariate shift and selection bias.
- Findings: naive evaluation can misestimate deployment risk when target covariates and label-observation mechanisms differ. Correcting for both shift and selection gives a more decision-relevant estimate of whether a model is reliable in the population where it will be used.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: validation data 不一定代表 deployment population，而且 deployment 中的 label 可能不是随机观察到的。例如高风险病人更可能有完整 outcome，低风险病人 label 缺失，或者 target population 的 covariate distribution 已经和训练时期不同。

为什么重要: 我的 historical portrait task 也有类似问题。某些时期、来源或年龄组可能更容易有标注，某些 elderly portraits 的真实年龄可能更不完整或更不准确。如果我只在 observed labeled subset 上算 MAE，就可能低估真实 target-domain risk。

技术直觉: 论文把 evaluation 视为 target-risk estimation，而不是 ordinary test-set scoring。它使用 shift correction and selection-bias correction 来估计 deployment population 中的 expected loss。对我的研究来说，这可以转化为: 在 historical source/period shift 下，估计 model 在真实 archival target distribution 中的 subgroup risk，而不是只在有年龄标签的样本上评估。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: ordinary validation MAE may be biased if labeled samples do not represent the target historical population. 这进一步解释了 aggregate metric masking: 不仅 metric 会平均掉 subgroup failure，validation sample 本身也可能偏。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly labels may be rarer, noisier, or concentrated in certain archive sources. Target-risk estimation can test whether observed elderly MAE underestimates actual elderly risk.
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing can alter the evaluation distribution if I evaluate on artificially balanced data. The paper reminds me to distinguish training intervention distribution from deployment target distribution.
- Cascade fragility: cascade performance can be misestimated when selection bias differs by route. If gender labels or age labels are observed unevenly, route-level risk estimates may look more stable than they are.
- Historical domain shift: this is the strongest connection. Historical portrait evaluation needs target-population risk under period/source shift and possible selective labeling, not only standard held-out test MAE.

#### Limitation

The paper is methodologically heavier than my current CV pipeline because it draws from causal inference and debiased machine learning. I should first use it as a conceptual guide for target-risk framing, then prototype simpler inverse-probability weighting or doubly robust checks if metadata support is sufficient.

#### Follow-up

Audit whether thesis labels are selectively observed across age, gender, source, and historical period. If selection patterns exist, compare ordinary MAE with target-weighted MAE and subgroup-weighted elderly MAE.

## 3. Two Research Questions

### Question 1

English:

- Research question: Under historical portrait domain shift, can an evidence-aware subgroup reliability panel distinguish stable elderly-group degradation from noisy small-slice alarms?
- Why it matters: my thesis found elderly-group degradation hidden by global MAE, but deeper slices such as elderly + gender + archive source may be statistically fragile.
- Possible data: thesis portrait images, true age, predicted age, gender labels, archive source, historical period, image-quality proxies, visual embeddings, and natural versus balanced training variants.
- Possible method: compute subgroup MAE, residual skew, underestimation rate, calibration error, confidence intervals, bootstrap stability, effective sample size, and source-support score for each age/gender/source slice.
- Evaluation metric: global MAE, elderly MAE, worst-slice MAE, alarm stability, confidence interval width, effective sample size, residual tail rate, and false-alarm sensitivity under resampling.
- Thesis connection: directly tests aggregate metric masking, elderly degradation, balancing trade-offs, cascade fragility, and historical domain shift while adding an evidence layer to subgroup claims.
- Risk or limitation: small elderly-source intersections may remain too sparse for strong conclusions, so the method should report uncertainty rather than force every slice into a reliable ranking.

中文:

研究问题: 在 historical portrait domain shift 下，evidence-aware subgroup reliability panel 能否区分稳定的 elderly-group degradation 和 noisy small-slice alarms？

这个问题现实可做，因为它建立在我的 thesis evaluation pipeline 上，只需要加入 confidence interval、bootstrap stability、effective sample size 和 source-support diagnostics。它能避免两个极端: 一方面不让 global MAE 掩盖 elderly failure，另一方面也不把每个小 slice 的高误差过度解释成稳定结论。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: When historical age labels are selectively observed across archive sources or age groups, how much does ordinary validation MAE underestimate target elderly-group risk?
- Why it matters: my thesis evaluation may be biased if labeled portraits are not representative of the historical target population, especially for elderly samples.
- Possible data: thesis labels, source metadata, period metadata, age/gender labels, missing-label indicators, image-quality proxies, predicted age, residuals, and source-level sample counts.
- Possible method: model label-observation probability, estimate target-weighted MAE, compare ordinary validation MAE with inverse-probability weighted or doubly robust risk estimates, and repeat the comparison for elderly and low-support strata.
- Evaluation metric: ordinary MAE, target-weighted MAE, elderly target-weighted MAE, risk-estimate confidence interval, calibration error, underestimation rate, and difference between natural and balanced evaluation distributions.
- Thesis connection: reframes aggregate metric masking as both an aggregation problem and a sampling/selection problem under historical domain shift.
- Risk or limitation: selection correction depends on observed metadata; if label missingness depends on unobserved archival processes, the analysis should be presented as sensitivity analysis rather than definitive correction.

中文:

研究问题: 如果 historical age labels 在不同 archive sources 或 age groups 中是 selectively observed，ordinary validation MAE 会在多大程度上低估 target elderly-group risk？

这个问题把我的 thesis 结果推进到 target-risk evaluation。重点不是只问模型在 labeled test set 上误差多大，而是问这个 labeled test set 是否代表真实 historical target population，尤其是否低估了 elderly subgroup 的风险。
