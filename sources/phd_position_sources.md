# PhD Position Sources

Use this file to track PhD opportunities, potential supervisors, labs, and research groups. The goal is not only to find openings, but to learn how labs frame research problems.

## Position Platforms

- FindAPhD: https://www.findaphd.com/
- jobs.ac.uk PhD: https://www.jobs.ac.uk/search/phd
- Euraxess: https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs
- Academic Positions: https://academicpositions.com/
- OpenReview profiles and lab pages for active researchers
- University department pages for computer science, AI, machine learning, HCI, and data science

## Search Keywords

Use combinations of:

- trustworthy machine learning PhD
- distribution shift PhD
- model monitoring PhD
- fairness machine learning PhD
- AI governance PhD
- responsible AI PhD
- AI auditing PhD
- multimodal misinformation PhD
- AIGC detection PhD
- provenance detection PhD

## Fit Criteria

High-fit positions usually mention at least two of:

- distribution shift
- trustworthy ML
- evaluation under real-world deployment
- fairness or subgroup robustness
- model monitoring
- AI governance or auditing
- multimodal authenticity
- data quality and dataset construction

Do not apply only because a position says "AI" or "large language models." Look for research fit, supervision fit, and evidence that my background can become an advantage.
