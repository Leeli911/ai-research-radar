## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Testing the Test: Score-Direction Instability in Class-Split Anomaly Detection
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02601
Year / Venue: 2026, arXiv
Source: official arXiv / Hugging Face paper metadata
Research line: anomaly-detection evaluation under representation-space instability
Research function: Diagnosis / Evaluation
Screening Score: 21/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for decision-relevant evaluation under hidden shift

#### English Summary

- Problem: class-split anomaly-detection benchmarks can make anomaly scores appear reliable even when the score direction depends on which classes are treated as normal or anomalous. This creates a hidden evaluation failure: a method may rank anomalies correctly in one split but invert or weaken its signal in another split.
- Method: the paper analyzes score-direction instability through representation-space overlap and neighborhood leakage between normal and anomaly classes. Instead of only asking whether an anomaly detector gets a high benchmark score, it asks whether the score keeps the same semantic direction across class splits.
- Evaluation: the study evaluates anomaly-detection settings where within-dataset class splits are used as a proxy for out-of-distribution testing, with attention to latent-space neighborhoods and how unknown anomaly characteristics affect score behavior.
- Findings: class-split evaluation can be unstable when anomaly classes are not cleanly separated from normal classes in representation space. A high aggregate anomaly-detection score can therefore hide protocol-specific reliability failure.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 anomaly detection evaluation 本身不稳定的问题。某个 anomaly score 在一个 class split 中可能代表 "more anomalous"，但换一个 split 后，score direction 可能变弱甚至反向。
- 为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 中 aggregate MAE masking 很接近。我的模型如果只在一个 validation split 上表现稳定，并不能证明 residual、confidence 或 embedding distance 在不同 historical source 和 subgroup 中都有同样含义。
- 技术直觉: 论文的核心直觉是，evaluation protocol 不是中性的。如果 normal/anomaly classes 在 representation space 中互相重叠，score 的含义会依赖 split。对我的研究来说，这相当于提醒我检查 "age error score" 或 "uncertainty score" 在不同 subgroup/source 中是否保持方向一致。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 这篇论文说明 benchmark-level AUROC 或 anomaly score 可以掩盖 split-specific score-direction failure。我的 thesis 中 global MAE 也可能掩盖 elderly group 的 residual direction 和 underestimation pattern。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits may form a representation region where age cues overlap with younger or poor-quality portraits. If score direction is unstable there, elderly degradation may appear as noisy residuals rather than a clean subgroup failure.
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing can change which samples define the local representation neighborhood. This helps explain why my balancing experiment could improve some averages while worsening elderly-group behavior.
- Cascade fragility: cascaded conditional models depend on upstream score semantics. If upstream gender confidence or age-group confidence changes direction across shifted regions, downstream conditional heads inherit unstable routing signals.
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits create source, era, pose, and quality shifts. This paper suggests that I should test whether reliability scores preserve direction across historical-source splits, not only whether the average validation metric looks acceptable.

#### Limitation

The paper studies anomaly detection rather than age regression. I would need to translate score-direction instability into regression diagnostics such as residual sign, underestimation probability, or uncertainty-error monotonicity.

#### Follow-up

Test whether uncertainty, embedding distance, or residual magnitude keeps the same relationship to error for elderly and non-elderly portraits across archive-source splits.

### Paper 2

Title: Exposing the Illusion of Fairness: Auditing Vulnerabilities to Distributional Manipulation Attacks
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20708
Year / Venue: 2025, arXiv
Source: official arXiv / Hugging Face paper metadata
Research line: fairness auditing under manipulated or unrepresentative evaluation distributions
Research function: Diagnosis / Evaluation
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal citation for aggregate fairness-metric masking

#### English Summary

- Problem: fairness audits can be vulnerable when the evaluation distribution is manipulated or unrepresentative. A classifier may appear fair under a reported group-level metric while still preserving harmful bias in the underlying population.
- Method: the paper studies distributional manipulation attacks against fairness auditing and proposes statistical tests to detect when a fairness claim may be produced by changing the audited distribution rather than correcting the classifier.
- Evaluation: the paper evaluates binary-classification fairness settings using group fairness metrics and distributional distance ideas, including representativeness checks and statistical tests for suspicious audit distributions.
- Findings: group-level fairness metrics can produce an illusion of fairness if the audit sample is distributionally distorted. This makes representativeness and distributional audit design part of the fairness evaluation problem, not just data preprocessing details.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 fairness metric 容易被 evaluation distribution 操纵的问题。模型可能并没有真正减少 bias，只是 audit sample 的分布被改变后，group fairness metric 看起来更好。
- 为什么重要: 这直接连接我的 thesis。我的 balancing experiment 也改变了样本分布，所以我不能只问 balancing 后某个平均指标是否更好，还要问这个指标是否仍然代表真实 deployment/historical distribution。
- 技术直觉: fairness 不是只算一个 group metric。需要同时检查模型输出、subgroup metric 和 evaluation sample 是否代表真实人群。否则，metric improvement 可能只是 distributional accounting，而不是 reliability improvement。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 论文强调 fairness metric can look acceptable while hidden bias remains. 我的 thesis 中 global MAE 也可以因为样本组合改变而看起来稳定，但 elderly failure 仍然存在。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly-group reliability depends on whether elderly samples in validation are representative of the real historical elderly population. If the audit distribution under-samples hard elderly portraits, subgroup degradation will be understated.
- Balancing trade-offs: this is the clearest thesis bridge. My balancing intervention may have changed metric composition rather than improving elderly reliability. This paper gives a language for separating genuine subgroup repair from distributional metric manipulation.
- Cascade fragility: final fairness metrics can miss upstream conditional errors. In my cascaded gender-conditional model, a fair-looking final aggregate could still hide gender-routing errors that disproportionately affect elderly age estimates.
- Historical domain shift: historical portrait datasets are naturally unrepresentative across era, archive source, image quality, and social class. This paper supports treating historical evaluation design as a distributional audit problem.

#### Limitation

The paper is focused on binary fairness metrics and adversarial or manipulated distributions. My thesis setting is not adversarial, so I should adapt the idea as unintentional distributional distortion rather than assume malicious manipulation.

#### Follow-up

Add an audit-distribution sensitivity check to historical age-estimation evaluation: recompute subgroup reliability under source-balanced, era-balanced, and original historical population mixtures.

### Paper 3

Title: MCGrad: Multicalibration at Web Scale
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19884
Year / Venue: 2025, arXiv
Source: official arXiv / Hugging Face paper metadata
Research line: scalable multicalibration for many subgroups without manual group specification
Research function: Intervention / Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor and paper-card candidate

#### English Summary

- Problem: a model can be globally calibrated while remaining miscalibrated on many meaningful subgroups. Manual subgroup lists are incomplete, and protected-attribute evaluation alone can miss hidden reliability failures.
- Method: MCGrad is a scalable multicalibration algorithm that searches over subgroup structure and improves calibration using gradient-based updates. The method aims to improve subgroup reliability without requiring every subgroup to be manually specified in advance.
- Evaluation: the paper reports large-scale deployment-style evaluation across many machine-learning models, with metrics such as log loss and area under the precision-recall curve, and with attention to calibration improvements across protected and non-manually defined groups.
- Findings: multicalibration can improve reliability across subgroups at scale, suggesting that subgroup calibration can be treated as an operational monitoring and correction problem rather than only a fairness-reporting exercise.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 global calibration 不等于 subgroup calibration 的问题。一个模型整体概率校准看起来不错，但在某些 hidden subgroup 或 protected group 上仍然过度自信或低估风险。
- 为什么重要: 这和我的 research direction 很贴合。我的 thesis 不是只需要报告 elderly MAE，而是需要知道模型的 confidence、residual 和预测区间是否在 elderly portraits、不同 archive source 和不同 visual style 中都可靠。
- 技术直觉: multicalibration 的思想是不断寻找 calibration 错误集中的子群，并对模型输出做局部修正。它把 subgroup reliability 从 "先定义群体再评估" 推向 "从模型行为中发现哪些群体需要校准"。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: MCGrad directly challenges global calibration and global performance summaries. This mirrors my thesis finding that global MAE can hide elderly-group failure.
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits can be treated as one visible subgroup, but MCGrad also suggests searching for hidden intersections such as elderly plus low image quality, elderly plus specific archive source, or elderly plus underrepresented gender presentation.
- Balancing trade-offs: instead of assuming sample balancing will repair reliability, multicalibration targets output behavior. This could explain why my balancing sometimes hurt elderly performance: the intervention changed representation composition but did not guarantee calibrated subgroup predictions.
- Cascade fragility: cascaded conditional models need calibrated intermediate outputs. MCGrad suggests that upstream routing probabilities and downstream age predictions should be checked for subgroup calibration separately.
- Historical domain shift: because historical portrait data contain latent source and era groups, multicalibration could become a monitoring layer for archive-specific or era-specific reliability without requiring perfect demographic labels.

#### Limitation

The paper is deployment-scale and may assume infrastructure and data volume beyond my thesis setup. A smaller research version would need lightweight subgroup search and careful uncertainty estimates for small groups.

#### Follow-up

Prototype a small-scale multicalibration audit for historical facial age estimation using age group, gender, archive source, image-quality proxies, and embedding-neighborhood clusters.

### Question 1

English:

- Research question: How sensitive are elderly-group MAE, underestimation rate, and calibration error to different historical evaluation mixtures in facial age estimation?
- Why it matters: my thesis showed that aggregate metrics can hide elderly degradation, and today's fairness-audit paper suggests that metric conclusions can change when the audit distribution changes.
- Possible data: my thesis portrait dataset, age labels, gender labels, predicted ages, residuals, archive source, estimated era, and image-quality proxies.
- Possible method: construct original, source-balanced, age-balanced, gender-balanced, and era-balanced evaluation mixtures; compare global MAE with elderly MAE, residual skew, underestimation rate, and subgroup calibration error.
- Evaluation metric: sensitivity range of each metric across mixtures, worst-mixture elderly MAE, underestimation-rate gap, subgroup ECE, and rank stability of model variants.
- Thesis connection: directly tests aggregate metric masking, elderly-group degradation, balancing trade-offs, and historical domain shift.
- Risk or limitation: if some source-era-age cells are small, the evaluation must use confidence intervals or shrinkage estimates to avoid overinterpreting noisy subgroup metrics.

中文:

研究问题: historical facial age estimation 中的 elderly-group MAE、underestimation rate 和 calibration error 对不同 evaluation mixture 有多敏感？

这个问题把我的 balancing observation 变成一个可测试的 evaluation-distribution problem。实验可以比较 original mixture、source-balanced mixture、age-balanced mixture 和 era-balanced mixture，检查哪些结论只是样本构成导致的。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: Can lightweight multicalibration detect hidden elderly-source reliability failures that are not visible from global MAE?
- Why it matters: MCGrad suggests that global calibration can miss subgroup-local miscalibration, and my thesis suggests elderly failure may be concentrated in specific shifted visual regions.
- Possible data: thesis model predictions, confidence scores or prediction intervals, residuals, age/gender labels, archive source, image-quality features, and embedding-neighborhood clusters.
- Possible method: define candidate groups from visible labels and representation clusters; iteratively identify groups with calibration or residual bias; compare a calibrated audit model with the original model and with naive balancing.
- Evaluation metric: subgroup ECE, worst-group MAE, residual bias, elderly underestimation rate, high-error detection AUROC, and number of hidden groups discovered before global MAE changes.
- Thesis connection: directly extends residual diagnostics, elderly-group degradation, cascade fragility, and decision-relevant monitoring under historical domain shift.
- Risk or limitation: small subgroup sample sizes may make calibration estimates unstable, so the method needs pooled or hierarchical group estimates.

中文:

研究问题: lightweight multicalibration 能否发现 global MAE 看不到的 elderly-source hidden reliability failure？

这个问题适合我的背景，因为它不要求重新训练大型模型，而是利用已有 predictions、residuals、confidence 和 embeddings 做 subgroup-local calibration audit。它可以把 thesis 中的 residual plots 升级成 monitoring 方法。
