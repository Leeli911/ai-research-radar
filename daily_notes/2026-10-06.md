## Paper 1 — Fairness of Deep Ensembles: On the interplay between per-group task difficulty and under-representation

Estanislao Claucich, Sara Hooker, Diego H. Milone, Enzo Ferrante, Rodrigo Echeveste. **FAccT 2025**; arXiv first submitted **2025-01-24**. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14551) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.14551v1) · [Conference version](https://facctconference.org/static/docs/facct2025-206archivalpdfs/facct2025-final2123-acmpaginated.pdf).

**Research function:** Explanation → Intervention → Evaluation.

### English Summary

**Verified from paper**

- **Problem:** Equal group counts may not equalize learning conditions.
- **Method:** Vary representation and task difficulty; compare homogeneous ensembles.
- **Evaluation:** Synthetic noise/boundary experiments plus CelebA and CheXpert classification.
- **Findings:** Balanced sampling can be suboptimal; ensembles benefit disadvantaged groups in the studied settings. Real-data difficulty is proxied by balanced-training performance, which can confound diversity and model choice. These are not historical-shift guarantees. [Methods, results, limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.14551v1).

### 中文解释

论文把样本数量和学习难度分开：数量相同不代表学习条件相同。实验中，偏向较难群体的采样可能更好，集成也可能缩小差距。难度代理并非内在属性的证明，不能直接推出历史年龄回归也有效。[原文](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.14551v1)

### Connection to My Thesis

**Explicit Conceptual Bridge — my interpretation:** My balancing result could reflect an interaction between sample allocation and learnability, rather than a contradiction of fairness objectives.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection |
|---|---|
| Aggregate metric masking | I would report each group's absolute error as well as the gap; a smaller gap can coexist with unacceptable error. |
| Elderly-group degradation | I would test whether elderly images have lower effective visual diversity or more uncertain age labels, without treating age itself as an intrinsic difficulty cause. |
| Balancing trade-offs | I would vary age sampling ratios at fixed training size and optimization budget, separating extra information from repeated samples. |
| Cascade fragility | I would compare simple regression ensembles against cascades, including an oracle-routing diagnostic when reliable route labels exist. Ensemble gains would not by themselves identify routing failure. |
| Historical domain shift | I would select ratios on development archives and evaluate them unchanged on a held-out archive or period. |

**Methodological Relevance:** I can begin with frozen visual embeddings and small regression heads across several seeds. A full backbone ensemble is unnecessary for the first diagnostic.

**Reinterpretation of My Thesis Findings:** My finding that simpler regression was more stable motivates measuring residual correlation across models: averaging may help variance but cannot be assumed to remove shared elderly underestimation.

**中文连接：** 我会同时保留整体误差和老年组绝对误差，控制平衡实验的样本量与训练预算，比较简单回归和级联的错误来源，并在独立历史档案上检验结论。学习难度只是待检验的解释，不能把我的观察直接归因于群体本身。

**Needs full-paper verification:** Transfer of the sampling protocol to continuous age targets, uncertainty from archive sampling, and whether improvements survive label-quality controls.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection |

## Emerging Trend / 新兴趋势

**English — my synthesis:** I see reliability as a sequence of resource-allocation decisions: how training examples are allocated, which visual evidence a model uses, and which predictions receive review. Each stage needs subgroup-specific endpoints. A fair-looking allocation does not establish fair outcomes; a robust-looking feature does not establish that it remains available after deployment.

**中文——我的综合判断：** 我把今天的线索理解为三类分配问题：训练样本如何分配、模型依赖哪些视觉证据、复核预算分给哪些预测。每一步都要用子群结果验证。表面平衡不能替代实际可靠性，训练中的稳定线索也未必在历史档案中继续可见。这是三篇论文带来的研究综合，而不是对整个领域趋势的统计判断。

## Two Research Questions / 两个研究问题

### RQ1 — When does balancing fail after visual evidence loss?

- **Research question:** In my historical age-estimation setting, does increasing elderly sampling help on clean images but worsen elderly MAE after texture loss, and does this interaction differ between simple regression and cascaded models?
- **Why it matters:** I can test a mechanism connecting balancing backfire to historical shift rather than merely repeat a performance ranking.
- **Possible data:** My licensed thesis portraits with age and archive labels; a modern source dataset if permitted. I would split identities across partitions and reserve a whole archive for final testing.
- **Possible method:** Freeze embeddings initially; compare natural, equal-age-bin, and capped elderly-heavy sampling at matched sample count and training steps across five seeds. Apply prespecified blur/downsampling levels to paired evaluation images. Repeat the key comparison with fine-tuning only if justified. Use route-label diagnostics only where labels are reliable.
- **Evaluation metric:** Elderly and worst-age-bin MAE, mean signed residual, severe-underestimation rate, and the sampling-by-corruption interaction; bootstrap by identity with archive-level results shown separately. A useful result requires absolute elderly improvement, not just a smaller intergroup gap.
- **Thesis connection:** I directly test my balancing trade-offs, elderly underestimation, cascade fragility, and the stronger generalization of simple regression.
- **Risk or limitation:** Corruption is an imperfect model of historical shift; few archives limit inference. “Difficulty” must remain a measured operational property, not a demographic explanation.

**中文：** 我的研究问题是：增加老年样本比例是否在清晰图像上有效，却在纹理损失后增加老年 MAE；这种交互是否因简单回归和级联而不同？我会在固定预算下比较三种采样比例、五个随机种子与成对图像干预，按身份拆分并留出档案。指标包括老年绝对误差、最差年龄组误差、有符号残差和严重低估率。主要限制是人工退化不等于真实历史偏移，档案数少也限制统计外推。

### RQ2 — Can targeted review find confidently wrong elderly predictions?

- **Research question:** At a 5% review budget, can a source-trained error scorer with bounded group allocation recover more severe elderly underestimation than uncertainty-only review on a held-out historical archive?
- **Why it matters:** I can evaluate monitoring by missed harmful predictions and annotation cost.
- **Possible data:** Frozen thesis-model predictions, source calibration labels, and a fully labeled held-out archive for retrospective evaluation only.
- **Possible method:** Define severe underestimation as predicted age at least 10 years below the reference, with 5/15-year sensitivity checks. Define high confidence using interval width fixed on source calibration data. Compare random review, widest-interval review, global failure scoring, and bounded allocation among eligible predictions. Fit and tune the scorer on separate source folds; lock thresholds before target evaluation. Include a random audit reserve to inspect the ineligible region.
- **Evaluation metric:** Elderly failure recall, precision at budget, unreviewed severe-error rate, group review rates, and yield as budget varies. Report both eligible-region and full-population denominators. If fewer than 5% are eligible, report unused targeted capacity and the prespecified reserve rule.
- **Thesis connection:** I connect aggregate masking and confident cascade failures to a concrete subgroup monitoring endpoint, comparing balanced and unbalanced models under identical review rules.
- **Risk or limitation:** True age defines the elderly evaluation group but is often unavailable before review. Allocation must use independently available metadata or validated predicted-age strata; I would label true-age allocation as an oracle upper bound. Narrow intervals are not guaranteed calibrated after shift, and historical age references can be uncertain.

**中文：** 我的问题是：在 5% 复核预算下，源域训练的错误评分加有限群体配额，能否比单纯不确定性排序找回更多历史档案中的老年严重低估？我会固定事件定义和源域阈值，用完整目标标签仅做最终回顾评估，报告召回、预算内精度、未复核错误率和群体复核率。真实年龄通常在复核前不可得，因此配额只能使用独立元数据或经验证的预测分层；真实年龄配额只能作为 oracle 上界。高置信资格区外还需随机抽查。
