### Paper 1

Title: Missingness-Aware Conformal Prediction Under Cross-Hospital Distribution Shift
Link: https://openreview.net/forum?id=h2I7GN4hTh
Year / Venue: 2026 / ICML 2026 SD4H Workshop Poster
Research function: Diagnosis
Research line: subgroup-valid uncertainty under cross-hospital shift
Screening score: 24/25 = relevance 5 + novelty 4 + citation value 5 + feasibility 5 + long-term fit 5
Usefulness: proposal anchor

#### English Summary

- **Problem:** Standard split conformal prediction can achieve its target marginal coverage while clinically meaningful subgroups remain undercovered after deployment to another hospital. The paper focuses on shifts in missingness patterns as a concrete source of hidden subgroup failure.
- **Method:** The authors decompose the subgroup coverage gap into calibration heterogeneity and within-group shift. They propose a label-free rule that selects the variable with the largest cross-site missingness shift, divides samples into two missingness subgroups, and applies Mondrian calibration within those groups.
- **Evaluation:** The method is evaluated on GOSSIS and MIMIC-IV under cross-hospital deployment, using marginal and subgroup coverage gaps, prediction-set size, model-update stability, and deployment-time stress tests. It is compared with pooled conformal calibration and optimization-based alternatives.
- **Findings:** Pooled calibration can hide severe subgroup undercoverage even when marginal coverage is near 90%. The proposed calibration approximately halves the maximum subgroup gap on both datasets, changes set size by less than 1% on GOSSIS, and remains stable under model updates and stress tests where optimization-based baselines degrade.
- **Technical intuition:** Missingness is not merely a preprocessing nuisance; it is a deployment-context signal. If hospitals measure variables differently, pooled calibration mixes distinct uncertainty regimes. Calibrating within stable, missingness-defined groups removes between-group calibration heterogeneity without requiring target labels.

#### 中文解释

- **解决的问题:** 这篇论文说明，conformal prediction 的整体 coverage 达标，不代表每个临床 subgroup 都被可靠覆盖。不同医院的 missingness pattern 会变化，因此 pooled calibration 可能把某个 subgroup 的 undercoverage 平均掉。
- **为什么重要:** 这正是 aggregate metric masking 的 uncertainty 版本。系统报告 90% marginal coverage 时，某个 missingness subgroup 仍可能明显低于目标 coverage；如果只看整体指标，部署风险会被隐藏。
- **技术直觉:** 先找出跨医院 missingness shift 最大的变量，再按 missingness 状态分组做 Mondrian calibration。这样可以把不同 uncertainty regime 分开，而不需要目标医院标签。
- **核心发现:** 方法在 GOSSIS 和 MIMIC-IV 上显著缩小最大 subgroup coverage gap，同时几乎不增加 prediction-set size，并且在模型更新和 stress test 下更稳定。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- **Aggregate metric masking:** 这篇论文把我 thesis 中“整体 MAE 掩盖 elderly-group error”推广到 uncertainty evaluation：marginal coverage 也会掩盖 subgroup undercoverage。
- **Elderly-group degradation:** missingness-defined subgroup 和我的 elderly group 扮演相同的方法学角色，都是整体指标下容易被平均掉的 vulnerable subset。我可以把 age group、image quality 和 metadata missingness 联合用于 historical portrait reliability profiling。
- **Balancing trade-offs:** 论文说明，把所有样本混合后做统一 calibration 并不可靠；同样，简单 balancing 也可能忽略 subgroup 内部的 shift mechanism。干预应针对产生异质性的变量，而不是只改变总体比例。
- **Cascade fragility:** Mondrian calibration 仍然包含分组逻辑，但 subgroup assignment 对模型更新保持稳定。这给我的 cascaded gender-conditional model 一个重要对照：如果 routing 依赖不稳定的模型预测，shift 会逐级放大；如果分组来自稳定、可观测的 deployment signal，条件化可能更可靠。
- **Historical domain shift:** 历史人像的拍摄年代、图像质量、缺失 metadata 和档案来源也会形成不同 missingness/quality regime。这个方法可以转化为 age-group-specific calibration 或 residual interval evaluation。

#### Limitation

The subgroup definition is deliberately narrow and based on one selected missingness variable. Hidden visual subgroups or intersectional age-quality groups may require richer discovery methods, and the workshop-paper status means the full empirical assumptions need careful verification.

#### Follow-up

I should inspect the full paper's exact coverage definitions, model/task setup, and stress-test construction before using the method as a proposal anchor.

### Paper 2

Title: Failure detection in medical image classification under realistic distribution shifts: A large-scale benchmark
Link: https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.04.26350496v1
Year / Venue: 2026 / medRxiv preprint
Research function: Monitoring
Research line: uncertainty-based silent-failure prevention under visual shift
Screening score: 22/25 = relevance 5 + novelty 4 + citation value 4 + feasibility 5 + long-term fit 4
Usefulness: evaluation and method reference

#### English Summary

- **Problem:** Confidence scoring functions are often evaluated in incompatible settings, making it unclear which uncertainty signal can reliably detect incorrect medical-image predictions under realistic deployment shifts.
- **Method:** The study benchmarks eight confidence scoring functions from margin-based, prediction-variability, and latent feature-distance families, plus two score-aggregation strategies. It compares simple scores such as maximum softmax response with Monte Carlo dropout, deep ensembles, test-time augmentation, greedy augmentation search, and KNN distance in representation space.
- **Evaluation:** The benchmark spans eight medical-image classification tasks, ResNet18 and ViT backbones, four training setups, and 160 configurations. It evaluates in-distribution errors, image corruptions, population shifts, and new-class shifts using balanced accuracy, AUROC-F for failure ranking, and AUGRC for silent-failure risk across rejection thresholds.
- **Findings:** No individual confidence score dominates across settings. Aggregating complementary scores matches or approaches the best method and reduces the retained silent-failure proportion by an average of 82%, 76%, 67%, and 63% for in-distribution, corruption, population, and new-class shifts, respectively. Simple margin-based scores are comparatively robust under within-label-space shifts, while deep-ensemble variability is stronger for new-class shifts. Latent-space KNN usually underperforms.
- **Technical intuition:** A shift detector and a failure detector answer different questions. A sample can be OOD but correctly classified, or in-distribution and wrong. Monitoring should therefore rank actual prediction failures and evaluate the classifier-score pair jointly, rather than treating distance from the training distribution as a sufficient proxy for risk.

#### 中文解释

- **解决的问题:** 这篇论文不是再提出一个新的 uncertainty score，而是系统比较不同 failure detection 方法在真实 visual shift 下是否真的能识别错误预测。
- **为什么重要:** 只检测 OOD 不等于检测 failure。历史人像可能离训练分布很远但仍预测正确，也可能看起来接近训练分布却在 elderly group 上产生严重 underestimation。
- **技术直觉:** 同时评估 classifier accuracy 和 confidence ranking，并用 risk-coverage 指标衡量拒绝不确定样本后还剩多少 silent failure。多个互补 confidence signal 的 aggregation 比押注单一 uncertainty score 更稳。
- **核心发现:** 没有一个复杂方法在所有 shift 上占优。简单 maximum softmax response 在多种 covariate/population shift 下很有竞争力；deep ensemble 对 semantic new-class shift 更强；latent KNN 距离通常不可靠。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- **Aggregate metric masking:** 平均 balanced accuracy 或全局 MAE 不能说明 failure 是否被有效识别。AUGRC 的思路提醒我，需要报告“拒绝/复核机制之后仍然保留多少 subgroup failure”，而不是只报告模型平均误差。
- **Elderly-group degradation:** 我可以把 elderly underestimation 定义为需要被监控的 silent failure，并比较 confidence、residual magnitude、ensemble variance 和 representation distance 哪个信号最早识别这类样本。
- **Balancing trade-offs:** 不同 training setup 会改变 classifier accuracy，也会改变 confidence score 的有效性。因此 balancing 不能只按 MAE 判断，还应检查它是否让 elderly failures 更难被 uncertainty signal 识别。
- **Cascade fragility:** 如果 cascade 的第一阶段高置信度地路由错误，后续 age head 无法修复。这个 benchmark 支持在每个 routing stage 评估 failure detection，而不是只看最终输出。
- **Historical domain shift:** corruption、population 和 acquisition shift 与历史人像中的扫描噪声、来源变化、年代差异和图像质量变化高度相似。该 benchmark 的 AUROC-F/AUGRC protocol 可以直接迁移到历史视觉数据。

#### Limitation

The benchmark does not center demographic subgroup fairness, and its population-shift datasets do not by themselves establish elderly-group validity. I would need to stratify AUROC-F and risk-coverage results by age, gender, period, and image quality.

#### Follow-up

I should test whether simple residual- or margin-based signals outperform representation-distance monitoring for elderly historical portraits before designing a more complex detector.

### Paper 3

Title: Why Invariant Risk Minimization Fails on Tabular Data: A Gradient Variance Solution
Link: https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.09.26350513v1
Year / Venue: 2026 / medRxiv preprint
Research function: Explanation
Research line: mechanism-aware diagnosis of robustness-method failure
Screening score: 21/25 = relevance 4 + novelty 5 + citation value 4 + feasibility 4 + long-term fit 4
Usefulness: warning and mechanism reference

#### English Summary

- **Problem:** Invariant Risk Minimization (IRM) is theoretically intended to learn stable predictors across environments, yet it can underperform ordinary Empirical Risk Minimization (ERM), especially on tabular deployment problems. The paper asks why this happens and when invariant learning is appropriate.
- **Method:** The authors propose CausTab, which penalizes the variance of the full parameter-gradient vector across environments rather than IRM's scalar output-layer penalty. They also introduce a Spurious Dominance Index (SDI) to diagnose whether environment variation is driven by unstable feature-outcome associations or by outcome-prevalence changes.
- **Evaluation:** Experiments use three controlled spurious-correlation regimes, four NHANES survey cycles, and leave-one-hospital-out evaluation across four UCI Heart Disease hospital systems. ERM, IRM, and CausTab are compared using AUC, expected calibration error, penalty dynamics, ablations, and cross-environment generalization.
- **Findings:** IRM loses up to 13.8 AUC points relative to ERM in spurious-dominant settings because its scalar penalty collapses toward zero without restructuring the learned representation. CausTab matches or exceeds ERM across the reported conditions and improves calibration on NHANES. However, invariant methods fail when environments differ mainly in outcome prevalence, because the shared causal-mechanism assumption is violated.
- **Technical intuition:** A complex robustness objective can appear satisfied for the wrong reason. Monitoring the full gradient disagreement across environments makes superficial output-layer compliance harder, but no invariance penalty can rescue a setting where the assumed stable mechanism is false.

#### 中文解释

- **解决的问题:** 这篇论文解释了为什么一个理论上为 distribution shift 设计的方法，实际可能比普通 ERM 更差。
- **为什么重要:** 它挑战了“方法越复杂、robustness objective 越强，就越可靠”的直觉。复杂 regularizer 可能发生 penalty collapse，只是在数值上满足约束，并没有真正学到稳定 representation。
- **技术直觉:** 如果某个参数依赖 spurious feature，它在不同 environment 中收到的 gradient direction 会不一致。CausTab 惩罚这种 full-gradient variance，比 IRM 的单一 scalar penalty 更难被表面性满足。
- **核心发现:** IRM 在强 spurious-correlation setting 中最多比 ERM 低 13.8 AUC points；CausTab 的 calibration 更好。但如果 shift 主要来自 outcome prevalence 改变，所有 invariant method 的核心假设都会失效。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- **Aggregate metric masking:** 单看平均 AUC 或 MAE 会掩盖训练机制是否已经失效。Penalty dynamics、calibration 和 environment-wise results 提供了比最终 aggregate score 更有解释力的诊断。
- **Elderly-group degradation:** 我可以检查 elderly portraits 是否对应 spurious-dominant regime：模型是否依赖只在年轻样本或某一历史时期成立的视觉线索，以及这些线索的 gradient/representation stability 是否更差。
- **Balancing trade-offs:** Balancing 会改变 environment 和 subgroup 的相关结构。如果它只改变 prevalence，而没有修复 feature-outcome mechanism，invariant or balanced training 可能不会改善 elderly reliability，甚至会破坏原本稳定的 signal。
- **Cascade fragility:** 论文中的 penalty collapse 为我的 cascade fragility 提供了机制类比：复杂条件结构可能在训练分布上满足局部目标，却没有保证每个 route 在 shift 后仍保持有效。
- **Historical domain shift:** NHANES survey cycles 和 cross-hospital evaluation提供了 temporal/institutional shift 的实验模板。我可以把 historical periods 或 archive sources 作为 environments，并验证“稳定机制”假设是否真的成立。

#### Limitation

This is a single-author preprint in tabular clinical data, not a subgroup-focused visual study. Its causal-feature assumptions and reported superiority need independent replication before CausTab is treated as a proposal method rather than a cautionary reference.

#### Follow-up

I should use the paper mainly as a warning: diagnose the type of shift and test a simple ERM/regression baseline before introducing an invariant objective.

## 2. Emerging Trend

### English

Today's papers point toward **mechanism-matched reliability evaluation**. Hidden degradation is not solved by adding one generic fairness constraint, uncertainty score, or invariance objective. The evaluation must identify the mechanism of change: missingness heterogeneity requires subgroup calibration; visual silent failures require classifier-aware confidence evaluation; spurious-correlation shift may benefit from invariant regularization, while prevalence shift can invalidate its assumptions. This moves my research direction from asking whether a model is robust on average to asking whether the monitoring or intervention mechanism matches the specific subgroup shift that produced the failure.

### 中文

今天的趋势可以概括为 **mechanism-matched reliability evaluation**。隐藏退化不能靠一个通用 fairness constraint、uncertainty score 或 invariance objective 解决。首先要判断 shift 的机制：missingness heterogeneity 需要 subgroup calibration；visual silent failure 需要同时评估 classifier 和 confidence signal；spurious-correlation shift 可能适合 invariant regularization，但 prevalence shift 会直接破坏它的假设。这让我更明确：研究重点不只是“模型平均是否 robust”，而是“diagnostic、monitoring 和 intervention 是否匹配造成 subgroup failure 的具体 shift mechanism”。

## 3. Two Research Questions

### Question 1

#### English

- **Research question:** Under historical portrait shift, can age- and image-quality-aware conformal calibration maintain elderly-group residual coverage while pooled calibration still meets its marginal target?
- **Why it matters:** This directly tests whether a seemingly valid global uncertainty guarantee hides the same elderly-group failure that global MAE hid in my thesis.
- **Possible data:** My historical portrait dataset, stratified by age group, archive source, period, scan quality, and missing metadata; a modern face dataset can serve as a controlled source domain.
- **Possible method:** Fit a simple age regressor, construct conformal residual intervals, compare pooled calibration with age-group Mondrian calibration and a label-free quality/missingness-aware grouping rule selected from source-target shift statistics.
- **Evaluation metric:** Marginal coverage, elderly-group coverage gap, worst-group coverage, interval width, elderly underestimation rate, and coverage-width trade-off across periods.
- **Thesis connection:** The experiment converts aggregate MAE masking into a formal uncertainty-coverage question and tests whether stable observed grouping is safer than hard prediction-based routing.
- **Risk or limitation:** Small elderly calibration sets may widen intervals or make subgroup estimates unstable; repeated splits and finite-sample confidence intervals are required.

#### 中文

- **研究问题:** 在 historical portrait shift 下，当 pooled conformal calibration 仍达到 marginal coverage 时，age- and image-quality-aware calibration 能否保持 elderly-group residual coverage？
- **重要性:** 这个问题直接检验 global uncertainty guarantee 是否会像整体 MAE 一样掩盖 elderly-group failure。

## 4. Personal Reflection

### English

Today's papers sharpen my research identity from general subgroup robustness to **diagnosing whether a reliability method matches the failure mechanism**. My thesis already showed that reasonable global MAE, balancing, and a more elaborate cascade could all give misleading signals. The new connection is that monitoring methods can fail in the same way: marginal conformal coverage can hide subgroup undercoverage, latent distance can be a weak proxy for actual visual failure, and a theoretically robust objective can collapse or be applied outside its assumptions. My strongest direction is therefore not to propose another universally robust model. It is to build an evaluation framework that first characterizes the shift, then measures hidden elderly-group degradation, and only then selects calibration, monitoring, or intervention tools whose assumptions are empirically testable.
