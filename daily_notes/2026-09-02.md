## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: A Statistical Framework for Data-Driven Discovery of Differential Performance in Clinical Risk Prediction Models
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01333
Year / Venue: 2026 / arXiv
Source: arXiv primary page and PDF
Research line: differential model performance, subgroup discovery, clinical risk prediction, unfairness trees
Research function: Diagnosis, Explanation, Monitoring, and Evaluation
Screening Score: 25/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 5; proposal citation value 5; feasibility 5; strategic long-term fit 5.
Usefulness: proposal anchor and Recommended for paper card

#### English Summary

- Problem: clinical risk models can perform unevenly across subpopulations, but predefining every subgroup is unrealistic because relevant failure groups may arise from interactions among patient characteristics.
- Method: the paper introduces a recursive partitioning framework, including `utree`, to discover subgroups with statistically significant unfairness while controlling type I error. It uses uncertainty quantification so discovered differential-performance claims are less likely to be unstable artifacts.
- Evaluation: the method is evaluated through simulation studies and applied to 30-day mortality prediction after myocardial infarction using the GUSTO-I randomized trial. The application compares several risk-prediction models and identifies clinically interpretable subgroups.
- Findings: the framework can detect higher-order subgroups whose performance differs from the overall population, including risk regions involving age, sex, blood pressure, heart rate, and Killip class. The result is not just a lower average score, but a map of where the model is unreliable.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: subgroup failure 不一定沿着我预先定义的 age 或 gender 轴出现。真实的 differential performance 可能是 age、sex、血压、心率、疾病严重程度等变量交互之后才显现。如果只看固定切片, 很容易漏掉隐藏失败。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 发现 elderly-group degradation, 但这可能只是一个可见入口。真正的 failure unit 可能是 `elderly x gender x archive x image quality x historical period`。这篇论文给了我一个更系统的方法: 不先假设 subgroup, 而是用统计约束的数据驱动树结构去发现 differential performance。

技术直觉: 如果模型错误不是均匀分布的, 可以把样本空间递归切分, 寻找误差显著偏离总体的区域。但这个过程容易 data snooping, 所以论文强调 type I error control 和 uncertainty quantification。这一点很适合我的研究方向, 因为 hidden subgroup discovery 必须避免把小样本噪声误写成机制性 failure。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 这篇论文直接解释为什么 global performance 会掩盖局部失败。它把隐藏在总体指标下的 differential performance 变成可以被统计检验的 subgroup tree。
- Elderly-group degradation: 我的 elderly failure 可以被重新检查为树结构中的一个节点, 或者一个更细的交互节点。这样我可以判断 elderly 本身是否是 failure unit, 还是 elderly 与 source、gender、period、image quality 的交互才是真正机制。
- Balancing trade-offs: 如果 balancing 只按单轴 age/gender 调整, 但真实 failure 是高阶交互, intervention 可能把样本权重推到错误区域。这能解释我的 balancing 有时改善总体却伤害 elderly group。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded gender-conditional model 把 gender 作为硬路由变量, 但 `utree` 的逻辑提示我: route variable 应该由 differential performance evidence 支撑。如果真实脆弱区域不是 gender 主导, cascade 会把复杂性放错位置。
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits 可以把 archive、period、restoration style、scan quality 和 age group 作为候选切分变量。这个方法可以把 historical domain shift 下的 hidden failure 从经验观察推进到可检验的 subgroup discovery。

#### Limitation

The paper is built around clinical risk prediction and mostly classification-style performance measures. For my proposal, I would need to adapt the framework to age-regression residuals, MAE gaps, underestimation rate, prediction-interval coverage, and small historical subgroup cells.

#### Follow-up

Deep-read this paper for a paper card. The most useful extraction is an experiment design: run differential-performance trees over residuals from the thesis models, then test whether discovered nodes explain why elderly-group MAE, balancing effects, and cascaded-model errors were unstable.

### Paper 2

Title: Fairness-Aware Mixture-of-Experts via Subgroup Reweighting and Gate Regularization
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22820
Year / Venue: 2026 / AVSS 2026
Source: arXiv primary page
Research line: mixture-of-experts fairness, subgroup reweighting, gate imbalance, routing-induced bias
Research function: Explanation, Intervention, and Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 5; proposal citation value 4; feasibility 5; strategic long-term fit 5.
Usefulness: strong cascade-fragility analogue for my thesis

#### English Summary

- Problem: mixture-of-experts models can improve specialization, but the gating network may route underrepresented subgroups into narrow or poorly balanced expert paths, creating subgroup-dependent error that aggregate accuracy does not reveal.
- Method: the paper proposes a fairness-aware MoE objective with subgroup reweighting and gate regularization. Subgroup reweighting increases optimization pressure on disadvantaged groups, while gate regularization discourages routing collapse and encourages more balanced expert use.
- Evaluation: the method is evaluated on CelebA with multi-attribute face classification tasks. The paper reports group fairness metrics including equalized odds and studies routing behavior in a four-expert ResNet-18 MoE setting.
- Findings: the method reduces equalized-odds disparity while preserving competitive accuracy. The key diagnostic contribution is that subgroup disparity can be linked to expert-routing imbalance, not only to the final classifier loss.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: MoE 或 conditional routing 模型看起来更灵活, 但 routing 本身可能产生新的 bias。如果 gate 把某些 subgroup 集中分配到少数 expert, 或者让 expert specialization 与 sensitive subgroup 强耦合, 整体 accuracy 可能不错, 但 subgroup reliability 会变差。

为什么重要: 这和我的 cascaded gender-conditional age model 非常接近。我的 cascade 不是 MoE, 但同样有条件路径: 先预测 gender, 再进入不同 age regression head。FAMoE 给我一个现代化语言来解释这种 failure: hidden degradation 可能来自 routing distribution, gate confidence, expert specialization, and subgroup-path mismatch。

技术直觉: 复杂模型的风险不只在 representation, 也在样本被送到哪条路径。Subgroup reweighting 试图让弱势 subgroup 对 loss 更有影响, gate regularization 则让 routing 不要过度集中。对我的研究来说, 这意味着我不能只比较 cascade 的 final MAE, 还应检查每个 route 里 residual distribution 是否稳定。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: FAMoE 显示整体 accuracy 可以掩盖 route-specific and subgroup-specific disparity。我的 thesis 中 global MAE 同样无法说明某条 conditional path 是否对 elderly group 特别脆弱。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits 可能在某个 gender route 或 quality route 内被系统性低估。FAMoE 的 routing analysis 提醒我把 elderly residuals 按 route assignment 和 route confidence 分解。
- Balancing trade-offs: subgroup reweighting 可能降低 disparity, 但也可能和 gate regularization、sample support、expert specialization 产生 trade-off。这个视角能解释为什么我的 balancing 不一定稳定改善 elderly performance。
- Cascade fragility: 这是三篇中和 cascade fragility 最直接相关的一篇。它把 conditional model 的失败从 "模型更复杂所以过拟合" 细化为 "subgroup evidence is routed through unstable expert paths"。
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits 可能改变 gate 输入特征, 使 route assignment 在 source/period shift 下漂移。如果我重新分析 thesis cascade, 可以检查 historical period 是否改变 gender-route confidence and elderly residuals。

#### Limitation

The paper focuses on classification fairness in CelebA, not continuous age regression. It also assumes known subgroup labels for reweighting. My use should focus on routing diagnostics and route-aware residual evaluation rather than copying the exact MoE architecture.

#### Follow-up

Use this paper to design a cascade audit: compare route assignment, route confidence, route-specific MAE, elderly underestimation rate, and worst route-subgroup error before and after balancing.

### Paper 3

Title: FairGlucose: A CGM Fairness Benchmark Reveals Subgroup Disparities Hidden in Population-Level Validation
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18296
Year / Venue: 2026 / arXiv
Source: arXiv primary page and PDF
Research line: continuous glucose monitoring, temporal forecasting, subgroup fairness benchmark, deployment-relevant evaluation
Research function: Diagnosis, Monitoring, and Evaluation
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 4; proposal citation value 5; feasibility 4; strategic long-term fit 5.
Usefulness: temporal reliability benchmark for subgroup-aware monitoring

#### English Summary

- Problem: continuous glucose forecasting models can look stable at the population level while producing uneven errors across age, gender, and diabetes-type strata, especially in clinically hard cases.
- Method: the paper introduces FairGlucose, a benchmark based on continuous glucose monitoring data with balanced demographic strata and standardized subgroup evaluation across model families.
- Evaluation: the benchmark evaluates 33 models on two-hour glucose forecasting across age, gender, and diabetes-type subgroups. It reports population-level performance, subgroup ratios, hard-case behavior, and input-length sensitivity.
- Findings: population-level out-of-distribution metrics remain close to stable, but subgroup ratios vary substantially. Type 1 diabetes cases show higher error, hard cases concentrate in specific strata, and input history length can change subgroup performance differently. This makes population validation insufficient for deployment reliability.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: 时间序列预测模型的 overall OOD performance 看起来稳定, 不代表每个 subgroup 都可靠。CGM 任务里不同 age、gender、diabetes type 的误差结构可能不同, clinically hard cases 也可能集中在某些 subgroup。

为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 中 global MAE 掩盖 elderly-group degradation 很相似, 只是领域从 historical faces 变成 temporal health forecasting。它特别强调 temporal input length 和 hard-case concentration, 这能帮助我把 subgroup reliability 和时间动态连接起来。

技术直觉: 一个模型在整体测试集上的误差可能几乎不变, 但 subgroup-level ratio 可以明显偏离。如果 subgroup 的动态模式更复杂, 或者历史窗口长度不适配某类样本, model performance 会在局部退化。对我的研究来说, historical period 或 image quality 也可能扮演类似的 temporal/contextual difficulty 角色。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: FairGlucose 的核心发现就是 population-level validation hides subgroup disparities。这直接对应我的 global MAE 掩盖 elderly-group error。
- Elderly-group degradation: 论文用 age strata 检查 forecasting reliability, 这给我一个跨领域证据: 年龄相关 subgroup 不是附加分析, 而是决策相关 reliability unit。
- Balancing trade-offs: benchmark 中 balanced demographic strata 仍然暴露 subgroup disparity, 说明 balanced evaluation composition 不等于 model reliability 已经平衡。这和我的 balancing 反而伤害 elderly group 的观察一致。
- Cascade fragility: FairGlucose 不是 cascade 模型, 但它显示 input-history design and model family 会对 subgroup 产生不同影响。我的 cascaded model 也可能因为路径设计改变了不同 subgroup 可用的信息历史或证据结构。
- Historical domain shift: CGM 的 temporal dynamics 可以类比 historical portraits 的 period dynamics。不同时间段、来源和视觉保存状态可能让 aggregate validation 稳定, 但 elderly or low-quality historical strata 局部失败。

#### Limitation

The paper is a benchmark rather than a new diagnostic algorithm. Its value for my research is strongest as an evaluation precedent for temporal subgroup monitoring and hard-case concentration analysis.

#### Follow-up

Borrow the benchmark logic for historical portraits: report aggregate MAE alongside age/gender/source/period subgroup ratios, then isolate hard residual cases and test whether period length, source composition, or image-quality proxies change subgroup error.

## 3. Two Research Questions

Each question is specific, testable, technically plausible, and naturally extends my thesis.

### Question 1

English:

- Research question: Can differential-performance trees over age-regression residuals identify hidden historical-portrait subgroups that explain elderly MAE degradation better than age-only slicing?
- Why it matters: My thesis found elderly degradation, but `utree` suggests that the real failure unit may be a higher-order subgroup involving age, gender, archive, period, and image quality.
- Possible data: my historical portrait dataset with predictions and residuals from regression, classification, multi-task, and cascaded models, plus age group, gender, source archive, period, and visual-quality proxies.
- Possible method: adapt unfairness-tree style recursive partitioning to continuous residuals, using minimum-support rules, bootstrap confidence intervals, and held-out validation for discovered nodes.
- Evaluation metric: node-specific MAE, residual skew, elderly underestimation rate, worst-node gap, discovered-node stability, and whether nodes explain more variance than age-only groups.
- Thesis connection: this directly extends aggregate metric masking and elderly-group degradation into a hidden-subgroup discovery protocol.
- Risk or limitation: historical subgroup cells may be small, so the method needs conservative splitting, uncertainty reporting, and validation on period-held-out splits.

中文:

- 研究问题: 对 age-regression residuals 做 differential-performance tree, 能否发现比 age-only slicing 更能解释 elderly MAE degradation 的 hidden historical-portrait subgroups?
- 重要性: 我的 thesis 发现 elderly degradation, 但 `utree` 提醒我真实 failure unit 可能是 age、gender、archive、period、image quality 的高阶交互。
- 可能数据: 我的 historical portrait 数据, 包含 regression、classification、multi-task 和 cascaded models 的 predictions/residuals, 以及 age group、gender、source archive、period 和 visual-quality proxies。
- 可能方法: 把 unfairness-tree recursive partitioning 改造成 continuous residual setting, 加入 minimum-support rules、bootstrap confidence intervals 和 held-out validation。
- 评估指标: node-specific MAE、residual skew、elderly underestimation rate、worst-node gap、discovered-node stability, 以及这些 nodes 是否比 age-only groups 解释更多误差方差。
- Thesis 连接: 直接把 aggregate metric masking 和 elderly-group degradation 扩展成 hidden-subgroup discovery protocol。
- 风险: historical subgroup cell 可能很小, 所以需要保守切分、不确定性报告和 period-held-out validation。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: Does route assignment in cascaded age-gender models amplify elderly residual error under historical domain shift, and can route-aware reweighting or gate regularization reduce this failure without increasing worst-group MAE?
- Why it matters: My thesis found the cascaded gender-conditional model fragile, and FAMoE suggests that conditional paths should be audited as a source of subgroup disparity.
- Possible data: the thesis models' route assignments, gender predictions, age residuals, embeddings, and metadata for age group, gender, source archive, period, and image quality.
- Possible method: compare simple regression, multi-task, and cascaded models using route-specific residual diagnostics; then simulate route-aware reweighting, confidence filtering, or soft routing under source/period splits.
- Evaluation metric: global MAE, elderly MAE, worst route-subgroup MAE, route entropy, route confidence, underestimation rate, subgroup calibration error, and balancing-induced gap change.
- Thesis connection: this directly links cascade fragility, balancing trade-offs, and historical domain shift to a testable architecture audit.
- Risk or limitation: if original cascade logs do not preserve route confidence, some diagnostics may require rerunning inference or rebuilding comparable soft-routing variants.

中文:

- 研究问题: cascaded age-gender models 的 route assignment 是否会在 historical domain shift 下放大 elderly residual error, route-aware reweighting 或 gate regularization 能否降低这种 failure 且不增加 worst-group MAE?
- 重要性: 我的 thesis 发现 gender-conditional cascade 比较脆弱, FAMoE 提醒我 conditional paths 本身应该被当成 subgroup disparity source 来审计。
- 可能数据: thesis models 的 route assignments、gender predictions、age residuals、embeddings, 以及 age group、gender、source archive、period 和 image quality metadata。
