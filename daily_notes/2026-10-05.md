## Paper 1 — ReCIRC: Rectified Conformal Risk Control

Bruno Marcondes e Resende, Helton Graziadei, Thiago Rodrigo Ramos, Rafael Izbicki. **2026-09-29**, arXiv preprint. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38112) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38112v1).

**Research function:** Intervention → Evaluation.

### English Summary

**Verified from paper**

- **Problem:** A shared risk-control threshold can protect easy cases excessively while leaving difficult cases exposed.
- **Method:** Estimate and invert local risk curves, then calibrate the resulting prediction family with ordinary conformal risk control.
- **Evaluation:** Three synthetic and five real-data settings, including regression and segmentation.
- **Findings:** Reported worst-group risk improves across settings. Finite-sample validity remains marginal, requires exchangeability and the specified bounded monotone losses, and is not an arbitrary-shift guarantee. Conditional conclusions require accurate risk estimation and further conditions. [Methods and theory](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38112v1).

### 中文解释

相同阈值不等于相同风险。论文先估计每个输入的风险曲线，再统一校准风险预算；这能帮助识别平均风险背后的困难样本。有限样本保证仍是总体层面的，并依赖可交换性，不能直接宣称历史域或老年组受到保证。[原文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38112v1)

### Connection to My Thesis

**Explicit Conceptual Bridge — my interpretation:** My thesis's low global MAE and high elderly error motivate checking who receives protection from an uncertainty procedure.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection |
|---|---|
| Aggregate metric masking | I would report interval failure by age and archive alongside marginal failure. |
| Elderly-group degradation | I would test whether local risk estimates remain accurate for sparse elderly portraits. |
| Balancing trade-offs | I would fit and calibrate the procedure separately after each training intervention, using equal label budgets. |
| Cascade fragility | I would evaluate intervals around final cascade predictions; router confidence alone cannot establish age reliability. |
| Historical domain shift | I would separate target-calibrated evaluation from deliberately shifted source-only calibration. |

**Methodological Relevance:** I can start with frozen age predictions and nested intervals, avoiding a new backbone. Separate data must fit risk curves, calibrate thresholds, and evaluate outcomes.

**Reinterpretation of My Thesis Findings:** A stable regression baseline may need less interval expansion to protect elderly cases; lower MAE alone does not establish that claim.

**中文连接：** 我会检查整体覆盖是否掩盖老年漏覆盖、平衡后风险估计是否失真、级联最终输出是否可靠，以及跨档案校准是否失效。我的扩展是年龄回归实验，不是把已有定理直接套到历史漂移上。

**Needs full-paper verification:** Regression implementation details, attainable budgets, and risk-estimation error bounds for small groups.

## Paper 2 — When Masking Helps or Hurts Robustness in Compressed CLIP: A Pre-Deployment Diagnostic

Muhammad Zawish, Steven Davy. **2026-09-30**, arXiv preprint. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39704) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39704v1).

**Research function:** Diagnosis → Explanation → Evaluation.

### English Summary

**Verified from paper**

- **Problem:** Masking can improve or destroy worst-group performance.
- **Method:** SIM compares background and foreground text similarities to guide masking.
- **Evaluation:** Eight datasets and six CLIP variants; validation is retrospective.
- **Findings:** Directional success is 7/8 with a zero-change tie counted as correct; excluding it gives 6/7 and p=0.063. MetaShifts is a persistent failure. Main sampling uses existing groups; an unstratified check is also reported. Foreground identification uses dataset-specific structural conventions. This is limited diagnostic evidence, not a general safety certificate. [Sections 3, 5–6](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39704v1).

### 中文解释

技术直觉是背景可能比主体更符合文本相似度，导致保留或删除错误信息。诊断帮助判断 masking 的方向，但八个数据集的结果对平局处理敏感，且前景划分含结构先验；“无需标签”不能理解为完全无需数据知识。[原文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39704v1)

### Connection to My Thesis

**Explicit Conceptual Bridge — my interpretation:** My balancing results suggest that a plausible intervention needs a diagnostic of when it might damage vulnerable examples.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection |
|---|---|
| Aggregate metric masking | I would compare paired subgroup changes, not only the average effect of preprocessing. |
| Elderly-group degradation | I would test whether background removal accidentally deletes age-relevant hair or facial boundaries. |
| Balancing trade-offs | I would test balancing and masking as separate factors; this paper does not explain resampling backfire directly. |
| Cascade fragility | I would compare interventions before routing versus before the age head, with controlled routing when labels permit. |
| Historical domain shift | I would assess whether archive-specific backgrounds predict intervention effects on an unseen archive. |

**Methodological Relevance:** I can audit frozen model predictions under a few controlled image transformations. SIM itself is CLIP-specific and cannot be assumed valid for my ConvNeXt-style regression models.

**Reinterpretation of My Thesis Findings:** Complexity may introduce an additional assumption about which evidence is useful; failure of that assumption is a testable alternative to a generic “complex models overfit” explanation.

**中文连接：** 我的实验应追踪整体掩盖、老年信息损失、平衡与预处理的交互、级联入口差异及跨档案稳定性。我需要独立验证诊断，不能用同一批目标标签挑选规则再报告成功。

**Needs full-paper verification:** Reproduce segmentation conventions and prospective diagnostic performance; distinguish masking benefit over the uncompressed model from policy benefit over a fixed fallback.

## Two Research Questions / 两个研究问题

### RQ1 — Can local risk calibration protect elderly intervals across archives?

- **Research question:** At a fixed 10% marginal miscoverage target, does local risk-curve calibration reduce elderly interval miscoverage relative to global calibration, and how much of the gain survives transfer to an unseen archive?
- **Why it matters:** I need to distinguish protecting difficult cases from simply widening every interval.
- **Possible data:** My labeled portraits, with person-disjoint splits and archive/period identifiers where available; no invented temporal metadata.
- **Possible method:** Freeze regression and cascade models. Use separate risk-fit, calibration, and evaluation partitions. Compare global and local calibration at equal label budgets, first within a target archive, then with source-only calibration under shift. Audit natural versus balanced training separately.
- **Evaluation metric:** Elderly and worst-group miscoverage, interval width, upper-endpoint failures, MAE, and person-bootstrap uncertainty. A successful result reduces elderly failures without merely inflating width everywhere.
- **Thesis connection:** I operationalize hidden elderly degradation while comparing simple regression, balancing, and conditional routing.
- **Risk or limitation:** Small elderly counts may make local estimation worse. Shifted tests are empirical stress tests, not exchangeability-based guarantees. True age is available for retrospective auditing, not for selecting a deployment interval.

**中文：** 在总体漏覆盖目标 10% 下，局部风险校准能否比统一校准减少老年组漏覆盖，并迁移到新档案？我会冻结模型，独立划分风险拟合、校准和测试数据，以相同标签预算比较覆盖与宽度，再检验平衡和级联是否改变结果。老年样本不足、标签误差及域漂移是主要限制；真实年龄只用于事后分组审计。

### RQ2 — Can a source-defined preprocessing diagnostic predict elderly harm?

- **Research question:** Can background sensitivity measured without target age labels predict whether a fixed face-cropping intervention raises elderly MAE in a held-out archive?
- **Why it matters:** I want to test intervention selection prospectively rather than explain damage after observing it.
- **Possible data:** My portraits with retained backgrounds and verified ages; at least several independent archive/period partitions are needed for a cross-domain claim.
- **Possible method:** On development archives, measure prediction changes between original, face-cropped, and background-perturbed images. Prespecify one simple diagnostic and threshold using development outcomes, then lock it before revealing held-out labels. Compare a gated intervention with always-crop and never-crop; repeat for regression/cascade and natural/balanced training.
- **Evaluation metric:** Directional prediction accuracy across archives, paired elderly MAE change, severe underestimation rate using a development-fixed threshold, overall MAE, and error from selecting the worse intervention. Count ties separately.
- **Thesis connection:** I test whether background dependence explains elderly harm and interacts with balancing or routing under historical shift.
- **Risk or limitation:** Prediction sensitivity need not imply error sensitivity; cropping can remove age evidence and introduce its own shift. With too few archives, this remains an exploratory pilot, not a validated gate.

**中文：** 我能否在不看目标年龄标签时，用背景扰动引起的预测变化，预测裁剪是否伤害新档案的老年组？规则只在开发档案拟定，目标标签最后揭示；比较始终裁剪、从不裁剪与规则选择，并单独统计平局。指标包括老年 MAE、严重低估和选错干预的代价。预测变化不等于误差变化，档案太少时只能作为探索实验。
