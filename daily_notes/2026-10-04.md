## Paper 1 — WATCH: Adaptive Monitoring for AI Deployments via Weighted-Conformal Martingales

Drew Prinster, Xing Han, Anqi Liu, Suchi Saria. First submitted **2025-05-07**; full-text version inspected: **v3, 2025-06-01**. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04608) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.04608v3).

**Research function:** Monitoring → Diagnosis.

### English Summary

**Verified from paper**

- **Problem:** Conventional shift alarms can react to changes that successful adaptation could accommodate.
- **Method:** Weighted conformal test martingales accumulate evidence against a specified adaptation-compatible null.
- **Evaluation:** Tabular regression and image classification, including CIFAR-10-C, compare benign, extreme covariate, and concept shifts.
- **Findings:** Experiments show fewer unnecessary alarms after benign-shift adaptation and rapid detection in harmful-shift scenarios. The practical null includes invariant label conditionals and accurate density-ratio weights; rejection does not uniquely identify concept shift. [Methods §§3.3–3.6 and experiments §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.04608v3).

### 中文解释

论文解决“变化是否值得告警”：用加权 conformal 检验允许可适应的变化，减少无必要告警。技术直觉是检验适应后的假设是否仍成立。权重估计错误也可能触发告警，因此不能直接把告警解释为概念漂移。[原文](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.04608v3)

### Connection to My Thesis

**Explicit Conceptual Bridge — my interpretation:** I can reinterpret historical shift as a question about whether a correction remains adequate for elderly portraits, rather than treating every embedding change as harmful.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection |
|---|---|
| Aggregate metric masking | I would compare global and prespecified age-group alarms; successful marginal correction might hide a failing elderly subset. |
| Elderly-group degradation | I would inspect both elderly residuals and support overlap before calling demographic change benign. |
| Balancing trade-offs | I would distinguish changing training counts from estimating deployment density ratios; they solve different problems. |
| Cascade fragility | I would log routing changes and head residuals separately; a global adaptation alarm would not establish the cascade's failure mechanism. |
| Historical domain shift | I would hold out archives and vary image quality separately from age composition to test which shifts remain correctable. |

**Methodological Relevance:** I can begin with stored predictions and embeddings from my existing models. Residual-based monitoring requires labels; an input-only monitor answers a different question.

**Reinterpretation of My Thesis Findings:** My balancing backfire may indicate a mismatched correction, but this remains a hypothesis rather than a paper-established explanation.

**中文连接：** 我会同时检查整体与老年子群、老年样本的支持重叠、训练平衡与目标域加权的区别、路由及分支残差，以及跨档案变化。对我的 thesis 而言，“能修正分布”需要实际子群误差来验证。

**Needs full-paper verification:** Exact weighting implementation, sequential validity under estimated weights, label timing, and error-budget allocation before adding subgroup monitors.

## Paper 2 — On Continuous Monitoring of Risk Violations under Unknown Shift

Alexander Timans, Rajeev Verma, Eric Nalisnick, Christian A. Naesseth. First submitted **2025-06-19**. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.16416) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.16416).

**Research function:** Monitoring → Evaluation.

### English Summary

**Verified from paper**

- **Problem:** A risk threshold validated before deployment can become invalid under changing distributions.
- **Method:** Sequential betting tests accumulate bounded observed losses against a risk budget, with false-alarm control under the stated null.
- **Evaluation:** Outlier detection and prediction sets, including temporal FMoW shift.
- **Findings:** Experiments demonstrate detection of risk violations; theory provides consistency and detection-time results under additional conditions. Labels are revealed after prediction. An alarm rejects continued risk validity; silence does not certify safety. [Problem setting §2, theory §4, experiments §6](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.16416).

### 中文解释

论文把“误差是否超过容忍水平”变成持续检验，而不只是检测输入变化。有界损失是关键，且预测后需要真实标签。没有告警可能只是证据不足；不能当作持续安全证明。[原文](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.16416)

### Connection to My Thesis

**Explicit Conceptual Bridge — my interpretation:** My thesis's elderly underestimation can become an explicit risk event, such as underestimation exceeding a prespecified number of years, rather than remaining an average-error observation.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection |
|---|---|
| Aggregate metric masking | I would monitor a prespecified elderly error event in addition to the population event, with a shared false-alarm budget. |
| Elderly-group degradation | I would define an unacceptable underestimation probability before examining target outcomes. |
| Balancing trade-offs | I would replay balanced and natural-training models on identical target people and compare risk alarms and missed violations. |
| Cascade fragility | I would compare end-to-end cascade risk with regression risk; any routing attribution needs an additional controlled analysis. |
| Historical domain shift | I would replay archive or period transitions with an explicit label-reveal schedule instead of pretending retrospective labels arrive instantly. |

**Methodological Relevance:** Bounded binary error events fit my diagnostic background. I would keep uncapped MAE as a separate descriptive metric; clipping changes the estimand.

**Reinterpretation of My Thesis Findings:** The practical advantage of simple regression may be fewer unacceptable elderly errors across domains, even when aggregate MAE differences are small.

**中文连接：** 我会把整体掩盖、老年严重低估、平衡代价、级联风险和历史域变化放入同一配对回放实验。我的主要扩展是明确风险事件、标签延迟和多个子群的误报预算，而不是直接宣称原论文已经提供老年组保证。

**Needs full-paper verification:** Filtration and independence conditions, simultaneous validity across groups/thresholds, and whether proposed windowing or restart rules preserve the desired guarantee.

## Paper 3 — Monitoring Risks in Test-Time Adaptation

Mona Schirmer, Metod Jazbec, Christian A. Naesseth, Eric Nalisnick. First submitted **2025-07-11**. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08721) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.08721).

**Research function:** Monitoring → Evaluation.

### English Summary

**Verified from paper**

- **Problem:** An adapting model can deteriorate without deployment labels.
- **Method:** Confidence-sequence risk monitoring uses loss proxies and an explicit proxy-validity assumption for changing models.
- **Evaluation:** ImageNet-C, Yearbook portrait gender classification, and FMoW-Time; includes adaptation collapse.
- **Findings:** The monitor detects deterioration in tested settings, but proxy-assumption violations occur, including during warm-up. Source calibration uses labels. Yearbook is not elderly age regression. [Methods §3 and experiments §5](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.08721).

### 中文解释

论文研究模型一边适应、一边变差时如何无目标标签告警。它用不确定性等代理构造风险下界，前提是代理满足有效性条件。历史年鉴人像实验与我的数据形态接近，但不等于验证了老年年龄估计。[原文](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.08721)

### Connection to My Thesis

**Explicit Conceptual Bridge — my interpretation:** I should test whether uncertainty remains informative within the elderly group before using it as a warning signal for my age-estimation models.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection |
|---|---|
| Aggregate metric masking | I would audit proxy-versus-error agreement within groups; global agreement could hide confidently wrong elderly predictions. |
| Elderly-group degradation | I would measure high-error cases missed by the proxy, especially systematic underestimation. |
| Balancing trade-offs | I would recalibrate and separately validate proxies after balancing, because intervention may change uncertainty-error relationships. |
| Cascade fragility | I would compare router confidence, head uncertainty, and end-to-end error; confidence in routing need not imply reliable age estimates. |

**Methodological Relevance:** My first step is an offline proxy-validity audit with labels hidden from the monitor and retained for evaluation; continuous neural adaptation is a later extension.

**Reinterpretation of My Thesis Findings:** My simpler regression model may yield both lower risk and a more stable monitoring signal, but these are separate empirical claims.

**中文连接：** 我会检验整体代理准确是否掩盖老年漏报、平衡是否改变代理有效性、级联路由置信度是否误导监控，以及这些关系能否跨档案成立。我不会把已有分类实验直接当成年龄回归结论。

## Two Research Questions / 两个研究问题

### RQ1 — Can a subgroup risk test detect masked elderly underestimation?

- **Research question:** In archive-held-out facial age estimation, can a prespecified elderly risk monitor detect increases in P(age − predicted age > 10 years | age ≥ 60) earlier than a pooled monitor at the same family-wise false-alarm budget?
- **Why it matters:** I need to detect the failure hidden by aggregate MAE in my thesis.
- **Possible data:** My labeled historical portraits, conditional on access and adequate elderly counts; person-disjoint train/calibration/test splits and archive-held-out evaluation.
- **Possible method:** Freeze regression and cascade models, each trained naturally and with balancing. Compare pooled-only monitoring with pooled-plus-elderly monitoring on identical replay streams. Allocate total alpha = 0.05 across active tests; fix thresholds and hypotheses using development data. Replay immediate and fixed delayed labels. Use controlled sampling from held-out labeled pools for known-risk scenarios, plus natural archive transitions as descriptive validation.
- **Evaluation metric:** False-alarm probability across null replays; detection delay in elderly observations and total arrivals; missed violations; elderly MAE and signed residuals. Define masking prospectively, for example global MAE increase ≤0.5 years while elderly underestimation-event probability rises ≥0.10.
- **Thesis connection:** I directly compare aggregate masking, elderly degradation, balancing, and cascade versus simpler regression under historical shift.
- **Risk or limitation:** Age 60 and ten-year error are provisional design choices to justify before testing. Sparse elderly labels, dependence, and delayed observation can sharply reduce power. Finite-pool replays establish only controlled experimental validity.

**中文：** 我的问题是：在相同总体误报预算下，预先定义的老年组严重低估检验，能否比整体检验更早发现跨档案退化？我会用按人物隔离的数据，固定回归与级联模型及其平衡版本，比较相同样本流中的检测延迟、漏报和误报。老年阈值、严重误差阈值与“整体掩盖”标准都需预先确定。小样本与标签延迟是核心限制。

### RQ2 — When does an uncertainty proxy stop supporting subgroup risk monitoring?

- **Research question:** Under archive and image-quality shifts, does an ensemble-disagreement proxy miss severe elderly age errors more often after balancing or conditional routing than in a simple regression baseline?
- **Why it matters:** I need evidence before interpreting an unlabeled uncertainty signal as a reliable warning.
- **Possible data:** The same person-disjoint portrait splits, with separate natural archive shifts and controlled blur/contrast perturbations. I would not infer chronological change if reliable dates are absent.
- **Possible method:** Train small equal-size seed ensembles for the existing regression and cascade designs; calibrate proxy thresholds on source data only. Hide target labels from the proxy and retain them for audit. Compare global versus age-stratified calibration with disjoint calibration/evaluation people. Measure the proxy false-positive/false-negative balance underlying the TTA paper's assumption as an offline diagnostic before attempting its regression extension.
- **Evaluation metric:** Elderly high-error recall at fixed alert rate; proxy false-positive/false-negative probabilities; subgroup uncertainty intervals; stability across archives; bootstrap uncertainty by person. A later sequential experiment would report false alarms and delays separately from proxy classification quality.
- **Thesis connection:** I test whether balancing and cascade fragility affect not only errors but also how visible those errors are to monitoring.
- **Risk or limitation:** Ensemble disagreement is my proposed proxy, not a guaranteed regression implementation of the paper. A satisfied empirical diagnostic does not prove future validity; confident shared bias may remain invisible. Compute and small subgroup counts constrain the pilot.

**中文：** 我的问题是：跨档案和图像质量变化下，集成分歧是否在平衡或级联模型中更容易漏掉老年严重误差？我会用少量随机种子集成、源域校准与隐藏目标标签的离线评估，比较固定告警率下的老年高误差召回及代理假阳性/假阴性。首先检验代理是否值得信赖，再考虑顺序监控；代理在已有数据上表现好不能证明未来有效。
