## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Fairness Without Labels: Pseudo-Balancing for Bias Mitigation in Face Gender Classification
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10191
Year / Venue: 2025, ICCV 2025 Workshop Proceedings
Source: arXiv official abstract and experimental HTML
Research line: semi-supervised demographic debiasing for face classification
Research function: Intervention
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference with high strategic value

#### English Summary

- Problem: face gender classifiers can amplify demographic bias from training data, producing uneven performance across gender and racial subgroups.
- Method: the paper introduces pseudo-balancing, which enforces demographic balance during pseudo-label selection using unlabeled race-balanced face images, without requiring ground-truth demographic annotations.
- Evaluation: the authors fine-tune a biased classifier with FairFace unlabeled images, stress-test controlled imbalance settings, and evaluate on All-Age-Faces, whose population is predominantly East Asian.
- Findings: pseudo-balancing improves overall accuracy and reduces subgroup gaps, including a large reduction of gender accuracy disparity in the East Asian subgroup.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文处理的是 face classification 中的 demographic bias，尤其是当训练数据不平衡时，不同 race/gender subgroup 的表现会很不均匀。
- 为什么重要: 它和我的 thesis 很接近，因为都是 face-based prediction，并且都暴露出整体 accuracy 或 MAE 不能说明 subgroup 是否可靠。
- 技术直觉: pseudo-balancing 不直接依赖人工标注的 subgroup label，而是在 pseudo-label selection 阶段约束样本组成，让 fine-tuning 不再继续放大原始模型的 subgroup bias。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 这篇论文说明 overall accuracy 可以和 subgroup disparity 同时存在；这和我的 thesis 中整体 MAE 掩盖 elderly-group degradation 的现象一致。
- Elderly-group degradation: All-Age-Faces 的设定提醒我，年龄结构和族群结构会共同影响 face model reliability；我的 elderly group failure 可以被重新理解为 demographic-composition shift 下的 subgroup failure。
- Balancing trade-offs: pseudo-balancing 是一个比 naive balancing 更细的 intervention。它支持我继续追问: balancing 为什么有时改善公平性，有时却加重 elderly group error。
- Cascade fragility: 如果我的 cascaded gender-conditional model 的第一阶段 gender routing 本身带有 subgroup bias，那么后续 age head 的误差会被放大；这篇论文给了我一个检查 routing-bias 的视角。
- Historical domain shift: 从 FairFace 到 AAF 的跨数据集测试类似于现代训练集到历史人像数据的 domain shift。它让我可以把历史人像年龄估计中的失败解释为 demographic and visual shift 的耦合问题。

#### Limitation

The method is evaluated on face gender classification rather than age regression, so I would need to test whether pseudo-balancing helps or hurts continuous age estimation and residual calibration.

#### Follow-up

Re-read the full paper for pseudo-label selection details and check whether the method can be adapted to age-bin balancing without increasing elderly underestimation.

### Paper 2

Title: Enabling Granular Subgroup Level Model Evaluations by Generating Synthetic Medical Time Series
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19728
Year / Venue: 2025, arXiv
Source: arXiv official abstract
Research line: synthetic data for fine-grained subgroup evaluation in medical AI
Research function: Evaluation
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor

#### English Summary

- Problem: subgroup evaluation in medical AI is often statistically weak because real test sets contain too few samples in fine-grained intersectional groups.
- Method: the paper proposes Enhanced TimeAutoDiff, a latent diffusion framework with distribution-alignment penalties for generating synthetic ICU time-series data.
- Evaluation: the authors benchmark TimeDiff, HealthGen, TimeAutoDiff, and Enhanced TimeAutoDiff on MIMIC-III and eICU for 24-hour mortality and binary length-of-stay prediction, including 32 intersectional subgroups.
- Findings: Enhanced TimeAutoDiff reduces the gap between real-on-synthetic and real-on-real evaluation, preserves training utility, and improves subgroup-level AUROC estimation relative to small real test sets.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 很多 subgroup reliability 问题不是模型没有错，而是小 subgroup 的真实测试样本太少，导致 AUROC 或 error estimate 不稳定。
- 为什么重要: 如果 subgroup estimate 本身噪声很大，研究者可能会误以为某个 subgroup 很可靠，或者误判某个 balancing intervention 的效果。
- 技术直觉: 这篇论文把 synthetic time-series data 当成 evaluation resource，而不只是 training augmentation。更大的 synthetic subgroup cohort 可以降低 subgroup metric 的估计方差。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 它直接支持我的核心判断: population-level metric 不够，必须估计 fine-grained subgroup performance。
- Elderly-group degradation: 我的 elderly subgroup 可能样本量有限，导致 MAE、residual variance 和 calibration error 的估计都不稳定；synthetic subgroup cohort 可以作为一种评估补充。
- Balancing trade-offs: 这篇论文提醒我，balancing 的效果评估需要可靠的 subgroup metric。如果 elderly group 的测试估计不稳定，我可能会错误判断 balancing 是否真的伤害了它。
- Cascade fragility: 对 cascaded model 来说，每个 routing branch 都会进一步切分样本。synthetic subgroup evaluation 可以帮助检查小 branch 上的年龄误差是否只是噪声，还是稳定的 failure mode。
- Historical domain shift: 历史人像数据很难补充真实样本。虽然不能直接用医疗时间序列方法，但它给了我一个思路: 用受约束的 synthetic evaluation set 检查历史时期、年龄组和性别组的交互 failure。

#### Limitation

Synthetic subgroup evaluation depends on generator fidelity. If the generator misses the same rare patterns that harm the predictive model, the evaluation may become falsely reassuring.

#### Follow-up

Extract this paper's TRTS/TSTR evaluation logic and adapt it conceptually to image-domain subgroup reliability, especially elderly historical portraits.

### Paper 3

Title: Mixed-feature Logistic Regression Robust to Distribution Shifts
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12012
Year / Venue: 2025, AISTATS 2025
Source: arXiv official abstract
Research line: distributionally robust interpretable modeling for high-stakes decisions
Research function: Intervention
Screening Score: 20/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference

#### English Summary

- Problem: logistic regression is common in high-stakes domains because it is simple and interpretable, but deployment data often shifts away from the training distribution.
- Method: the paper studies distributionally robust logistic regression over a Wasserstein ambiguity set and allows shift likelihood to vary across mixed continuous and categorical features.
- Evaluation: the authors evaluate the model and graph-based optimization approach on multiple public datasets against state-of-the-art robust logistic regression methods.
- Findings: the approach improves optimization speed and reports lower average and worst-case calibration error, with gains in average and worst-case AUC.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 高风险决策场景常用 logistic regression，但真实部署时不同 feature 的 shift 风险并不一样，统一假设所有 feature 同样会 shift 太粗糙。
- 为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 中 simple regression model 更稳的发现呼应。简单模型不是低级 baseline，而可能是更容易解释、校准和监控的可靠性工具。
- 技术直觉: 这篇论文不是盲目增加模型复杂度，而是在简单模型上加入 feature-aware 的 robustness assumption，让模型在可能发生 shift 的 feature 上更保守。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 它强调 average AUC/average calibration 之外还要看 worst-case calibration 和 worst-case AUC；这与我不信任单一 global MAE 的经验一致。
- Elderly-group degradation: 如果 elderly samples 的 visual features 或 metadata features 更容易发生 shift，那么 feature-specific robustness 可以帮助我设计 subgroup-specific robustness checks。
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing 只改变样本比例，未必改变 feature-level shift risk。这篇论文让我区分 sample composition intervention 和 feature-shift robustness intervention。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded conditional models把误差传播隐藏在 routing structure 中，而 robust logistic regression 提供了一个更透明的对照: 简单模型在 shift 下可能更容易被解释和校准。
- Historical domain shift: 历史人像的 shift 可能来自图像质量、拍摄风格、年龄分布和性别分布。feature-aware robustness 的思想可以帮助我把 shift 分解为多个风险来源，而不是只说 historical data is different。

#### Limitation

The paper is not mainly about subgroup fairness, and its tabular optimization setting does not directly solve representation shift in computer vision.

#### Follow-up

Use it as a methodological baseline idea: compare complex subgroup-aware models with simpler calibrated regression-style models under controlled historical portrait shifts.

### Question 1

English:

- Research question: Can pseudo-balancing with unlabeled face data reduce elderly-group age-estimation error under historical domain shift without worsening residual calibration?
- Why it matters: my thesis found that balancing sometimes worsened elderly performance, so I need to distinguish helpful representation correction from harmful composition forcing.
- Possible data: historical portrait dataset from my thesis, FairFace or another demographically diverse face dataset for unlabeled balancing, and age/gender subgroup metadata where available.
- Possible method: compare baseline age regression, naive age/gender balancing, and pseudo-balanced fine-tuning; evaluate both global and subgroup metrics.
- Evaluation metric: global MAE, elderly-group MAE, worst-group MAE, residual skew, calibration error, and subgroup prediction variance.
- Thesis connection: directly tests aggregate metric masking, elderly-group degradation, balancing trade-offs, and historical domain shift.
- Risk or limitation: pseudo-labels may encode the same demographic bias as the original model, especially for older faces.

中文:

研究问题: 使用未标注 face data 做 pseudo-balancing，能否在 historical domain shift 下减少 elderly-group age-estimation error，同时不恶化 residual calibration？

这个问题重要，因为我的 thesis 已经观察到 balancing 有时会伤害 elderly group。我需要区分真正有帮助的 representation correction 和只是在样本比例上做表面平衡的 intervention。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: How much synthetic subgroup expansion is needed before elderly-group MAE and calibration estimates become stable enough for model selection in historical face age estimation?
- Why it matters: small subgroup test sets can make hidden degradation look like noise, which weakens decision-relevant evaluation.
- Possible data: my historical portrait validation/test splits plus synthetic or augmented subgroup cohorts generated under controlled age, gender, and visual-quality conditions.
- Possible method: simulate different subgroup test-set sizes; compare real-only estimates with synthetic-augmented estimates; measure confidence intervals and rank stability across model families.
- Evaluation metric: confidence interval width for elderly-group MAE, model-rank stability, subgroup calibration error, and false reassurance rate when global MAE is stable.
- Thesis connection: extends my residual diagnostics into a reliability-estimation question under historical domain shift.
- Risk or limitation: synthetic augmentation may stabilize estimates numerically while failing to represent historically meaningful visual variation.

中文:

研究问题: 在 historical face age estimation 中，需要多少 synthetic subgroup expansion，elderly-group MAE 和 calibration estimate 才足够稳定，可以用于 model selection？

这个问题把我的 thesis 从"发现 elderly group failure"推进到"判断这个 failure 的估计是否足够可靠"。它也能帮助我设计更严谨的 subgroup-aware evaluation protocol。
