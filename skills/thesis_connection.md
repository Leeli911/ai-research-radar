# Skill: Thesis Connection / Thesis 连接分析

## Purpose / 目的

**English**

Every selected paper must explicitly connect back to my master thesis. The connection should not be generic. It should explain how the paper helps reinterpret, extend, or challenge my empirical findings from historical facial age estimation.

**中文**

每篇入选论文都必须明确连接回我的 master thesis。不能只写“相关”，而要说明它如何帮助我重新解释、扩展或挑战我在 historical facial age estimation 中的实证发现。

## Required Connection Points / 必须分析的连接点

For each paper, check its relation to:

1. **Aggregate metric masking**  
   Does the paper explain why global metrics can hide subgroup failure?

2. **Subgroup degradation**  
   Does it study uneven error, subgroup robustness, fairness degradation, or worst-group performance?

3. **Balancing trade-offs**  
   Does it help explain why balancing can help some groups but hurt elderly-group performance?

4. **Cascade fragility**  
   Does it explain why conditional routing, multi-stage models, or complex architectures may become fragile under shift?

5. **Historical domain shift**  
   Can the method or concept be adapted to historical portraits, temporal shift, archival data, or changing visual distributions?

中文:

每篇论文都要检查它是否能连接到五个 thesis 观察：平均指标掩盖问题、subgroup 退化、balancing 的 trade-off、cascade model 的脆弱性，以及 historical domain shift。

## Output Template / 输出模板

### Explicit Conceptual Bridge

English:

This paper connects to my thesis because...

中文:

这篇论文和我的 thesis 的概念连接是...

### Methodological Relevance

English:

The method may help my research by...

中文:

这个方法对我的研究有用，因为...

### Reinterpretation of My Thesis Findings

English:

This paper suggests that my thesis finding may be interpreted as...

中文:

这篇论文让我可以把 thesis 里的发现重新解释为...

## Quality Bar / 质量标准

**English**

A good thesis connection should be specific enough to become a proposal sentence or interview answer. It should mention at least one concrete thesis observation and one concrete paper contribution.

**中文**

好的 thesis connection 应该能直接变成 proposal 句子或面试回答。它必须同时提到一个具体 thesis 观察和一个具体论文贡献。
