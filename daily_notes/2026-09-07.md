### Paper 1

Title: A five-phase evaluation framework for diagnostic and predictive medical artificial intelligence
Link: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-03155-7
Year / Venue: 2026 / npj Digital Medicine
Source: npj Digital Medicine primary article page
Research line: lifecycle evaluation, clinical AI validation, operational robustness, real-world monitoring
Research function: Evaluation, Monitoring, and Diagnosis
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 4; proposal citation value 5; feasibility 4; strategic long-term fit 4.
Usefulness: proposal-level evaluation framework and monitoring reference

#### English Summary

- Problem: medical AI evaluation often stops at retrospective, algorithm-centered metrics, while clinical deployment requires evidence about external validity, workflow interaction, clinical benefit, operational robustness, drift, and real-world safety.
- Method: the paper proposes a five-phase evaluation framework: technical validation, operational robustness validation, controlled interaction validation, clinical evidence validation, and real-world integration validation. It adds dynamic phase gates, fallback triggers, versioned evaluation, and phase re-entry when drift or safety signals appear.
- Evaluation: this is a conceptual and methodological framework built from diagnostic-test research, clinical-trial methodology, regulatory expectations, robustness testing, and human-AI interaction study designs. It maps multicenter external validation, shadow-mode testing, human-AI comparison, randomized trials, post-market surveillance, and adaptive designs into one lifecycle.
- Findings: the paper argues that trustworthy medical AI needs staged evidence rather than one-off performance claims. Calibration, subgroup stability, operational behavior, human-AI confidence alignment, and real-world surveillance should be treated as evolving evaluation dimensions, not optional add-ons.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: medical AI 常常在 retrospective test set 上报告一个 AUC、accuracy 或 calibration result, 但真实临床使用还需要知道模型在不同医院、设备、工作流、医生交互和长期数据流中是否可靠。它把 evaluation 从一次性 test 扩展成 lifecycle evidence。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 看到 global MAE 掩盖 elderly-group failure, 但如果把这个问题放到更真实的部署逻辑中, evaluation 不能只问模型是否平均准确, 还要问 subgroup reliability 是否经过 external validation, calibration 是否稳定, shift 是否触发 fallback, intervention 后是否需要重新评估。

技术直觉: 这篇论文不是提出一个模型, 而是提出一个 evidence architecture。先建立技术鲁棒性, 再检查真实操作条件, 再检查人机交互, 再验证临床获益, 最后进入 real-world monitoring。对我的研究方向来说, 它把 subgroup degradation 从 post-hoc plot 变成 lifecycle gate 的一部分。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 论文批评 algorithm-centric retrospective metrics, 这直接对应我的 thesis 中 global MAE 无法代表 elderly-group reliability。它支持我把 aggregate MAE 降级为 Phase 1 的基础指标, 而不是最终证据。
- Elderly-group degradation: calibration stability and cross-subgroup robustness 可以把 elderly-group error 放进正式 evaluation components, 而不是作为附录分析。我的 elderly failure 可以被表述为 technical validation 中尚未通过的 subgroup-stability signal。
- Balancing trade-offs: 如果 balancing 后某些 subgroup 变差, 这个 framework 会要求 phase-gated re-evaluation, 而不是只看 intervention 后的平均分。它帮助我说明为什么 fairness intervention 需要 fallback trigger。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded conditional model 的 fragile behavior 可以被视为 model-version or architecture-specific risk。更复杂结构上线前需要 operational robustness and subgroup phase gates, 不能只依赖 internal validation。
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits 可以类比 multicenter and temporal clinical validation。archive, period, scan quality, and annotation uncertainty should be treated as external validation dimensions under a staged evidence framework.

#### Limitation

The framework is broad and clinical, so it does not provide a direct algorithm for detecting hidden subgroup failure. For my proposal, I should use it as evaluation architecture, then pair it with concrete diagnostic methods such as differential-performance trees, slice discovery, or conformal subgroup monitoring.

#### Follow-up

### Paper 2

Title: AIA^2: Attribute-Agnostic Imbalance Augmentation for Subgroup Robustness
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30297
Year / Venue: 2026 / arXiv
Source: arXiv primary page and PDF metadata
Research line: hidden subgroup imbalance, worst-group robustness, latent slice discovery, augmentation
Research function: Diagnosis, Intervention, and Evaluation
Screening Score: 25/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 5; methodological novelty 5; proposal citation value 5; feasibility 5; strategic long-term fit 5.
Usefulness: proposal anchor and Recommended for paper card

#### English Summary

- Problem: subgroup imbalance is not only label imbalance. Attributes such as topic, demographic context, style, or content can create underrepresented subgroups that degrade model performance, even when explicit subgroup labels are unavailable.
- Method: AIA^2 discovers latent imbalance patterns from semantic distributions, identifies slices that combine learning difficulty and subgroup imbalance deficits, and uses LLM-based subgroup-aware augmentation to add targeted examples without requiring explicit attribute annotations.
- Evaluation: the paper evaluates the framework on five corpora with rich domains and attribute values, including social-issue and diverse-topic settings. It compares against competitive baselines and reports worst-subgroup robustness, ablations, and analyses of each component.
- Findings: AIA^2 improves performance on the lowest-performing subgroups and gives consistent gains over baselines. The useful contribution is not generic text generation, but the pipeline linking hidden attribute imbalance, hard-slice diagnosis, targeted augmentation, and worst-group evaluation.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: imbalance 不只是 label 数量不平衡。某些 content/context attributes, 比如 topic、demographic context、style 或 data source, 会形成隐藏 subgroup。模型可能在这些 subgroup 上表现差, 但训练数据里没有现成的 subgroup label。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 中 balancing 有时让 elderly group 更差, 这说明只按 age/gender 的显式标签平衡可能没有对准真实 failure unit。AIA^2 的思路是先找 latent slices, 再判断哪些 slice 同时困难且样本不足, 最后做 targeted augmentation。这比简单 balancing 更接近我的研究问题。

技术直觉: 如果一个 subgroup 既少又难, overall loss 很容易忽视它。AIA^2 先用 semantic distribution 找到潜在属性结构, 再把 learning difficulty 和 imbalance deficit 合起来排序, 最后生成或补充针对性样本。对我的 historical portraits 来说, latent slice 可以从 archive style、portrait period、image quality、pose、gender route confidence 和 age residual pattern 中学习出来。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: AIA^2 关注 lowest-performing subgroups, 说明 overall performance gain 不能证明 hidden slices 已经可靠。我的 thesis 中 global MAE 掩盖 elderly failure 可以被重新表述为 worst-slice performance 没有被优化。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly group may be only a visible proxy for a harder latent slice, such as `elderly x low image quality x specific archive period`. AIA^2 gives me a way to search for such latent imbalance before deciding what to augment.
- Balancing trade-offs: 这篇论文最直接连接我的 balancing backfire。它说明 intervention 不应该只做 label or demographic balancing, 而要区分 which subgroup is underrepresented and hard. Otherwise balancing may move mass toward the wrong cells.
- Cascade fragility: cascaded gender-conditional age estimation could create route-specific latent slices. AIA^2 suggests checking whether one route contains harder and more imbalanced elderly cases before adding data or changing the architecture.
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits contain context attributes that are not always annotated. A latent attribute-agnostic slice method could help diagnose period/source/style imbalance under historical shift, then test whether targeted augmentation improves elderly residuals.

#### Limitation

The empirical setting is NLP and uses LLM augmentation, so direct transfer to historical facial age regression is not automatic. I should borrow the hidden-slice diagnosis and targeted augmentation logic, not the exact text-generation mechanism.

#### Follow-up

Deep-read this paper for a paper card. The most valuable extraction is an experiment design: define latent visual/context slices from embeddings and metadata, rank slices by residual difficulty and support deficit, then compare naive age/gender balancing with targeted slice augmentation.

### Paper 3

Title: Counterfactually Fair Conformal Prediction
Link: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v300/guldogan26a.html
Year / Venue: 2026 / AISTATS 2026, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research
Source: PMLR primary page
Research line: conformal prediction, counterfactual fairness, fair uncertainty quantification
Research function: Evaluation, Monitoring, and Intervention
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Rubric: conceptual relevance 4; methodological novelty 5; proposal citation value 4; feasibility 4; strategic long-term fit 5.
Usefulness: uncertainty-aware fairness method reference

#### English Summary

- Problem: conformal prediction provides finite-sample valid prediction sets, but standard conformal sets can still be unfair because the prediction set for an individual may change under counterfactual protected-attribute interventions.
- Method: the paper proposes Counterfactually Fair Conformal Prediction, a post-training split-conformal procedure that symmetrizes conformity scores across protected-attribute interventions. The goal is to preserve marginal coverage while enforcing set-level counterfactual fairness.
- Evaluation: the method is tested on synthetic and real datasets across regression and classification tasks. The paper compares standard split CP, post-hoc union, counterfactually fair predictors followed by CP, counterfactual fair representations, plug-in counterfactual fairness, and CF-CP.
- Findings: CF-CP achieves counterfactually fair prediction sets while maintaining target coverage, usually with only modest increases in prediction-set size. It does not require retraining the base predictor, which makes it useful as a monitoring and evaluation layer.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: uncertainty quantification 本身也可能不公平。一个模型即使 coverage 达标, prediction set 仍然可能因为 protected attribute 的 counterfactual change 而改变。换句话说, 不只是 point prediction 要 fair, uncertainty set 也要检查 fairness。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 里 calibration 和 confidence analysis 已经说明, subgroup reliability 不能只看 residual mean。对 elderly portraits 来说, prediction interval 或 uncertainty flag 也可能对某些 subgroup 更宽、更窄或 coverage 不足。CF-CP 提醒我, uncertainty-aware monitoring 也需要 fairness definition。

技术直觉: 标准 conformal prediction 用 conformity score 构造 prediction set。CF-CP 的关键是对 protected attribute interventions 下的 conformity scores 做对称聚合, 让 set-valued output 对 protected attribute 的反事实变化保持不变, 同时保留 marginal coverage。对我的任务, 可以把 protected attribute 从 gender 扩展为 age-group, source period, or route assignment, 但 causal assumptions must be handled carefully.

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: CF-CP shows that even valid average coverage can hide fairness problems in individual or subgroup uncertainty sets. This extends my aggregate MAE masking observation into uncertainty-aware evaluation.
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits may have wider intervals, lower coverage, or overconfident underestimation. A conformal layer could measure whether elderly uncertainty sets are decision-relevant rather than only whether point residuals are high.
- Balancing trade-offs: if balancing changes residual distribution by subgroup, conformal thresholds and set sizes may shift unevenly. CF-CP gives a way to ask whether uncertainty outputs remain fair after intervention.
- Cascade fragility: cascaded models may produce different uncertainty behavior across route assignments. A route-aware conformal audit could test whether conditional routing creates unfair prediction-set behavior, not only higher MAE.
- Historical domain shift: under historical domain shift, exchangeability can fail. This limitation is useful: it forces my proposal to distinguish standard conformal validity, group-conditional coverage, and shift-aware coverage before claiming reliable monitoring.

#### Limitation

The method assumes a structural causal model and exchangeability conditions that may be difficult for historical portraits. It is a strong uncertainty-fairness reference, but my use should start with empirical subgroup coverage and route-aware conformal diagnostics before making counterfactual claims.

#### Follow-up

Use this as a method reference for fair uncertainty monitoring: compare global coverage, elderly coverage, route-specific coverage, interval width, and underestimation-with-high-confidence across historical source and period splits.
