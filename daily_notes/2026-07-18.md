## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Rethinking fairness in medical imaging: Maximizing group-specific performance with application to skin disease diagnosis
Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1361841526000198
Year / Venue: 2026, Medical Image Analysis, Volume 109, Article 103950
Source: official ScienceDirect article page, verified 2026-07-18
Research line: group-specific medical-image reliability and fairness intervention under distributional differences
Research function: Explanation / Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor, intervention reference, and warning paper for balancing trade-offs

#### English Summary

- Problem: conventional fairness objectives in medical imaging can equalize group metrics without maximizing performance for each patient group, especially when out-group data have different or harmful distributional structure.
- Method: the paper reframes fairness as maximizing group-specific performance and proposes SPARE, a sample reweighting method that decides how much each out-group sample should contribute when training a model for a target group.
- Evaluation: experiments are conducted on skin disease diagnosis datasets with group-specific and overall performance metrics, comparing group-specific training, all-data training, and reweighted use of out-group data.
- Findings: group-specific models can benefit from out-group samples when those samples are selectively weighted, but blindly pooling all groups can create performance trade-offs. The paper argues that fairness should be evaluated through whether each group receives a stronger model, not only whether gaps shrink.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: fairness 不应该只被理解为不同 group 的指标差距变小，而应该看每个 patient group 是否真正得到更好的预测性能。如果某些 out-group samples 和 target group 的分布差异很大，直接合并数据可能会伤害目标组。

为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 中 balancing sometimes worsened elderly-group performance 很接近。我的问题不是简单地让 age groups 数量更平衡，而是要判断哪些额外样本真的帮助 elderly reliability，哪些样本只是改变 overall distribution 并引入新的 shift。

技术直觉: SPARE 的核心直觉是 out-group data 不是全部有用或全部有害，而是要根据它们对 target group model 的 utility 和 similarity 做 reweighting。对我的研究来说，这可以转化为: 在 historical portrait age estimation 中，不同 source、period、gender 或 image-quality slice 的样本不应被同等地加入 elderly-focused training。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 如果只看 overall diagnosis accuracy 或 overall MAE，就可能看不到某个 group 的模型是否因为数据池化而受损。论文支持我把 aggregate metric masking 写成 evaluation objective mismatch。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly group 可以被看作一个 target group。论文提醒我，提升 elderly performance 需要检查哪些 non-elderly or non-source samples 对 elderly model 有帮助，而不是默认全量平衡。
- Balancing trade-offs: 这是最直接的连接。SPARE 把 balancing trade-off 具体化为 sample-level utility 问题，能帮助我解释为什么 naive balancing 在 thesis 中可能 worsened elderly-group performance。
- Cascade fragility: group-specific models需要判断何时使用哪个 group model。如果 routing 或 group definition 在 historical shift 下不稳定，它可能复现我 cascaded conditional model 的脆弱性。
- Historical domain shift: skin disease datasets 的 group distribution differences 可以类比 historical portraits 中的 era/source/quality shift。方法上，我可以测试 source-aware or age-aware reweighting 是否比 naive balancing 更稳。

#### Limitation

The paper studies dermatology classification rather than facial age regression. I should borrow the sample-utility and group-specific evaluation logic, while translating the metrics into elderly MAE, residual skew, underestimation rate, calibration error, and worst-slice MAE.

#### Follow-up

Use this paper to design a thesis-extension experiment: compare naive age balancing, all-source pooling, source-aware reweighting, and elderly-targeted reweighting under held-out historical source or period splits.

### Paper 2

Title: Discovering Latent Groups for Robust Classification
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23609
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint, submitted 2026-06-29
Source: official arXiv record, verified 2026-07-18
Research line: hidden subgroup discovery and robustness without known group labels
Research function: Diagnosis / Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for hidden reliability-unit discovery

#### English Summary

- Problem: robust classification often assumes that group labels are known, but real failures may concentrate in latent subgroups defined by spurious correlations, image conditions, or hard-to-name data slices.
- Method: the paper proposes a Neural Classification Tree that jointly learns a latent group partition and classifiers at tree nodes, using correctness patterns to separate easy and difficult regions of the data.
- Evaluation: the method is evaluated on spurious-correlation and group-robust classification benchmarks, where performance is judged by robust or worst-group behavior rather than only average accuracy.
- Findings: learning latent groups can improve robustness when explicit group annotations are unavailable. The paper makes hidden subgroup discovery an optimization target rather than a post-hoc error analysis step.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: many subgroup failures are hidden because the relevant group labels are not available. 在我的 thesis 中，elderly group 是 visible subgroup，但真正的 failure unit 可能是 elderly + low image quality + specific source + gender-conditioned representation shift。

为什么重要: 如果我只按 age group 或 gender group 做评估，仍然可能漏掉更细的 hidden reliability degradation。这个 paper 提供了一个方向: 让模型或 diagnostic method 自动发现 performance-relevant latent groups。

技术直觉: Neural Classification Tree 把数据递归分到不同节点，每个节点对应一种 latent reliability region。和普通 subgroup reporting 不同，它不是先假设 subgroup 再评估，而是从 model correctness 和 representation structure 中学习哪些 slices 容易失败。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: latent groups 的动机就是平均准确率可能隐藏少数 failure regions。它支持我把 aggregate MAE 的问题进一步扩展为 hidden-stratification problem。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly group 可能只是 visible symptom；真正的高误差 slice 可能是 elderly samples 中某个 latent branch。这个方法能帮助我找出 elderly degradation 的内部异质性。
- Balancing trade-offs: 如果 balancing 用的是粗 age bins，它可能没有平衡真正的 latent failure groups。论文帮助我解释为什么粗粒度 balancing 可能改善一个 group label，却伤害另一个 latent subgroup。
- Cascade fragility: Neural Classification Tree 本身也有 routing structure，因此它既是方法参考，也是 warning case。我的研究需要检验 learned routes 在 historical source shift 下是否稳定，否则 latent routing 也可能变成新的 cascade fragility。
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits 的 shift 未必被 metadata 完整记录。latent group discovery 可以帮助识别由 pose, archive source, restoration style, or image degradation 共同形成的 hidden reliability units。

#### Limitation

The paper focuses on classification benchmarks. My task is age regression, so I would need to adapt the idea from correctness-based group discovery to residual-risk, underestimation-risk, or calibration-error based latent group discovery.

#### Follow-up

Prototype a residual-risk tree over facial embeddings and image-quality proxies, then test whether discovered latent groups explain elderly MAE better than age/gender labels alone.

### Paper 3

Title: Demographic Calibration Gaps in Breast Cancer Risk Prediction
Link: https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2026.06.17.26355900v1
Year / Venue: 2026 medRxiv preprint, posted 2026-06-22
Source: official medRxiv record/search metadata, verified 2026-07-18
Research line: subgroup calibration gaps, external validation, and conformal coverage degradation
Research function: Diagnosis / Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: monitoring reference for subgroup calibration and decision-relevant reliability

#### English Summary

- Problem: a risk model can appear well calibrated on average while specific demographic groups receive unreliable probabilities, especially after external distribution shift.
- Method: the paper introduces Demographic Calibration Gap Score variants to measure the range and average pairwise differences of subgroup calibration errors, then evaluates calibration and conformal prediction across demographic groups.
- Evaluation: experiments use the Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer dataset for internal validation and a MIMIC-IV breast cancer cohort for external validation, comparing raw models, global calibration, subgroup calibration, and conformal prediction coverage.
- Findings: strong internal discrimination did not transfer to the external cohort. Global calibration did not reliably close subgroup calibration gaps, and conformal coverage could become demographically uneven under external shift.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: global calibration 或 global AUROC 看起来不错，不代表每个 demographic subgroup 都能得到可靠风险分数。外部 cohort shift 之后，某些群体可能出现更严重的 calibration error 或 coverage failure。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 已经做过 calibration analysis 和 residual diagnostics。这个 paper 给了我一个更直接的指标思路: 不只是看整体 calibration error，而是看 subgroup calibration gap 在 external shift 下是否扩大。

技术直觉: DCGS 把每个 subgroup 的 calibration error 放在一起比较，关注最大差距或平均 pairwise gap。这个思路很适合我的 age-estimation setting: 我可以把 calibration error 换成 residual calibration、prediction interval coverage、underestimation risk, or high-residual confidence calibration。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 论文的核心就是 global calibration and discrimination can mask subgroup calibration failure。这直接对应我的 global MAE 掩盖 elderly-group degradation。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly group 可以作为一个 demographic subgroup，也可以和 gender/source/quality 形成 intersectional calibration slices。它帮助我把 elderly failure 从 error magnitude 扩展到 confidence reliability。
- Balancing trade-offs: 如果 balancing 改善 overall MAE 但扩大 elderly calibration gap，那么它仍然是有害 intervention。DCGS-style metric 可以作为 balancing side effect 的监测工具。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded conditional models may have route-specific calibration gaps. 如果 gender route 下的 elderly predictions 更 miscalibrated，global calibration 不会暴露这个问题。
- Historical domain shift: external validation on MIMIC-IV maps naturally to held-out historical source or period validation. The paper gives me a template for asking whether calibration gaps widen under archival shift.

#### Limitation

The paper studies binary breast cancer risk prediction and demographic calibration, not continuous facial age estimation. I need a regression version of calibration-gap scoring, such as group-wise prediction interval coverage gap, residual-risk calibration gap, or underestimation-risk calibration gap.

#### Follow-up

Build a small metric prototype: Subgroup Residual Calibration Gap for age estimation, computed across age group, gender, source, and discovered latent slices under source-held-out validation.
