## Paper 1 — A statistical framework for identifying subgroup vulnerability to predictive multiplicity in clinical AI

Enock Adu Bonsu. Submitted **2026-09-29**. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37064v1) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37064v1).

**Research function:** Diagnosis → Evaluation.

### English Summary

**Verified from paper**

- **Problem:** Model disagreement can concentrate in clinically important subgroups.
- **Method:** A vulnerability index combines model disagreement with severity, with subgroup inference and multiplicity adjustment.
- **Evaluation:** Random forest versus logistic regression across 158 prespecified groups in MIMIC-IV/eICU, plus simulations.
- **Findings:** The age ≥80/cardiac intersection has the largest point estimate but lacks adequate power. No group passes the full high-priority rule. Logistic regression falls outside the prespecified near-optimal model tolerance, so this comparison does not establish a Rashomon-set bound. Cross-split ranking correlation is only 0.032, and small-sample inference is poorly calibrated. [Results §§3.2–3.8](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37064v1).

### 中文解释

论文试图把模型分歧、后果严重性和统计证据分开。它的重要价值也在限制：老年交叉组的高估计值不能直接成为确定的高风险结论，近优模型条件未满足，风险排序跨划分不稳定。技术直觉是同时报告“风险有多大”和“证据有多稳”。[原文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37064v1)

### Connection to My Thesis

**Explicit Conceptual Bridge — my interpretation:** I should distinguish the severity of elderly underestimation in my thesis from how precisely a small historical subgroup supports that estimate.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection |
|---|---|
| Aggregate metric masking | I will compare model-pair residual differences within groups even when overall MAE is similar. |
| Elderly-group degradation | I will report elderly error severity and uncertainty separately; insufficient power does not establish safety. |
| Balancing trade-offs | I will check whether balancing changes subgroup error or only the precision and composition of my audit. |
| Cascade fragility | I will compare cascade and regression predictions on identical people; a near-optimality claim requires a tolerance fixed before testing. |
| Historical domain shift | I will repeat the audit across archive-held-out samples and measure whether subgroup rankings survive the change of source. |

**Methodological Relevance:** My first experiment can use paired residuals and bootstrap intervals; I need not import a clinical severity index into age estimation.

**Reinterpretation of My Thesis Findings:** My simpler-regression result is a model comparison in a particular domain, not proof about every nearly optimal model.

**中文连接：** 我会把整体 MAE 相近时的子群分歧、老年组误差与区间、平衡后的样本组成、级联与回归的配对残差，以及跨档案排名稳定性放在同一评估表中。我不会把统计把握度不足当成没有风险，也不会把临床指数直接移植成年龄估计结论。

**Needs full-paper verification:** Index construction, simulation calibration, data splitting, and reproducibility need deeper scrutiny before methodological adoption.

## Paper 2 — A Rank Graduation metric for Algorithmic fairness

Dalia Atif and Paolo Giudici. Submitted **2026-09-30**. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39025v1) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39025v1).

**Research function:** Evaluation → Diagnosis.

### English Summary

**Verified from paper**

- **Problem:** Group summaries can obscure who bears large prediction errors.
- **Method:** RGF/AURGF examine ranked error burdens, supplemented by a centered Cramér–von Mises permutation test and feature-removal analysis.
- **Evaluation:** Simulations and four classifiers on 2017 New York HMDA applications; the empirical split preserves group imbalance.
- **Findings:** Imbalance can reverse descriptive fairness comparisons. Random forest has the strongest AURGF in the reported application, yet the fairness null is rejected for every model. Relative ranking therefore does not establish fairness. This is not a temporal deployment-shift experiment. [§4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39025v1).

### 中文解释

技术直觉是先按误差负担排序，再检查高误差区域中的群体差异。价值在于把相对排名与统计检验分开：某模型比其他模型“更公平”，仍可能有明显差异。群体不平衡会影响描述性排名，因此不能只看一个公平分数。[原文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39025v1)

### Connection to My Thesis

**Explicit Conceptual Bridge — my interpretation:** My residual diagnostics can become an explicit audit of who occupies the severe-error tail, alongside average age error.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection |
|---|---|
| Aggregate metric masking | I will examine elderly representation in the largest absolute and signed-underestimation errors. |
| Elderly-group degradation | I will retain error magnitude in years; a rank alone cannot tell me whether elderly mistakes are consequential. |
| Balancing trade-offs | I will separate training resampling from evaluation-population composition, testing both natural and standardized mixtures. |
| Cascade fragility | I will stratify tail errors by routing correctness when reference gate labels exist; this is my diagnostic extension. |
| Historical domain shift | I will compare error-tail composition across archives with fixed definitions rather than interpreting mixture changes as within-group deterioration. |

**Methodological Relevance:** Sorting saved residuals and plotting group-specific empirical distributions fits my existing evaluation skills. I would validate any regression adaptation independently.

**Reinterpretation of My Thesis Findings:** An apparent improvement after balancing may depend on which population weights and error summaries I report; it need not imply reduced elderly underestimation.

**中文连接：** 我会检查老年样本是否集中在大误差尾部，同时保留以年为单位的误差；分别改变训练平衡与测试群体比例，按路由正确性分析级联，再跨档案比较。我的关键区分是“同组样本变差”与“测试组成改变”。这仍是待验证解释。

**Needs full-paper verification:** Permutation exchangeability, tie handling, and adaptation from probabilistic classification errors to age residuals. Feature removal alone does not identify causal discrimination.

## Two Research Questions / 两个研究问题

### RQ1 — Does model disagreement identify reproducible elderly failure under archive shift?

- **Research question:** Among age models within a validation-MAE tolerance fixed in advance, does elderly prediction disagreement identify excess underestimation that replicates in a held-out archive?
- **Why it matters:** I need to separate useful warning signals from unstable subgroup rankings.
- **Possible data:** Existing thesis predictions and age labels, subject identifiers, and archive metadata where available; otherwise archive shift remains untested.
- **Possible method:** Freeze a simple regression, a multitask model, and a cascade. Select eligible models on validation data only. Define elderly groups before audit; use one archive for threshold development and another for confirmation, with person-disjoint splits. Compare disagreement with random sampling and single-model uncertainty. Keep the natural/balanced training factor separate.
- **Evaluation metric:** Elderly MAE, mean signed residual, rate of underestimation exceeding a preregistered age-error threshold, ranking stability, and detection precision at fixed audit coverage; paired person-level bootstrap intervals and multiplicity correction for prespecified groups.
- **Thesis connection:** I test whether cascade disagreement helps expose the elderly failure hidden by aggregate MAE.
- **Risk or limitation:** Models may share the same error; no eligible model pair or too few elderly identities may prevent a conclusion. Disagreement is not causal explanation.

**中文：** 在验证 MAE 落入预先固定容差的年龄模型中，老年样本上的预测分歧能否识别并跨档案复现严重低估？我会用已有预测与年龄标签，按人划分、按档案确认，比较分歧、随机抽样和单模型不确定性。指标包括老年 MAE、带符号残差、严重低估率及固定审计覆盖率下的检出精度。这延伸级联脆弱性观察；共同犯错和小样本可能让分歧信号失效。

### RQ2 — Can changing group proportions reverse the apparent benefit of balancing?

- **Research question:** With predictions fixed, can changing only elderly prevalence reverse the natural-versus-balanced model ranking while elderly error-tail risk stays unchanged?
- **Why it matters:** I need to distinguish an evaluation-mixture artifact from a genuine intervention effect.
- **Possible data:** The same labeled portrait test set, with enough observations per age group; no new foundation-model training is required.
- **Possible method:** Evaluate existing natural/balanced models under a prespecified grid of age-group weights, preserving every within-group residual distribution. Repeat separately by archive and model architecture. Plot weighted MAE, tail composition, and within-group risks. Use rank summaries as descriptive diagnostics, not a fairness certificate.
- **Evaluation metric:** Ranking-reversal frequency across the fixed grid, elderly MAE, elderly probability of absolute error above a fixed threshold, underestimation tail, and stratified bootstrap uncertainty.
- **Thesis connection:** I directly test a measurement explanation for balancing trade-offs without claiming it explains the original training outcome.
- **Risk or limitation:** Reweighting simulates mixture shift only; it cannot reproduce new image quality, label noise, or within-group concept changes. Multiple portraits per person require clustered resampling.

**中文：** 固定所有预测，只改变老年组在测试总体中的权重，是否会让自然训练与平衡训练模型的排名反转，而老年组尾部风险不变？我会用已有标注与残差，预设年龄权重网格，按档案及架构分别比较整体 MAE、尾部组成和老年组风险，并给出分层区间。这能检验平衡收益是否依赖评估组成，但不能模拟组内视觉变化或解释训练中的因果机制。
