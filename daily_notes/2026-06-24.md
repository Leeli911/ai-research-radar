## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Disentanglement and Assessment of Shortcuts in Ophthalmological Retinal Imaging Exams
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09640
Year / Venue: 2025 / arXiv preprint
Research function: Diagnosis / Evaluation
Research line: age-related shortcut encoding, subgroup disparity, and intervention instability
Screening score: 24/25 = relevance 5 + novelty 4 + citation value 5 + feasibility 5 + long-term fit 5
Usefulness: proposal anchor and direct thesis analogue

#### English Summary

- **Problem:** High aggregate diabetic-retinopathy performance may coexist with demographic disparity because models encode age or gender/sex as shortcuts. The paper asks whether sensitive-attribute disentanglement improves fairness without damaging task performance.
- **Method:** ConvNeXt V2, DINOv2, and Swin V2 are trained on mBRSET fundus images to predict diabetic retinopathy and sensitive attributes. The authors then apply representation disentanglement intended to remove age and gender/sex information from disease prediction.
- **Evaluation:** The study reports disease AUROC, sensitive-attribute predictability, subgroup fairness gaps, and pre/post-disentanglement performance across three architectures on the diverse mBRSET dataset.
- **Findings:** Disease performance reaches 94% AUROC, while age and gender/sex remain predictable at 91% and 77% AUROC. DINOv2 shows a 10-point AUROC gap between age groups. Disentanglement improves DINOv2 by 2 points but reduces ConvNeXt V2 and Swin V2 performance by 7 and 3 points.
- **Technical intuition:** Removing demographic information is not equivalent to removing harmful shortcut use. Age can be entangled with legitimate disease cues, image-acquisition patterns, and subgroup prevalence. The same intervention can therefore help one representation and damage another.

#### 中文解释

- **解决的问题:** 论文检验高 overall AUROC 是否掩盖 age-group disparity，以及去除 age、gender/sex representation 是否真的能改善 fairness。
- **为什么重要:** 模型可能在总体上表现很好，但仍能从 embedding 中高精度恢复敏感属性，并在某个 age group 上明显退化。更重要的是，同一个 disentanglement intervention 在不同 architecture 上出现相反结果。
- **技术直觉:** Sensitive attribute 既可能是 harmful shortcut，也可能和真实疾病线索、采集条件或 prevalence 纠缠。直接删除相关信息可能减少偏差，也可能误删有效 signal，因此 intervention 必须做 subgroup-level、architecture-specific evaluation。
- **核心发现:** DINOv2 出现约 10-point age-group AUROC gap；age 和 gender/sex 可被高精度预测；disentanglement 对 DINOv2 有益，但使 ConvNeXt V2 和 Swin V2 退化。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- **Aggregate metric masking:** 最高 94% overall AUROC 仍可掩盖 10-point age-group gap，与我的 global MAE 掩盖 elderly-group failure 直接同构。
- **Elderly-group degradation:** 论文把 age 当作可预测的 sensitive attribute，并报告 age-group disparity。我可以进一步检验 elderly underestimation 是否随 age-decodability、residual variance 和 calibration error 一起上升。
- **Balancing trade-offs:** Disentanglement 的效果随 architecture 反转，说明 fairness intervention 不是单调改进。这支持我把 balancing 导致 elderly error 上升解释为 representation-dependent trade-off，而不是简单的数据量问题。
- **Cascade fragility:** 如果 gender router 和 age head 都编码 demographic shortcut，硬删除或错误路由会在 cascade 中累积。论文提示我需要分别测 router leakage、route-specific error 和最终 age error。
- **Historical domain shift:** mBRSET 的采集与人群结构对应我的 archive source、period 和 photographic-process variation。相同的 attribute-removal 方法必须在跨时期 shift 下验证，不能只在随机 split 上判断有效。

#### Limitation

The abstract does not establish whether sensitive-attribute information causally drives disease predictions. The study is also evaluated on one retinal dataset; cross-site and temporal robustness of disentanglement remains uncertain.

#### Follow-up

I should measure age and gender decodability in existing portrait embeddings, then test whether leakage predicts elderly residual structure across archive periods before attempting representation removal.

### Paper 2

Title: Improving Performance, Robustness, and Fairness of Radiographic AI Models with Finely-Controllable Synthetic Data
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16783
Year / Venue: 2025 / arXiv preprint
Research function: Intervention / Evaluation
Research line: demographic balancing, synthetic pretraining, and out-of-distribution fairness
Screening score: 24/25 = relevance 5 + novelty 5 + citation value 5 + feasibility 4 + long-term fit 5
Usefulness: data-intervention and balancing-strategy reference

#### English Summary

- **Problem:** Real medical datasets often lack both scale and demographic coverage. Naively adding synthetic samples may increase volume without improving generalization or subgroup fairness.
- **Method:** RoentGen-v2 generates chest radiographs conditioned jointly on clinical findings and sex, age, and race/ethnicity. The authors create more than 565,000 demographically balanced synthetic images and compare supervised synthetic pretraining followed by real-data fine-tuning with naive real-synthetic mixing.
- **Evaluation:** Downstream disease classifiers are evaluated on more than 137,000 radiographs from five institutions, including out-of-distribution generalization and demographic underdiagnosis gaps.
- **Findings:** Synthetic pretraining followed by real fine-tuning improves downstream accuracy by 6.5%, versus 2.7% for naive mixing, while reducing the underdiagnosis fairness gap by 19.3%.
- **Technical intuition:** Synthetic data is most useful as a representation-shaping pretraining distribution. Fine-tuning on real data then restores clinical realism. Naive mixing lets synthetic artefacts and real examples compete in one objective and can dilute rather than repair minority signals.

#### 中文解释

- **解决的问题:** 论文研究如何用可控 synthetic data 同时改善模型的 accuracy、OOD generalization 和 demographic fairness，而不是只增加样本量。
- **为什么重要:** 结果表明“加数据”本身不够。训练顺序决定 synthetic data 是帮助 representation learning，还是把 synthetic artefact 混入最终 decision boundary。
- **技术直觉:** 先在 demographic-balanced synthetic data 上 pretrain，可以让模型看到更完整的 subgroup combination；再用 real data fine-tune，可以校正生成域与真实域之间的差异。Naive mixing 缺少这种阶段分工。
- **核心发现:** 五机构评估中，synthetic pretraining + real fine-tuning 的 accuracy gain 明显高于 naive mixing，并同时降低 underdiagnosis fairness gap。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- **Aggregate metric masking:** 论文同时报告 accuracy、OOD generalization 和 underdiagnosis gap，避免把平均性能提升误写成 subgroup reliability 提升。
- **Elderly-group degradation:** Age-conditioned generation可以针对 elderly portraits 构造受控补充数据，但必须单独评估 elderly MAE、underestimation 和 tail error，而不能只看 global gain。
- **Balancing trade-offs:** 论文直接说明 balanced synthetic data 的使用方式很重要。我的 balancing 失败可能来自 naive mixing/resampling 改变了训练分布，却没有先学习稳定的 elderly representation。
- **Cascade fragility:** Synthetic-to-real 是一个两阶段 pipeline，但其作用是 pretrain then correct；我的 gender-conditional cascade 则是 prediction-time hard routing。前者提示阶段化学习可以帮助，后者提醒阶段间误差传递需要独立评估。
- **Historical domain shift:** 我可以把 age、archive period、photographic process 和 image degradation 写入生成条件，构造 controlled historical shifts；但 synthetic-to-archive gap 必须通过真实 later-period test set 验证。

#### Limitation

The intervention depends on a large domain-specific generative model and extensive synthetic data. Demographic control does not guarantee removal of hidden image artefacts, and performance across five institutions does not by itself prove robustness to future temporal shift.

#### Follow-up

I should test a small-budget version using existing image-generation or style-transfer models, comparing synthetic pretraining, naive mixing, and uniform age resampling under identical data budgets.

### Paper 3

Title: Beyond Procedure: Substantive Fairness in Conformal Prediction
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16794
Year / Venue: 2026 / arXiv preprint
Research function: Evaluation / Decision
Research line: uncertainty disparity and downstream decision fairness
Screening score: 23/25 = relevance 5 + novelty 5 + citation value 5 + feasibility 4 + long-term fit 4
Usefulness: decision-relevant uncertainty and evaluation reference

#### English Summary

- **Problem:** Conformal coverage can be statistically valid while the resulting prediction sets impose unequal downstream burdens. The paper distinguishes procedural fairness of the uncertainty method from substantive fairness of the full decision pipeline.
- **Method:** The authors derive an upper bound decomposing prediction-set size disparity and study label-clustered conformal prediction as a way to control method-driven unfairness. They also introduce an LLM-in-the-loop evaluator to approximate human assessment of downstream fairness across modalities.
- **Evaluation:** Conformal variants are compared using coverage, prediction-set size disparity, and substantive-fairness assessments across multiple modalities.
- **Findings:** Label-clustered conformal variants consistently improve substantive fairness. Equalized set size correlates more strongly with downstream fairness than equalized coverage.
- **Technical intuition:** Coverage measures whether the correct label is included, but set size determines how much ambiguity reaches the decision maker. Two groups can have equal coverage while one systematically receives larger, less actionable sets and therefore bears more uncertainty.

#### 中文解释

- **解决的问题:** 论文指出 conformal prediction 即使满足 coverage，也可能在 downstream decision 中产生不平等，因为不同 subgroup 可能收到大小差异很大的 prediction set。
- **为什么重要:** 可靠性不能只看 statistical guarantee，还要看 uncertainty 如何改变实际 decision burden。Equal coverage 不等于 equal usefulness。
- **技术直觉:** Prediction set 越大，后续判断越模糊、成本越高。如果 vulnerable subgroup 长期收到更宽的 set，那么形式上 coverage 公平，实质上仍然承担更多 uncertainty。
- **核心发现:** Label-clustered CP 的 substantive fairness 更好；set-size equality 比 coverage equality 更能解释 downstream fairness。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- **Aggregate metric masking:** Marginal coverage与 global MAE 一样，可能掩盖 subgroup uncertainty burden。我的评估应同时报告 coverage、interval width 和 subgroup decision cost。
- **Elderly-group degradation:** Elderly portraits可能得到更宽的 age interval，或者在相同宽度下 coverage 更低。两种情况都比单一 elderly MAE 更接近实际 reliability。
- **Balancing trade-offs:** Balancing 可能降低 elderly MAE，却扩大 interval width 或增加 abstention burden。论文提供了识别这种“点预测改善、uncertainty burden 恶化”的评估框架。
- **Cascade fragility:** Cascade 可以把 router uncertainty 传递到 age prediction。若只对最终输出做 pooled calibration，route-specific uncertainty 可能被平均掉；需要按 route 与 age group 分层检查 coverage 和 width。
- **Historical domain shift:** Source-calibrated conformal guarantee 在 later-period shift 下可能失效。我可以按 archive period 做 rolling calibration，并比较 pooled、age-conditioned 和 archive-conditioned interval 的稳定性。

#### Limitation

The abstract does not establish a distribution-shift guarantee. Its substantive-fairness assessment partly relies on an LLM evaluator, so agreement with domain experts and robustness of the evaluator require independent validation.

#### Follow-up

I should add subgroup coverage, interval width, and decision-weighted underestimation cost to the evaluation panel for historical age regression.

## 2. Emerging Trend

### English

Today's papers move subgroup reliability from a single-score audit to a pipeline question. Sensitive information can remain encoded even when aggregate performance is high; fairness interventions can help one representation and harm another; controlled data addition works differently depending on training order; and valid uncertainty coverage can still impose unequal decision burden. The emerging trend is **mechanism- and consequence-aware evaluation**: reliability should be tested at the representation, data-intervention, prediction, uncertainty, and downstream-decision levels.

### 中文

今天的趋势是把 subgroup reliability 从“报告一个 fairness score”推进到 **mechanism- and consequence-aware evaluation**。Aggregate performance 很高时，sensitive information 仍可能被编码；同一个 fairness intervention 可以帮助一个 architecture、伤害另一个；controlled data addition 的效果取决于训练顺序；即使 uncertainty coverage 合法，不同 subgroup 仍可能承担不同的 decision burden。可靠性需要同时检查 representation、data intervention、prediction、uncertainty 和 downstream consequence。

## 3. Two Research Questions

### Question 1

#### English

- **Research question:** Under archive-period shift, does age-and-archive-controlled synthetic pretraining reduce elderly-group MAE and worst archive-age cell error more reliably than uniform age resampling or naive real-synthetic mixing, and are gains associated with lower archive-source leakage in portrait embeddings?
- **Why it matters:** This directly tests whether my harmful balancing result arose from sample counts, training order, or representation-level shortcut preservation.
- **Possible data:** My historical portrait dataset, partitioned by archive source and period, plus a fixed-budget synthetic set conditioned on age bin, gender, image degradation, and archive style.
- **Possible method:** Compare four equal-budget pipelines: original data, uniform age resampling, naive real-synthetic mixing, and synthetic pretraining followed by real fine-tuning. Probe embeddings for age, gender, and archive source, then test whether probe accuracy predicts target-period subgroup error.
- **Evaluation metric:** Global and elderly MAE; elderly underestimation rate; 90th-percentile absolute error; worst archive-age cell MAE; calibration error; archive-source probe AUROC; and bootstrap confidence intervals.
- **Thesis connection:** The design jointly tests aggregate masking, elderly degradation, balancing trade-offs, cascade versus simple-regression stability, and historical domain shift.
- **Risk or limitation:** Synthetic portraits may introduce generator-specific artefacts or identity leakage. A small fixed budget, source-held-out evaluation, and real-only controls are necessary.

#### 中文

- **研究问题:** 在 archive-period shift 下，age-and-archive-controlled synthetic pretraining 是否比 uniform age resampling 或 naive real-synthetic mixing 更稳定地降低 elderly-group MAE 和 worst archive-age cell error？改进是否伴随 portrait embedding 中 archive-source leakage 的下降？
- **重要性:** 这个问题直接检验我的 harmful balancing result 来自 sample count、training order，还是 representation-level shortcut preservation。
- **可能数据:** 我的历史人像数据，按 archive source 和 period 划分；另构建固定预算、按 age bin、gender、image degradation 和 archive style 控制的 synthetic set。
- **可能方法:** 在相同预算下比较 original data、uniform age resampling、naive mixing 和 synthetic pretraining + real fine-tuning；同时 probe embedding 中的 age、gender 和 archive source，并检验 probe accuracy 是否预测 target-period subgroup error。
- **评估指标:** Global/elderly MAE、elderly underestimation rate、90th-percentile error、worst archive-age cell MAE、calibration error、archive-source probe AUROC 和 bootstrap confidence interval。
- **Thesis 连接:** 同时检验 aggregate masking、elderly degradation、balancing trade-off、cascade/simple regression stability 和 historical domain shift。
- **风险:** Synthetic portrait 可能引入 generator artefact 或 identity leakage，需要固定小预算、source-held-out evaluation 和 real-only control。

### Question 2

#### English

- **Research question:** Under later-period historical shift, can age- and archive-conditioned conformalized quantile regression reduce elderly undercoverage and interval-width disparity relative to pooled calibration while maintaining global interval efficiency?
- **Why it matters:** This converts hidden elderly error into a decision-relevant uncertainty problem and tests whether marginal coverage masks unequal ambiguity across subgroups.
- **Possible data:** Existing thesis predictions and residuals, with source-period calibration sets and later-period target sets stratified by age group and archive source.
- **Possible method:** Fit one simple quantile-regression model and compare pooled split conformal calibration with age-conditioned, archive-conditioned, and age-by-archive calibration. Use shrinkage or hierarchical pooling for sparse elderly cells.
- **Evaluation metric:** Marginal and subgroup coverage; mean and 90th-percentile interval width; worst-cell coverage gap; elderly underestimation cost; abstention rate; and interval-weighted decision loss.

## 4. Personal Reflection

### English

The most useful result today is not that one fairness method wins, but that intervention effects reverse across representations and training pipelines. The retinal study is almost a direct analogue of my thesis: a strong global score hides an age gap, and a plausible mitigation helps one architecture while harming two others. The synthetic-data paper shows that balancing is also procedural—pretraining then fine-tuning is materially different from mixing samples. The conformal paper adds the missing decision layer: even statistically valid uncertainty can burden one subgroup with wider, less actionable predictions. My research direction is becoming a study of where subgroup reliability is lost and how evaluation can detect whether an intervention merely moves the failure from error to uncertainty, or from one subgroup to another.

### 中文

今天最有价值的结论不是某个 fairness method 获胜，而是 intervention effect 会随 representation 和 training pipeline 反转。Retinal paper 几乎直接复现了我的 thesis 结构：global score 很强，但 age gap 被隐藏；一个看似合理的 mitigation 帮助一个 architecture，却伤害另外两个。Synthetic-data paper 进一步说明 balancing 也是 procedural 的——先 pretrain 再 fine-tune 与直接混合样本不是同一件事。Conformal paper 补上了 decision layer：即使 uncertainty guarantee 在统计上成立，某个 subgroup 仍可能承担更宽、更难行动的 prediction。我的研究方向正在变成：定位 subgroup reliability 在哪里丢失，并判断 intervention 是真正减少 failure，还是把 failure 从 error 移到 uncertainty，或从一个 subgroup 移到另一个。
