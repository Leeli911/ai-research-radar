# Skill: Paper Screening / 论文筛选

## Purpose / 目的

**English**

This skill defines how papers should be screened for my daily research radar. The goal is not to collect many papers, but to identify papers that strengthen my research identity around distribution shift, subgroup reliability, decision-relevant evaluation, and trustworthy ML.

**中文**

这个 skill 用来约束每日论文筛选。目标不是多收论文，而是筛出真正能帮助我发展 PhD 方向的论文，尤其是和 distribution shift、subgroup reliability、decision-relevant evaluation、trustworthy ML 相关的工作。

## Thesis Relevance Check / 与 thesis 的相关性检查

A paper is relevant if it helps explain, extend, or challenge at least one of my thesis findings:

- aggregate MAE can hide subgroup failure;
- balancing sometimes worsened elderly-group performance;
- cascaded conditional models were fragile under shift;
- simpler regression models generalized better;
- residual and calibration diagnostics revealed hidden failure patterns.

中文:

一篇论文只有在能解释、扩展或挑战我的 thesis 发现时，才算真正相关。尤其要看它是否能帮助我理解 aggregate metrics、subgroup failure、balancing trade-offs、cascade fragility 和 historical domain shift。

## Scoring Rubric /25

Score each paper out of 25.

### 1. Conceptual Relevance /5

- 5: Directly addresses distribution shift, subgroup reliability, fairness degradation, or monitoring.
- 3: Related to trustworthy ML but not directly about subgroup degradation.
- 1: General ML relevance only.
- 0: Mostly hype or unrelated.

### 2. Methodological Novelty /5

- 5: Offers a clear diagnostic, monitoring, evaluation, or correction method.
- 3: Uses known methods in a useful setting.
- 1: Mostly empirical observation without method.
- 0: No technical method.

### 3. Proposal Citation Value /5

- 5: Can support a PhD proposal paragraph.
- 3: Useful background citation.
- 1: Maybe useful later.
- 0: Not worth citing.

### 4. Feasibility for My Background /5

- 5: Builds naturally on my CV/thesis skills.
- 3: Feasible with some learning.
- 1: Requires a major shift into unfamiliar systems or theory.
- 0: Not feasible for my profile.

### 5. Strategic Long-Term Fit /5

- 5: Strengthens my long-term identity as a researcher in subgroup reliability under shift.
- 3: Some strategic fit.
- 1: Interesting but peripheral.
- 0: Distracts from my core direction.

## Decision Rule / 决策规则

**English**

- 21-25: Must include; consider a paper card.
- 16-20: Include if today's theme matches.
- 11-15: Mention only if it fills a specific gap.
- 0-10: Reject.

**中文**

- 21-25 分: 必选，考虑做 paper card。
- 16-20 分: 如果和当天主题匹配，可以选。
- 11-15 分: 只有在补充特定空白时才提。
- 0-10 分: 拒绝。

## Reject Papers That Are / 拒绝标准

Reject papers that are:

- generic AI hype;
- product or startup news;
- too systems-heavy for my research direction;
- disconnected from trustworthy ML;
- only about architecture novelty without evaluation insight;
- only about benchmark gains without subgroup, shift, or reliability analysis;
- too far from my thesis grounding.

中文:

如果论文只是追热点、堆模型、做产品发布、系统工程太重、或者只有 architecture novelty 而没有 evaluation insight，就不要选。我的 radar 应该服务 research identity，而不是追 AI 新闻。
