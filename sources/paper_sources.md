# Paper Sources

Use this file to guide daily paper collection. Prefer recent papers, but do not include a paper unless it has a clear connection to the research lines.

## arXiv Categories

Check recent submissions first:

- cs.LG: https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/recent
- cs.CV: https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CV/recent
- cs.AI: https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/recent
- cs.CL: https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CL/recent
- stat.ML: https://arxiv.org/list/stat.ML/recent

Useful arXiv search queries:

- distribution shift
- out-of-distribution generalization
- dataset shift
- subgroup robustness
- fairness under distribution shift
- uncertainty estimation
- model monitoring
- model auditing
- data-centric AI
- representation robustness
- multimodal misinformation
- AI-generated image detection
- provenance detection

## Daily Paper Selection Checklist

For each candidate paper, answer:

- Does it study a real reliability, shift, fairness, governance, or authenticity problem?
- Is the method clear enough to explain in 3 to 5 sentences?
- Does it use datasets or evaluations that could be reused or criticized?
- Does it reveal a limitation or open problem?
- Can it connect to facial age estimation under historical domain shift?
- Could it support a PhD proposal paragraph or interview answer?

## Relevance Scoring

Use this quick score before adding a paper:

- 3: Directly supports one of my PhD research lines.
- 2: Indirectly relevant, but useful for methods, data, or evaluation.
- 1: Interesting but probably too far from my current direction.
- 0: Popular but not useful for my research plan.

Only include score 2 or 3 papers in daily notes.
