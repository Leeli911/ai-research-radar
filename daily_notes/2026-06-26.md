## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: "Who experiences large model decay and why?" A Hierarchical Framework for Diagnosing Heterogeneous Performance Drift
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00756
Year / Venue: 2025, ICML 2025 / arXiv
Source: arXiv official abstract and experimental HTML
Research line: subgroup-specific performance drift diagnosis under source-to-target shift
Research function: Diagnosis / Explanation
Screening Score: 25/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor

#### English Summary

- Problem: deployed ML models can lose performance unevenly. Average decay may look small while a clinically or socially meaningful subgroup experiences a large drop.
- Method: the paper proposes SHIFT, a subgroup-scanning hierarchical inference framework. It first tests whether covariate or outcome shift causes large decay in any sufficiently large subgroup, then tests whether variable-subset-specific shifts explain the decay.
- Evaluation: the authors validate the framework with simulations, health-insurance prediction across states, readmission prediction across hospitals, and an extension to high-dimensional text embeddings.
- Findings: SHIFT identifies affected subgroups and separates where performance decay happens from how it happens. In real-world cases, targeted updates guided by SHIFT improved affected subgroups better than broad non-targeted updates.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 deployment shift 后模型性能不均匀下降的问题。整体 accuracy 只下降一点，并不代表每个 subgroup 都安全。
- 为什么重要: 它把 hidden reliability degradation 变成一个可检验的问题: 是否存在一个足够大的 subgroup，因为 covariate shift 或 outcome shift 出现了不可接受的 performance decay？
- 技术直觉: SHIFT 不是只报告 subgroup accuracy，而是先问 "where" - 哪个 subgroup 被伤害，再问 "how" - 哪些变量或变量子集解释了这种下降。这个结构很适合把监控信号连接到后续 intervention。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: SHIFT 的起点正是 overall performance decay 不足以解释 subgroup harm。这和我的 thesis 中 global MAE 掩盖 elderly-group error 的现象直接一致。
- Elderly-group degradation: 我的 elderly-group underestimation 可以被重新表述为一个 subgroup-specific decay problem: historical domain shift 是否让 elderly portraits 的 conditional loss 超过可接受阈值？
- Balancing trade-offs: 这篇论文提醒我，干预不应该先假设 balancing 是正确答案。需要先判断是 covariate shift、outcome shift，还是某个变量子集导致 elderly performance decay。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded gender-conditional model 的失败可以被看成 routing 后的 subgroup-specific decay。SHIFT 的 "where/how" 分解能帮助我检查是 gender routing、age representation，还是历史图像特征造成误差传播。
- Historical domain shift: source-to-target framing可以自然映射到 modern face training data 和 historical portrait data。我的研究可以把历史时期、拍摄风格、图像质量和年龄组当作候选 shift variables。

#### Limitation

The detailed tests are most natural for structured or concept-level variables. For historical portraits, I would need interpretable visual concepts or representation summaries before the full "how" layer becomes usable.

#### Follow-up

Read the full method carefully and test whether a simplified SHIFT-style diagnostic can be designed for age-estimation residuals using age group, gender, image quality, era, and embedding-cluster variables.

### Paper 2

Title: Subgroups Matter for Robust Bias Mitigation
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21363
Year / Venue: 2025, ICML 2025 / arXiv
Source: arXiv official abstract and experimental HTML
Research line: subgroup definition as a control lever for robust bias mitigation
Research function: Explanation / Intervention
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference with high strategic value

#### English Summary

- Problem: bias mitigation methods often fail inconsistently, and it is unclear when reweighting, resampling, or subgroup-conditioned architectures help rather than hurt.
- Method: the paper systematically varies subgroup definitions, including coarse, fine-grained, intersectional, noisy, and random subgroupings, while applying mitigation methods such as gDRO, resampling, Domain Independent classifiers, and CFair.
- Evaluation: experiments span multiple vision and language tasks, with performance measured by AUC, worst-group accuracy, and subgroup accuracy gaps under biased-train to unbiased-test generalization.
- Findings: subgroup choice can make mitigation succeed or fail. Some groupings produce worse outcomes than no mitigation, and the subgroup that should be used for mitigation may differ from the subgroup used for fairness evaluation.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文处理的是 bias mitigation 为什么有时无效甚至反效果。它指出问题不只在方法本身，也在于我选择了什么 subgroup 来做 intervention。
- 为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 中 balancing sometimes worsened elderly-group performance 非常接近。一个 observed disparity 不等于这个 subgroup 就是正确的 mitigation unit。
- 技术直觉: 如果真正导致 error 的是 spurious correlation 或 data-generating mechanism，那么按表面 demographic subgroup 做 balancing 可能不能修复因果机制，甚至会让模型更依赖错误特征。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 论文同时看 overall AUC、worst-group accuracy 和 gap，说明整体性能和 subgroup fairness 可以朝不同方向变化。
- Elderly-group degradation: 我的 elderly group 可能只是表现出失败的 evaluation subgroup，而不一定是最合适的 mitigation subgroup。真正要修的可能是 image quality、era、pose、gender-age interaction 或 annotation pattern。
- Balancing trade-offs: 这是最直接的连接。论文显示某些 subgroup groupings 会比 ERM 更差，为我 thesis 中 balancing 伤害 elderly group 提供了机制解释。
- Cascade fragility: Domain Independent 这类 subgroup-specific heads 和我的 cascaded conditional model 有结构相似性。它提醒我，按错误 subgroup 路由或建 head 可能会把 disparity 固化进模型结构。
- Historical domain shift: 历史人像的 shift 可能不是单一年龄差异，而是年龄、时代、画质和社会采样机制的组合。subgroup definition 本身需要作为研究对象，而不是预先固定。

#### Limitation

The experiments focus heavily on controlled spurious-correlation settings. In my historical portrait setting, the true source of subgroup degradation may be harder to annotate and may require slice discovery or concept extraction first.

#### Follow-up

Use this paper to design a small experimental grid: compare balancing by age group, by gender-age intersection, by image-quality strata, and by embedding-discovered historical slices.

### Paper 3

Title: Detecting and Monitoring Bias for Subgroups in Breast Cancer Detection AI
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10562
Year / Venue: 2025, arXiv
Source: arXiv official abstract and full-text HTML
Research line: subgroup bias analysis and drift monitoring for medical image classifiers
Research function: Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference

#### English Summary

- Problem: high-performing breast cancer detection models can still underperform for demographic or clinical subgroups after deployment, especially as patient mix, scanners, sites, or workflows change.
- Method: the paper evaluates mammography models across subgroups defined by age, race, tissue density, view position, scanner/site attributes, and then applies CUSUM-style statistical process control to monitor subgroup performance over time.
- Evaluation: experiments use RSNA 2022 and EMBED mammography data, with metrics including PPV, sensitivity, F1, specificity, AUROC, uncertainty, statistical tests for subgroup differences, false alarm rate, and detection delay.
- Findings: the models achieve strong overall AUROC, but subgroup analysis reveals lower sensitivity or PPV in specific age, racial, density, and imaging subgroups. CUSUM monitoring can flag performance drops as local distributions shift.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文关注的是 medical imaging model 在真实部署后是否对所有 subgroup 都可靠。整体 AUROC 很高，不代表某些 age/race/density subgroup 的 sensitivity 足够好。
- 为什么重要: 它把 subgroup evaluation 和 post-deployment monitoring 连接起来，强调 reliability 不是一次性测试，而是需要持续追踪的动态状态。
- 技术直觉: 先用 subgroup metrics 找出 vulnerable groups，再用 CUSUM control chart 监控这些 subgroup 的 metric drift。如果 local population、scanner 或 workflow 变化导致性能下降，系统可以更早报警。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 论文中的 EMBED/RSNA 模型整体 AUROC 很强，但 subgroup PPV 和 sensitivity 仍然不均衡。这和我的 global MAE 掩盖 elderly-group degradation 是同一类问题。
- Elderly-group degradation: 它直接包含 age-group analysis，并观察到某些年龄段 sensitivity 下降。这加强了我把 elderly performance 当作 deployment reliability issue 而不只是模型误差的想法。
- Balancing trade-offs: 论文提醒我，低样本 subgroup 的问题不能只靠 balancing 解决。少数 subgroup 的 sensitivity estimate 本身可能不稳定，盲目 retraining 可能带来错误安全感。
- Cascade fragility: 医疗模型中 thresholding、recalibration、workflow alert 和临床后续决策形成隐性 cascade。我的 gender-conditional age cascade 也需要类似地检查每个阶段是否放大 subgroup error。
- Historical domain shift: scanner/site/workflow shift 对应到历史人像中的 camera type、portrait style、archival quality 和 era shift。CUSUM-style monitoring 可被改写成按时间段或档案来源追踪 age-estimation residual drift。

#### Limitation

The paper is application-specific to breast cancer detection, and CUSUM monitoring still depends on the availability of timely labels or reliable proxy metrics. Historical portrait datasets may not provide streaming labels.

#### Follow-up

Adapt the monitoring idea conceptually: track elderly-group residual mean, residual variance, calibration error, and uncertainty over historical era or archive source, then test which signal detects degradation before global MAE changes.

- Evaluation metric: elderly-group MAE, worst-group MAE, residual bias, residual variance, calibration error, and subgroup-level performance-decay magnitude.
- Thesis connection: directly extends aggregate metric masking, elderly-group degradation, cascade fragility, and historical domain shift into a formal diagnostic framework.

中文:

研究问题: 在 historical portrait domain shift 下，能否用类似 SHIFT 的诊断框架，把 elderly-group age-estimation degradation 分解为 visual covariate shift、annotation/outcome shift 和 subgroup-definition mismatch？

这个问题重要，因为我的 thesis 已经发现 elderly group error，但还没有明确解释机制。把发现推进到 mechanism diagnosis，会更接近 PhD proposal 的核心问题。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: When does balancing by observed age group improve historical face age estimation, and when is balancing by alternative subgroups such as era, image quality, gender-age intersection, or embedding-discovered slice more reliable?
- Why it matters: my thesis found that balancing sometimes worsened elderly performance, so the key question is not whether to balance, but what subgroup definition should guide balancing.
- Possible data: my thesis splits plus generated or annotated subgroup variables for image quality, historical era, gender-age intersections, and representation clusters.
- Possible method: compare naive age balancing, intersectional balancing, slice-based balancing, and no balancing across the same model family; evaluate both average and subgroup metrics.
- Evaluation metric: global MAE, elderly MAE, worst-slice MAE, residual calibration, model-rank stability, and degradation transfer to non-elderly groups.
- Thesis connection: directly tests balancing trade-offs and checks whether my cascaded conditional model failed because the chosen routing subgroup did not match the true failure mechanism.
- Risk or limitation: discovered slices may be hard to interpret, and overfitting subgroup definitions could create an evaluation protocol that is too tailored to one dataset.

中文:

研究问题: 在 historical face age estimation 中，什么时候按 observed age group balancing 有帮助，什么时候按 era、image quality、gender-age intersection 或 embedding-discovered slice balancing 更可靠？

这个问题把我的 thesis 中 "balancing 有时伤害 elderly group" 变成一个可测试的 subgroup-definition 问题。重点不是是否 balancing，而是用什么 subgroup definition 来指导 balancing。
