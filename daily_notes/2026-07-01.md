## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: When the Past Misleads: Training Data Selection to Improve Fairness under Temporal Distribution Shifts
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01060
Year / Venue: 2025, arXiv / AIES 2025
Source: official arXiv metadata
Research line: temporal distribution shift and fairness-aware historical data selection
Research function: Diagnosis / Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor and paper-card candidate

#### English Summary

- Problem: training data usually expands over time, but older data can mislead models under temporal distribution shift. The paper asks how to choose historical training windows when different sociodemographic groups experience different shift patterns.
- Method: the paper develops a temporal training-data selection method. It estimates the utility of multiple historical datasets through an optimization problem and selects training data to improve fairness across groups rather than simply using all past data.
- Evaluation: the method is evaluated on COMPAS and ACSIncome, two temporally structured datasets often used for fairness and decision-model evaluation.
- Findings: using all historical data is not always best. The paper reports improved error-rate parity compared with training on all past data, while also preserving or slightly improving overall accuracy. The main message is that more historical data can worsen fairness when the past is not representative of the present.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 temporal distribution shift 下如何选择训练数据。它不是默认认为历史数据越多越好，而是问哪些历史窗口对当前 subgroup fairness 有帮助，哪些历史窗口会误导模型。
- 为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 很接近。historical portraits 本身就是时间结构数据，不同时代、archive source 和 subgroup 的视觉分布都可能变化。把所有历史样本混在一起训练，可能让 overall MAE 看起来稳定，但让 elderly group 或某些 era/source 的误差变差。
- 技术直觉: 论文把历史数据当成多个候选来源，为每个来源估计对当前公平性的 utility。这个思路比 naive data accumulation 更细，也比简单 balancing 更贴近 deployment shift。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 这篇论文说明，整体 accuracy 或 error 可能掩盖 temporal fairness degradation。我的 global MAE 同样可能掩盖 elderly group 在某些 historical windows 下的退化。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits 可以被视为一个在时间上更脆弱的 subgroup。如果某些时代的 elderly face representation 更稀疏或图像质量更差，全量历史训练可能无法保护这个 subgroup。
- Balancing trade-offs: 我的 thesis 中 balancing 有时 worsened elderly-group performance。这个 paper 提供了一种解释: 干预不只取决于 group ratio，还取决于历史数据窗口是否和当前 subgroup distribution 对齐。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded conditional models 依赖稳定的条件路径。如果 gender 或 age-group 分布随时间变化，旧数据训练出的 routing logic 可能在当前窗口误导 downstream age estimation。
- Historical domain shift: 这篇论文直接强化了我的 historical domain shift 方向。它把 "past data" 的使用转化为可优化、可评估的问题，而不是默认把历史样本当成同分布训练资源。

#### Limitation

The experiments use tabular fairness datasets, not visual age estimation. I would need to translate temporal data selection into archive-source, era, and image-quality windows for historical portraits.

#### Follow-up

Test whether source/era-aware training-window selection reduces elderly MAE and worst-group MAE compared with training on all available historical portraits.

### Paper 2

Title: Transfer Learning under Group-Label Shift
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22268
Year / Venue: 2025, arXiv
Source: official arXiv metadata
Research line: target-domain transfer when group-label composition changes
Research function: Explanation / Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for subgroup reliability under shift

#### English Summary

- Problem: many transfer-learning settings face group-label shift: the joint distribution of subgroup membership and class label changes between source and target domains, even if class-conditional feature distributions are assumed stable. This captures subpopulation imbalance and spurious-correlation shifts.
- Method: the paper proposes a semiparametric framework using labeled source data plus unlabeled target data with observed group labels. It derives a source-outcome-weighted conditional likelihood estimator for logistic regression and provides inference tools such as confidence intervals and hypothesis tests.
- Evaluation: the method is tested in synthetic experiments and benchmark examples including Waterbirds-style spurious-correlation settings.
- Findings: explicitly modeling group-label shift improves transfer estimation and gives a statistical way to reason about target-domain subgroup composition, rather than assuming the source group-label mix remains valid.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文关注的是 target domain 里 group 和 label 的 joint distribution 变了怎么办。比如某个 subgroup 在 target domain 中比例、标签结构或 spurious correlation 都和 source domain 不一样。
- 为什么重要: 我的 thesis 里的 historical portraits 很可能存在 group-label shift。不同历史时期的 age distribution、gender distribution、portrait availability 和 image quality 都会一起变化。source validation performance 不能直接代表 target archive reliability。
- 技术直觉: 论文没有只做黑箱 domain adaptation，而是把 group-label composition 的变化显式建模。这个思路可以帮助我把 "balancing 失败" 重新理解为 source-target subgroup composition mismatch。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 如果 source 和 target 的 group-label mix 不同，global MAE 可以保持类似，但 target elderly subgroup 的 error distribution 可能完全不同。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly group 在 historical portraits 中不仅样本少，还可能和 gender、era、image quality 共同发生 composition shift。group-label shift 是描述这种复合变化的合适概念。
- Balancing trade-offs: naive balancing 只调整 source sample weights，未必匹配 target group-label structure。我的 balancing trade-off 可以被解释为错误地修正了 source imbalance，却没有修正 target shift。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded gender-conditional model 会放大 group-label shift。如果 gender prediction 或 subgroup routing 在 target distribution 下偏移，后续 age regression 会继承这个偏移。
- Historical domain shift: 这个方法可以自然转化为 archive-source or era shift: source labels from one historical period may not transfer when the target period has a different age-gender-source composition.

#### Limitation

The statistical assumptions are cleaner than my portrait setting. Historical face features may also shift conditionally, so group-label shift alone may not explain all visual degradation.

#### Follow-up

Estimate whether my thesis data shows group-label composition change across archive source or era, then compare naive balancing with target-aware reweighting for elderly and intersectional reliability.

### Paper 3

Title: Validity in Machine Learning for Extreme Event Attribution
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19039
Year / Venue: 2025, arXiv
Source: official arXiv metadata
Research line: decision-valid evaluation metrics under distribution shift
Research function: Diagnosis / Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 21/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: citation value for decision-relevant evaluation

#### English Summary

- Problem: machine-learning models for extreme event attribution can look strong under common metrics such as mean squared error or negative log likelihood, while still being invalid for the downstream attribution question.
- Method: the paper formalizes validity for ML-based attribution and introduces attribution-error measures. It also uses subgroup and propensity diagnostics to reveal invalid model behavior that standard aggregate metrics miss.
- Evaluation: the authors evaluate models using WeatherBench-style data, comparisons with climate-model evidence, and perfect-model experiments.
- Findings: standard ML metrics do not reliably correlate with attribution validity and can select models that are decision-invalid. Subgroup and propensity diagnostics are more informative for identifying when a model should not be trusted for the intended scientific decision.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 evaluation metric 是否真的服务 downstream decision。模型在 MSE 或 likelihood 上好，不代表它对 extreme event attribution 这个决策问题有效。
- 为什么重要: 我的 thesis 也不是只需要一个低 MAE 模型。如果 age estimation 被用于 historical demographic reconstruction，那么 aggregate MAE 低并不代表 elderly group、稀有时代或低质量图像的推断足够可信。
- 技术直觉: 论文把 "validity" 和普通 predictive accuracy 分开。它提醒我，evaluation metric 必须和实际研究用途对齐，否则模型选择会偏向平均表现好、但关键 subgroup 不可靠的模型。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 这篇论文最直接的贡献是说明 common metrics can select invalid models。我的 global MAE 也可能选择一个对 elderly demographic reconstruction 不可靠的模型。
- Elderly-group degradation: subgroup diagnostics 可以对应到 elderly group residual skew、underestimation rate、calibration error 和 high-error tail risk。
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing 是否有效不应只看 global MAE 或 overall calibration，而要看它是否改善 decision-valid subgroup diagnostics。如果 balancing 降低平均误差但增加 elderly underestimation，它对我的研究目的就是无效的。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded models 可能在普通误差指标上可接受，但如果 routing errors concentrate in elderly or low-quality historical portraits，它们在 decision-validity 层面仍然失败。
- Historical domain shift: historical portrait evaluation也需要 validity framing: source/era/image-quality shift 下的模型不仅要预测年龄，还要支持可靠的历史人口结构分析。

#### Limitation

The domain is climate attribution, not computer vision. Its main value for my research is conceptual and evaluative: it gives a rigorous argument for why decision-specific diagnostics matter.

#### Follow-up

Define decision-validity diagnostics for historical age estimation: elderly underestimation rate, worst-era MAE, residual skew by archive source, and calibration error for age intervals used in demographic reconstruction.

English:

- Research question: Under archive-source or era shift, can temporal training-window selection reduce elderly-group MAE more reliably than using all historical portrait data?

- Thesis connection: directly tests aggregate metric masking, elderly-group degradation, balancing trade-offs, and historical domain shift.

中文:

研究问题: 在 archive-source 或 era shift 下，temporal training-window selection 是否比使用全部 historical portrait data 更稳定地降低 elderly-group MAE？

这个问题直接延伸我的 thesis。实验可以比较 all-data training、recent-window training、weighted historical windows 和 naive balancing，看哪一种更能保护 elderly group，而不是只让 global MAE 好看。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: Can group-label-shift diagnostics explain when age/gender balancing worsens elderly reliability in historical facial age estimation?
- Why it matters: my thesis found that balancing sometimes hurt elderly performance. Group-label shift offers a testable explanation: balancing may correct source imbalance while mismatching the target archive's age-gender-era composition.
- Possible data: my thesis models, portrait embeddings, age/gender labels, archive source, era metadata, and subgroup residuals.
- Possible method: estimate group-label distributions across source and target splits; compare source balancing, target-aware reweighting, and simple regression baselines; inspect whether changes in group-label composition predict elderly residual variance.
- Evaluation metric: target elderly MAE, worst-intersectional-group MAE, calibration error, group-label shift distance, residual variance, and confidence intervals for subgroup performance gaps.
- Thesis connection: directly tests balancing trade-offs, cascade fragility, subgroup degradation, and historical domain shift.
- Risk or limitation: if conditional visual features also shift strongly, group-label shift diagnostics alone may under-explain the failure.

中文:

研究问题: group-label-shift diagnostics 能否解释为什么 age/gender balancing 有时会 worsens elderly reliability？

这个问题把我的 balancing 结果变成一个可检验机制: balancing 可能修正了 source imbalance，但没有匹配 target archive 的 age-gender-era composition。它适合用我的 existing models、embeddings 和 subgroup residuals 做实验。
