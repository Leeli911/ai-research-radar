## Paper 1 — Population-Level Decision Curve Analysis May Mislead the Evaluation of Prediction Model Usefulness under Subgroup Utility Heterogeneity

**Authors:** Junfeng Wang, Kim Zhipei Wang, Ben Van Calster, Nan van Geloven, Ewout Steyerberg, Laure Wynants.  
**Date/status:** 2026-09-28; arXiv preprint.  
**Research function:** Evaluation → Diagnosis.  
**Source:** [Primary arXiv record and abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34521).

### English Summary

- **Problem:** Population net benefit may misrepresent utility when subgroup utility scales differ.
- **Method:** Derive conditions where net benefit and utility rank strategies differently, with a robustness framework.

- **Findings:** Opposing subgroup net-benefit differences flag possible inconsistency; heterogeneous true-positive versus false-negative utility increments matter.

## Paper 2 — Guided Uncertainty-Aware Robust Domain Transfer

**Authors:** Xin Xiong, Zijian Guo, Tianxi Cai.  
**Date/status:** 2026-09-28; arXiv preprint.  
**Research function:** Intervention → Evaluation.  
**Sources:** [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35654); [method and assumptions](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35654v1).

### English Summary

- **Problem:** Updating deployed models with scarce target labels and heterogeneous sources.
- **Method:** GUARD combines target-anchored linear updates, cross-fitting, and uncertainty-aware source mixing.
- **Evaluation:** Simulations and longitudinal rheumatoid-arthritis EHR prediction.
- **Findings:** Authors report more stable future prediction than target-only and standard transfer alternatives in studied settings.

### 中文解释

GUARD 借助多来源数据和少量目标标签更新已有模型，同时考虑来源估计的不确定性。直觉是“来源多”不等于“信息可靠”。作者用模拟与纵向 EHR 任务评估；其结果不能直接转化为老年组保证。

**Verified boundary:** The future-mixture formulation holds the current target covariate law fixed while mixing conditional outcome mechanisms. Inference additionally requires overlap, sampling, and nuisance-estimation conditions. This is not protection against arbitrary future shift. [Sections 2 and 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35654v1)

### Connection to My Thesis

**Explicit Conceptual Bridge — my interpretation:** My balancing results suggest that sample counts alone do not establish transfer value. I can investigate whether archive compatibility and uncertainty explain why additional weight sometimes harms elderly predictions.

| Thesis observation | My proposed connection, not a result of this paper |

## Two Research Questions / 两个研究问题

### Question 1 — Does a smaller age-group gap hide cumulative elderly harm?

- **Research question:** In historical age-estimation replay, can subgroup-gap-triggered updates reduce cumulative age-group MAE disparity while increasing cumulative elderly MAE relative to a frozen regressor?
- **Why it matters:** I need to distinguish reduced disparity from actual improvement for elderly portraits.
- **Possible data:** My thesis portraits, person IDs, age labels, archive/date metadata, frozen embeddings, and saved model outputs; feasibility depends on access and enough elderly examples.
- **Possible method:** Use person-disjoint development and replay sets. Compare frozen, scheduled, global-loss-triggered, and age-gap-triggered ridge-head updates on identical streams. Fix windows, thresholds, label delay, and training history before testing. Calibrate triggers only on development data; report update-count distributions and a budget-matched scheduled comparator. Cross balanced/unbalanced training as a separate factor.
- **Evaluation metric:** Primary: cumulative elderly absolute error per elderly observation. Secondary: equal-window mean elderly MAE, sum of absolute age-group MAE gaps, overall MAE, signed elderly residual, and update cost. Report paired differences with uncertainty across independent replay repetitions; do not treat correlated windows as independent samples.
- **Thesis connection:** This operationalizes aggregate masking, elderly degradation, balancing backfire, and the stability advantage of simple regression. A cascade is a secondary model comparison.
- **Risk or limitation:** Sparse groups, noisy ages, and arbitrary source ordering can dominate results. I will report group counts and distinguish chronological evidence from constructed drift. I will not select a policy using final test trajectories.

**中文：** 我的问题是：按年龄组差距触发更新，是否会缩小累计差距，却增加相对冻结回归模型的累计老年组误差？我用人物隔离的数据、统一到达顺序和标签延迟，比较冻结、定期、整体误差触发与组差距触发更新，平衡训练单独作为实验因素。主要指标是每个老年样本承担的累计绝对误差，同时报告窗口平均、组间差距、残差方向和更新次数。阈值只在开发集确定，预算与不确定性一起报告。样本稀少、年龄噪声与人工排序是主要限制；这直接延伸我的 masking、balancing 和简单模型稳定性发现。

### Question 2 — When does MAE-based model selection change under asymmetric age errors?

- **Research question:** Across held-out archives, how often does the global-MAE winner among simple, balanced, and cascaded models lose under an explicit cost for misassigning elderly portraits below the thesis age threshold?
- **Why it matters:** I want decision-relevant evaluation without inventing a universal utility for historical demography.
- **Possible data:** Existing held-out predictions and age labels, archive identifiers, thesis age threshold, and uncertainty flags for estimated ages.
- **Possible method:** Predefine a cost grid on development data: one unit for false elderly assignment and 1, 2, or 5 units for missed elderly assignment. These are sensitivity scenarios, not elicited real-world utilities. Compute population-weighted and equal-archive-weighted loss on untouched tests; show each group's error contributions. Bootstrap persons within archives and repeat after excluding uncertain labels.
- **Evaluation metric:** Model-ranking reversals, elderly false-negative rate, non-elderly false-positive rate, scenario loss, global/elderly MAE, and error in the reconstructed elderly population share.
- **Thesis connection:** I test whether elderly underestimation and cascade fragility change model choice despite good aggregate MAE, and whether balancing remains preferable after historical composition changes.
- **Risk or limitation:** Utility weights are normative choices, not facts inferable from model errors. I will present a sensitivity map rather than a single best policy. This adapts the evaluation question from Paper 1; it is not a clinical DCA replication.

**中文：** 我的问题是：在留出档案中，按整体 MAE 选出的模型，是否会在“漏识别老年人”的成本提高后失去优势？我使用已有预测与 thesis 年龄阈值，比较简单、平衡和级联模型；把漏识别成本设为 1、2、5，误识别成本设为 1，仅作为预先设定的敏感性场景。分别按实际人群和等档案权重评估，报告排序反转、两类错误率、老年人口比例偏差及人物级 bootstrap 区间。这个设计连接老年低估、平衡取舍与历史构成变化，但不能从误差数据推导“正确”效用，也不是临床决策曲线的直接复现。
