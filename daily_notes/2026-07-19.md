## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Rethinking external validation for the target population: Capturing patient-level similarity with a generative model
Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.11284
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint
Source: official arXiv PDF, verified 2026-07-19
Research line: external validation, target-population similarity, distribution shift, clinical model reliability
Research function: Diagnosis / Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 25/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor and Recommended for paper card

#### English Summary

- Problem: standard external validation often reports average target-cohort performance, but this can miss whether the development data actually contain patients similar to the target population and which target patients are least supported by the original training distribution.
- Method: the paper uses a generative model to capture patient-level similarity between development and target populations. Instead of treating external validation as one target-cohort score, it stratifies the target population by similarity to the source data.
- Evaluation: the paper evaluates predictive performance and calibration across similarity-defined patient strata, asking whether patients who are less represented by development data show different reliability patterns.
- Findings: the key finding is that external validation should not only ask whether a model transfers on average. It should identify target-population regions where the model is less supported by source data and where subgroup reliability may degrade.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: external validation 不能只看一个 target cohort 的平均表现。即使整体性能可以接受，target population 中某些 patients 可能和 development data 很不相似，因此模型对这些人群的预测缺少数据支持。

为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 中 historical domain shift 很接近。我的 historical portraits 也不是一个均匀 target domain。某些 elderly portraits 可能因为年代、图像质量、服饰、拍摄风格或 archive source 与训练分布更远，所以整体 MAE 不能说明这些样本是否可靠。

技术直觉: 论文的核心直觉是把 external validation 拆成 patient-level similarity 问题。对我的研究来说，可以把 patient similarity 改成 portrait similarity: 用 embedding、image-quality proxy、source/period metadata 或 reconstruction error 定义哪些 historical portraits 更接近训练分布，哪些是低支持区域。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 这篇论文直接支持我把 global validation MAE 看成不充分指标。整体 target-domain performance 可能掩盖 low-similarity target strata 的高误差。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly group 的失败可能集中在 source-support 较低的 elderly samples，而不是所有 elderly portraits 上均匀发生。similarity-defined strata 可以帮助我解释 elderly degradation 的内部结构。
- Balancing trade-offs: 如果 balancing 增加了样本数量但没有增加与 elderly target samples 相似的支持数据，balancing 仍然可能 worsened elderly performance。论文把这个问题重新表述为 support mismatch。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded gender-conditional model 可能在 low-similarity regions 中出现 routing or calibration instability。相似度分层可以检查 cascade failure 是否集中在某些 target-support 较弱的样本。
- Historical domain shift: 这是最强连接。historical portraits 可以被看作一个 target population，外部验证应检查 source-to-target similarity，而不只是报告 held-out MAE。

#### Limitation

The paper is grounded in clinical prediction and patient-level tabular structure, not visual age regression. I need to translate patient-level similarity into portrait-level support using visual embeddings, source metadata, image-quality measures, or reconstruction-based distance.

#### Follow-up

Design a similarity-stratified validation panel for thesis models: group historical portraits by source-support score, then compare global MAE, elderly MAE, residual skew, calibration error, and underestimation rate across similarity strata.

### Paper 2

Title: Differential Subgroup Discovery: Characterizing Where Two Populations Differ, and Why
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27741
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint
Source: official arXiv record, verified 2026-07-19
Research line: subgroup discovery, population comparison, distribution shift explanation, interpretable diagnostics
Research function: Diagnosis / Explanation / Evaluation
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for hidden reliability-unit discovery

#### English Summary

- Problem: distribution shift is often summarized globally, but researchers need to know where two populations differ and which subgroup descriptions explain the difference.
- Method: the paper proposes differential subgroup discovery, a framework for finding interpretable subgroups that characterize population differences. The goal is not only to detect shift, but to describe the subpopulations where shift is concentrated.
- Evaluation: the method is tested as a population-comparison tool, emphasizing subgroup descriptions that are both statistically meaningful and interpretable.
- Findings: the paper gives a technical route from "the distributions differ" to "these specific subgroups explain the difference." That makes it useful for diagnosing hidden reliability degradation before treating shift as one global covariate-change number.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: 只说 source population 和 target population 不同是不够的。我需要知道它们在哪里不同，哪些 subgroup 贡献了这种差异，以及这些差异是否和模型误差相关。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 中 elderly-group degradation 可能不是单纯的 age effect，而是 elderly + period + image quality + gender route + archive source 的组合。Differential Subgroup Discovery 可以帮助我把 historical domain shift 拆成可解释的 subgroup difference。

技术直觉: 论文不是只计算一个 global distribution distance，而是搜索能解释 population difference 的 subgroup rules。对我的研究来说，这些 rules 可以成为 reliability units: 如果某个 rule 同时解释 source-target difference 和 high residual risk，它就是一个值得监控的 hidden subgroup。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: global shift metrics 和 global MAE 都可能隐藏局部差异。这个方法帮助我定位被平均掉的 subgroup-level distribution changes。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly degradation 可以被进一步拆成 differential subgroups，例如 elderly + low-resolution + late-period source。这样我不会把 elderly group 当成内部均匀群体。
- Balancing trade-offs: naive balancing 可能只平衡 age counts，却没有平衡真正发生 population difference 的 subgroup rules。论文支持我解释为什么粗粒度 balancing 可能制造新的 trade-off。
- Cascade fragility: cascade model 的 fragility 可能来自某些 branch 上 source-target subgroup difference 更大。Differential subgroup rules 可以用来检查 gender-conditioned route 是否面对不同程度的 shift。
- Historical domain shift: 这篇论文和 historical domain shift 的连接很自然，因为 archival data 的差异经常是多因素组合，而不是单一变量改变。

#### Limitation

The paper is a population-comparison method rather than a full reliability-monitoring pipeline. I would need to connect discovered differential subgroups to downstream residuals, calibration error, and uncertainty before using it as a model-evaluation contribution.

#### Follow-up

Apply differential subgroup discovery between natural and balanced training sets, and between training and held-out historical sources. Then test whether discovered subgroups predict elderly residual tails better than age/gender bins alone.

### Paper 3

Title: Predicting Current Outcomes From Historical Survey Data With Weighted Conformal Prediction
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10563
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint; submitted to Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, Series B
Source: official arXiv record, verified 2026-07-19
Research line: uncertainty-aware evaluation, temporal covariate shift, subgroup-specific weighting, conformal prediction
Research function: Monitoring / Evaluation / Intervention
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for uncertainty-aware monitoring under historical shift

#### English Summary

- Problem: historical data are often used to predict current outcomes, but temporal covariate shift makes ordinary prediction intervals unreliable for the current target population.
- Method: the paper develops weighted conformal prediction using subgroup-specific density-ratio estimation and survey weighting ideas, so predictive uncertainty can be adjusted toward the current target population rather than the historical source population.
- Evaluation: the method is evaluated in survey-data settings where historical and current populations differ, with attention to prediction validity under shifted covariate distributions.
- Findings: the paper shows that uncertainty guarantees need to be aligned with the target population. Marginal validity on historical data is not enough when subgroup composition and covariate distributions change over time.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: 用 historical data 预测 current outcomes 时，普通 uncertainty interval 可能对当前人群不再可靠。原因不是模型一定变差，而是 calibration population 和 target population 的 covariate distribution 已经变了。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 也属于 historical shift 问题。模型在某个 historical source 或 split 上的 residual interval，不一定能覆盖另一个时期或 archive source 的 elderly samples。global prediction interval 可能 coverage 看起来可以，但 elderly subgroup coverage 已经不足。

技术直觉: conformal prediction 本来可以给 prediction interval，但它通常依赖 calibration data 与 target data exchangeable。weighted conformal 的直觉是通过 density ratio 把 calibration 更接近 current target distribution。对我来说，这可以转化为 source/period/image-quality-aware conformal intervals for age estimation。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: global interval coverage or global MAE 可能隐藏 elderly subgroup undercoverage。论文提醒我，uncertainty validity 也需要 subgroup-aware and target-aware evaluation。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly samples under historical shift may need wider or differently calibrated intervals. 如果 elderly coverage 低，说明模型不仅 error 大，而且 uncertainty 也不可信。
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing 可以改变 calibration distribution。若 balancing 后 global coverage 提升但 elderly coverage 下降，它仍然是 harmful intervention。weighted conformal 可以作为 intervention side-effect audit。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded gender-conditional age models may produce route-specific residual distributions. Weighted conformal can test whether intervals remain valid within each route and subgroup under shift.
- Historical domain shift: 论文标题中的 historical-to-current prediction directly matches my concern about historical portrait data. The method gives me a principled way to evaluate uncertainty when source period and target period differ.

#### Limitation

The paper is about survey outcomes rather than computer vision regression. I should borrow the weighted-coverage logic, but adapt it to age-estimation residuals, image embeddings, source/period metadata, and elderly-specific interval coverage.

#### Follow-up

Prototype weighted conformal age intervals under held-out source or period splits. Evaluate global coverage, elderly coverage, interval width, undercoverage rate, and whether density-ratio weights are stable for small elderly slices.

## 3. Two Research Questions

### Question 1

English:

- Research question: Under historical portrait domain shift, can similarity-defined validation strata explain elderly-group age-estimation degradation better than coarse age/gender subgroup reporting?
- Why it matters: my thesis found elderly degradation hidden by global MAE, but today's external-validation and differential-subgroup papers suggest that the deeper mechanism may be target-support mismatch rather than age alone.
- Possible data: thesis portrait images, predicted age, true age, gender labels, archive source, period metadata, image-quality proxies, visual embeddings, and reconstruction or nearest-neighbor similarity scores.
- Possible method: compute portrait-level source-support scores using embeddings or generative reconstruction; discover differential subgroups between training and target sources; compare residuals across similarity strata, age/gender groups, and discovered subgroup rules.
- Evaluation metric: global MAE, elderly MAE, low-support elderly MAE, residual skew, underestimation rate, calibration error, worst-slice MAE, and explained residual variance by subgroup definition.
- Thesis connection: directly tests aggregate metric masking, elderly-group degradation, cascade fragility, balancing trade-offs, and historical domain shift.
- Risk or limitation: similarity scores may capture superficial image quality rather than age-relevant support, so the analysis needs ablations using source metadata, image quality, and representation distance separately.

中文:

研究问题: 在 historical portrait domain shift 下，similarity-defined validation strata 是否比粗粒度 age/gender subgroup reporting 更能解释 elderly-group age-estimation degradation？

这个问题现实可做，因为它直接接在我的 thesis evaluation pipeline 上，只需要加入 source-support score、differential subgroup rules 和 residual diagnostics。它能把 "elderly group 表现差" 进一步拆成 "哪些 elderly samples 因为 target-support mismatch 而表现差"。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: Can subgroup-aware weighted conformal intervals maintain reliable elderly-group coverage under archive-source or historical-period shift?
- Why it matters: global uncertainty coverage can look acceptable while vulnerable subgroups are undercovered, which would repeat the aggregate-metric masking pattern from my thesis at the uncertainty level.
- Possible data: age-estimation residuals, prediction intervals, age and gender groups, source/period fields, image-quality proxies, embeddings, and held-out historical target splits.
- Possible method: compare unweighted conformal calibration, age-group conformal calibration, source-aware weighted conformal calibration, and similarity-weighted conformal calibration under held-out source or period validation.
- Evaluation metric: global coverage, elderly coverage, elderly undercoverage rate, interval width, worst-group coverage gap, residual calibration gap, false-safe rate, and coverage stability across temporal splits.
- Thesis connection: extends my calibration analysis and residual diagnostics into uncertainty-aware subgroup reliability monitoring under historical domain shift.
- Risk or limitation: density-ratio estimation may be unstable for small elderly subgroups, so the experiment should report uncertainty bands and sensitivity to weight clipping.

中文:

研究问题: subgroup-aware weighted conformal intervals 能否在 archive-source 或 historical-period shift 下维持 reliable elderly-group coverage？

这个问题把我的 thesis 中的 calibration analysis 推进到 uncertainty-aware monitoring。重点不是让 interval 平均覆盖率好看，而是检查 elderly group 在真实 target shift 下是否被 undercovered。
