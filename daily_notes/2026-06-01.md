### Paper 1

Title: "Who experiences large model decay and why?" A Hierarchical Framework for Diagnosing Heterogeneous Performance Drift
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00756
Year / Venue: 2025, ICML 2025
Source: arXiv
Research line: distribution shift; subgroup reliability; deployment degradation
Screening score: 24/25

English Summary

- Problem:
  The paper studies post-deployment performance decay and asks which subgroups degrade the most, instead of only reporting average drift.
- Method:
  It proposes SHIFT, a subgroup-scanning hierarchical inference framework. The first stage asks where unacceptable subgroup decay appears; the second stage asks how more detailed covariate or outcome shifts explain that decay.
- Evaluation:
  The paper reports real-world experiments and shows that SHIFT can identify interpretable subgroups with large decay and point to targeted mitigation actions.
- Findings:
  Performance drift is heterogeneous rather than uniform. A useful deployment diagnostic should therefore localize degradation to affected subgroups and connect that degradation to specific shift mechanisms.

中文解释

- 它解决的问题：
  这篇论文解决的是 deployment 之后“谁在掉性能、为什么掉”的问题，而不是只看整体 accuracy 或整体 calibration 下降多少。
- 为什么重要：
  如果只看 aggregate metric，我很可能看不到真正受影响的 subgroup。论文正面回应了我 thesis 里的核心问题：平均指标会掩盖不均匀失败。
- 技术直觉：
  它先做 subgroup scan，定位哪里发生了异常大的 performance decay，再往下分解是 covariate shift 还是 outcome-related shift 在驱动这个现象。这个层次化过程比单纯做 drift detection 更接近 research diagnosis。

Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking:
  This paper directly explains why global metrics can hide harmful subgroup decay.
- Elderly-group degradation:
  In my thesis, elderly samples degraded more severely under historical shift. SHIFT gives a concrete framework for testing whether elderly-group decay is statistically larger than overall decay.
- Balancing trade-offs:
  If balancing changes average error but not worst-affected subgroup decay, SHIFT would help show that the intervention improved optics more than reliability.
- Cascade fragility:
  A cascaded model may fail only on specific routed subgroups. SHIFT provides a way to detect whether those failures are concentrated rather than global.
- Historical domain shift:
  This is the strongest bridge to my thesis because historical portrait data naturally creates deployment-like shift across time and visual style.

Explicit thesis bridge:

This paper connects to my thesis because it turns my qualitative observation about elderly-group degradation under historical shift into a formal subgroup-drift diagnosis problem.

Methodological relevance:

The method may help my research by giving me a principled way to test whether performance decay is concentrated in historically vulnerable subgroups and to ask what type of shift explains that decay.

Reinterpretation of my thesis findings:

This paper suggests that my thesis finding may be interpreted as heterogeneous deployment decay masked by aggregate evaluation.

Recommended for paper card.

### Paper 2

Title: Subgroup Performance Analysis in Hidden Stratifications
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10382
Year / Venue: 2025, MICCAI 2025 submission / arXiv preprint
Source: arXiv
Research line: hidden subgroup discovery; trustworthy evaluation; reliability monitoring
Screening score: 22/25

English Summary

- Problem:
  The paper asks how to detect important performance disparities when available metadata does not reflect the true hidden axes of model failure.
- Method:
  It applies subgroup discovery on learned feature representations to produce more granular performance reports, without requiring classification labels or metadata for subgroup definition.
- Evaluation:
  The evaluation uses chest X-ray and skin lesion classification, along with new evaluation strategies for hidden subgroup discovery in realistic settings.
- Findings:
  The discovered subgroups expose larger performance disparities than traditional metadata-based subgroup analysis, suggesting that metadata alone can miss the most important reliability failures.

中文解释

- 它解决的问题：
  它想解决的是 hidden stratification。也就是数据里真正导致失败的 subgroup，未必正好对应我手头已有的 demographic metadata。
- 为什么重要：
  这和我 thesis 很像。我看到 elderly-group degradation，但我也一直怀疑，真正的 failure axis 可能不只是年龄或性别，还可能混合了 pose、image quality、historical style、annotation noise。
- 技术直觉：
  论文的核心直觉是从 learned representation 里自动发现 cohesive subgroups，然后比较这些 subgroup 上的性能差异。这样做不是依赖“已有标签定义 subgroup”，而是让数据自己暴露隐藏结构。

Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking:
  The paper strengthens my thesis claim that average performance can hide severe failures that only appear after finer-grained subgroup discovery.
- Elderly-group degradation:
  My elderly-group result may only be a visible surface signal. This paper suggests there may be more specific hidden clusters inside the elderly group with even worse reliability.
- Balancing trade-offs:
  Balancing on coarse labels can miss hidden strata. That gives a plausible explanation for why balancing may help one visible group but still harm a vulnerable hidden subgroup.
- Cascade fragility:
  If cascade routing depends on features correlated with hidden strata, performance could collapse for discovered clusters even when explicit metadata groups look stable.
- Historical domain shift:
  Historical portraits likely contain latent strata linked to photographic era, restoration artifacts, or portrait conventions. This paper offers a way to think beyond predefined subgroup labels.

Explicit thesis bridge:

This paper connects to my thesis because it challenges the assumption that subgroup analysis should be limited to predefined demographic categories.

Methodological relevance:

The method may help my research by motivating representation-based subgroup discovery before I conclude that age-group or gender-group analysis is sufficient.

Reinterpretation of my thesis findings:

This paper suggests that my thesis finding may be interpreted as a partial observation of deeper hidden stratifications inside historical data.

### Paper 3

Title: Group Fairness Under Distribution Shifts: Analysis and Robust Post-Processing
Link: https://openreview.net/forum?id=FL98GeTuwf
Year / Venue: 2025 submission to ICLR 2026
Source: OpenReview
Research line: fairness degradation under shift; robust evaluation; post-deployment robustness
Screening score: 21/25

English Summary

- Problem:
  The paper studies how fairness guarantees break under distribution shift, even when a classifier seems fair on the source distribution.
- Method:
  It derives bounds on fairness violation and excess risk under covariate and concept shift, then proposes a robust post-processing algorithm based on an uncertainty set over possible shifts.
- Evaluation:
  The evaluation uses geographic shift on ACSIncome, plus tests with noisy group labels and worst-case covariate shifts.
- Findings:
  Fairness is highly shift-sensitive. The analysis also suggests that attribute-aware methods can be more robust than attribute-blind ones when fairness must hold under unseen deployment conditions.

中文解释

- 它解决的问题：
  它讨论的是 fairness guarantee 在 distribution shift 下为什么会失效，以及如何通过 robust post-processing 做更稳的修正。
- 为什么重要：
  这正好能帮助我解释 thesis 里的 balancing trade-off。一个 intervention 在 source distribution 上看起来更公平，不代表它在 shifted target distribution 上仍然更可靠。
- 技术直觉：
  论文把 fairness degradation 分解到 covariate shift 和 concept shift，再在这些潜在 shift 的 uncertainty set 上学习更稳的后处理规则。重点不是让一个静态指标更好看，而是让 fairness 在 shift 下不那么脆弱。

Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking:
  A model can preserve acceptable overall error while subgroup fairness degrades under shift. This paper gives that failure mode a formal analysis.
- Elderly-group degradation:
  If elderly samples act as a vulnerable subgroup, the paper supports analyzing whether fairness-style interventions remain stable when the target distribution changes over time.
- Balancing trade-offs:
  This is the most direct connection. My thesis found that balancing could worsen elderly-group error. This paper offers a principled explanation: interventions optimized for one distribution may not transfer robustly.
- Cascade fragility:
  Complex fairness fixes or routing rules can inherit new vulnerabilities under shift. The paper reinforces my caution about multi-stage corrections that look stable only in-sample.
- Historical domain shift:
  Even though the evaluation is not in vision, the conceptual lesson transfers cleanly to historical portrait modeling: subgroup-aware corrections must be tested under realistic target shifts.

Explicit thesis bridge:

This paper connects to my thesis because it gives a theoretical and algorithmic account of why fairness-oriented corrections can fail once the deployment distribution moves.

Methodological relevance:

The method may help my research by suggesting uncertainty-aware subgroup correction rather than naive balancing or one-shot fairness adjustment.

Reinterpretation of my thesis findings:

This paper suggests that my thesis finding may be interpreted as fairness degradation under historical deployment shift, not just a dataset imbalance artifact.

## 2. Emerging Trend

English

The strongest emerging trend is a shift from average-model evaluation to subgroup-conditional reliability diagnosis under realistic deployment shift. Recent papers are no longer satisfied with saying that a model drifts or that fairness changes. They increasingly ask three linked questions: which subgroup degrades, what hidden structure explains that degradation, and whether any corrective action remains stable when the target distribution changes again. This trend is very close to my research direction because it treats subgroup reliability as the main object of study rather than as a side metric.

中文

我看到的最强趋势，是研究正在从“整体指标评估”转向“真实 deployment shift 下的 subgroup 条件可靠性诊断”。现在更有价值的问题不再只是模型有没有 drift、fairness 有没有变差，而是：到底哪个 subgroup 在退化，背后有没有 hidden structure，以及 correction 在下一次 shift 来临时是否仍然稳。这条趋势和我的研究方向高度一致，因为它把 subgroup reliability 从附属指标变成了核心研究对象。

## 3. Two Research Questions

### Question 1

English

Research question:
Can representation-based subgroup discovery reveal hidden elderly-related failure clusters in historical facial age estimation before global MAE changes noticeably?

Why it matters:
My thesis already showed elderly-group degradation, but coarse age bins may still hide more specific vulnerable clusters.

Possible data:
My thesis dataset with historical-period splits; optional public age-estimation datasets with time or style variation.

Possible method:
Learn image representations, perform unsupervised subgroup discovery within age-defined slices, then compare subgroup MAE, calibration error, and residual skew across historical periods.

Evaluation metric:
Worst-subgroup MAE, subgroup calibration error, residual variance, drift magnitude across time windows.

Thesis connection:
This directly extends aggregate metric masking, elderly-group degradation, and historical domain shift.

Risk or limitation:
The discovered clusters may be hard to interpret semantically, so diagnosis quality may depend on visualization and manual inspection.

中文

研究问题：
在历史人像年龄估计中，基于 representation 的 subgroup discovery 能否在整体 MAE 明显变化之前，提前暴露 elderly-related hidden failure clusters？

为什么重要：
我的 thesis 已经看到 elderly-group degradation，但粗粒度年龄分组可能仍然掩盖了更细的脆弱群体。

可能数据：
我的 thesis 数据集，按历史时期切分；也可以补充带有时间或风格变化的公开年龄估计数据。

可能方法：
先学习图像表征，再在年龄分层内部做无监督 subgroup discovery，比较不同历史时期下各 subgroup 的 MAE、calibration error 和 residual skew。

评估指标：
worst-subgroup MAE、subgroup calibration error、residual variance、跨时间窗口的 drift magnitude。

Thesis 连接：
它直接延伸了 aggregate metric masking、elderly-group degradation 和 historical domain shift。

风险或限制：
发现出来的 cluster 可能语义解释性有限，因此需要可视化和人工诊断配合。

### Question 2

English

Research question:
When balancing or subgroup-aware post-processing is applied to historical age estimation, under what temporal shifts does it improve average accuracy but worsen worst-group reliability?

Why it matters:
This question directly targets the balancing trade-off I observed in my thesis.

Possible data:
Historical portrait data with time-based train-test splits and subgroup labels or pseudo-subgroup assignments.

Possible method:
Compare baseline regression models, balanced training variants, and uncertainty-aware subgroup post-processing under multiple temporal shift settings.

Evaluation metric:
Global MAE, worst-group MAE, elderly-group MAE, calibration error, and stability of subgroup rankings across periods.

Thesis connection:
This directly tests balancing trade-offs and asks whether simple models remain more stable than more complex interventions.

Risk or limitation:
The main challenge is separating true temporal shift effects from confounding changes in image quality or label quality.

中文

研究问题：
当我在历史年龄估计中使用 balancing 或 subgroup-aware post-processing 时，在哪些 temporal shift 条件下它会提升平均精度，却恶化 worst-group reliability？

为什么重要：
这个问题正面对应我 thesis 里观察到的 balancing trade-off。

可能数据：
带时间切分的历史人像数据，以及真实 subgroup 标签或 pseudo-subgroup assignment。

可能方法：
比较 baseline regression、balanced training variant 和 uncertainty-aware subgroup post-processing，在多种 temporal shift 设定下观察表现。

评估指标：
global MAE、worst-group MAE、elderly-group MAE、calibration error，以及不同时期间 subgroup 排名的稳定性。

Thesis 连接：
它直接检验 balancing trade-off，并追问简单模型是否比复杂 correction 更稳定。

风险或限制：
最大的困难是把真正的 temporal shift 与图像质量变化、标注质量变化区分开。
