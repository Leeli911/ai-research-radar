### Paper 1

Title: When the Past Misleads: Rethinking Training Data Expansion Under Temporal Distribution Shifts
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01060
Year / Venue: 2025 / AIES 2025
Source: arXiv / AIES paper
Research line: temporal shift, fairness degradation, historical-data selection
Research function: Diagnosis and Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor; **Recommended for paper card**

#### English Summary

- **Problem:** More historical training data is commonly assumed to improve future prediction, but temporally stale data can harm both performance and fairness when feature–outcome relationships change.
- **Method:** The authors separate covariate shift from concept shift in simulations, then study training-window expansion on 12 years of student-retention data from 23 minority-serving community colleges. They examine overall performance and subgroup fairness as the historical window grows.
- **Evaluation:** The paper varies shift type and magnitude in simulation, then uses temporal data splits and subgroup/intersectional analyses in the empirical study.
- **Findings:** Longer historical windows help only when the relevant relationship is stable. Under concept shift, they can reduce performance; fairness also deteriorates when demographic groups experience shift at different magnitudes. Intersectional effects are not simply additive.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是：训练数据“更多、历史更长”并不天然代表模型在未来更可靠。如果 feature–label relationship 随时间改变，加入较早的数据会把已经失效的规律带回训练过程，并且不同群体经历的 shift 强度不同，fairness 也会一起变差。

技术直觉是把 shift 拆开看。Covariate shift 主要改变输入分布；concept shift 则改变输入与结果之间的关系。后者更危险，因为它会让过去有效的预测规则在目标时期失效。论文用模拟和真实的多年教育数据说明，historical window 应该是一个可检验的设计选择，而不是越长越好。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- **Aggregate metric masking:** A pooled temporal score can make an expanded historical window look useful while hiding deterioration for one demographic or intersectional subgroup. This directly parallels my global MAE masking elderly-group error.
- **Elderly-group degradation:** My elderly portraits may represent a temporally distinct or conditionally shifted slice rather than merely a scarce age bin. The paper supports testing whether their feature–age relationship changes across archive eras.
- **Balancing trade-offs:** Age balancing would not repair a stale feature–label relationship. The paper gives a concrete mechanism for why adding or reweighting elderly historical examples could worsen their error.
- **Cascade fragility:** A gender-conditional cascade can learn time-specific routing or conditional associations; when these move across eras, errors at the routing and regression stages may compound.
- **Historical domain shift:** This is the clearest bridge. My portrait archives are historical data, so I can compare short recent-era windows, broad historical windows, and source/era-aware training rather than treating all past portraits as exchangeable.

### Paper 2

Title: Beyond Visual Forensics: Auditing Multimodal Robustness for Synthetic Medical Image Detection
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25375
Year / Venue: 2026 / arXiv preprint
Source: arXiv
Research line: multimodal authenticity, context-induced decision shift, deployment auditing
Research function: Diagnosis and Evaluation
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: high-value method reference for multimodal authenticity auditing

#### English Summary

- **Problem:** Synthetic-image detectors are often evaluated from pixels alone even though deployed vision-language systems combine images with reports or metadata. Context can therefore alter an authenticity decision while the visual evidence is unchanged.
- **Method:** The authors introduce a paired audit: they hold each medical image fixed while swapping controlled metadata, including provenance cues, and quantify **Text-Induced Decision Shift** and flip signatures.
- **Evaluation:** The benchmark covers NIH-CXR14, ISIC2019, and a pediatric chest X-ray dataset, testing medical and general open-weight VLMs, frontier API models, and multimodal fake-detection systems under image-only and image-plus-record conditions.
- **Findings:** Metadata alone can sharply change authenticity judgments. On real NIH-CXR14 images, an AI-origin provenance field drove MedGemma-27B accuracy from 97.6% to 0.0% in the reported stress test. Model scale did not consistently protect against this failure, and image-only evaluation underestimated deployment risk.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是：multimodal authenticity system 不只是看图像，也会看 accompanying record 或 metadata；因此即使 image 完全不变，文字或来源字段也可能让最终真假判断翻转。

技术直觉是 paired counterfactual audit。固定 image，只替换 metadata 的一个受控字段，再测量 prediction 是否改变。这样可以把“模型真的看到了 visual evidence”与“模型被 context shortcut 引导”区分开。对于 multimodal authenticity，单独报告 image-only accuracy 不足以说明真实工作流是否可靠。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- **Aggregate metric masking:** A strong average authenticity accuracy can conceal a decisive context-sensitive failure mode. This is analogous to my MAE not exposing systematic elderly residual patterns.
- **Elderly-group degradation:** For historical portraits, age-related visual ambiguity may make elderly cases especially susceptible to nonvisual cues such as archive descriptions, source labels, or provenance metadata; this should be measured rather than assumed.
- **Balancing trade-offs:** Adding balanced age metadata or captions could unintentionally create shortcuts. This paper suggests auditing whether an intervention improves visual reliability or merely shifts reliance toward a contextual proxy.
- **Cascade fragility:** Image-plus-record pipelines are conditional systems. The paper's decision flips show why a downstream context-integration stage can override a stable visual judgment, much like my cascaded model can propagate an earlier conditional error.
- **Historical domain shift:** Archive source, era, cataloguing practice, and restoration metadata may change together with image quality. A paired metadata-swap audit could test whether a historical age estimator or authenticity model relies on these unstable contextual correlations.

### Paper 3

Title: Omni-Fake: Benchmarking Unified Multimodal Social Media Deepfake Detection
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01638
Year / Venue: 2026 / arXiv preprint
Source: arXiv
Research line: multimodal authenticity, OOD generalization, decision-localization evaluation
Research function: Evaluation
Screening Score: 19/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: secondary-track benchmark reference; useful for defining authenticity stress tests

#### English Summary

- **Problem:** Existing deepfake benchmarks often isolate one modality or use simplified manipulations, so their scores provide weak evidence for generalization to realistic, cross-modal authenticity threats.
- **Method:** Omni-Fake introduces a unified dataset spanning image, audio, video, and audio-video talking-head content, plus an OOD split intentionally excluded from training. It also proposes a detector that jointly returns a decision, localization, and explanation.
- **Evaluation:** The benchmark contains more than one million in-distribution samples and 200,000 OOD samples. It evaluates detection, localization, explanation, and cross-modal generalization rather than a single in-domain detection score.
- **Findings:** The reported experiments show that the proposed multimodal detector improves detection accuracy, cross-modal generalization, and explainability over the evaluated baselines. Its central contribution is the evaluation protocol: realistic authenticity reliability must be checked on held-out manipulation and modality conditions.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是：deepfake detection 如果只在单一 modality 或固定 generator 上评估，得到的高分未必能迁移到真实的跨模态、未见 manipulation 条件。论文提出统一的 multimodal benchmark，并明确提供训练中未出现的 OOD split。

技术直觉是把 authenticity reliability 从“一个 binary accuracy”扩展成多层任务：是否识别、在哪里识别、解释是否支持判断，以及在 modality 和 generator 改变时是否还能工作。这个思路与我关心的 decision-relevant evaluation 一致：模型输出要能对应实际失败风险。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- **Aggregate metric masking:** In-domain aggregate accuracy can conceal failures on an unseen modality, generator, or manipulation type, just as my global MAE concealed elderly errors.
- **Elderly-group degradation:** Age group can be added as a reliability axis to an authenticity benchmark: I would test whether elderly faces or age-related visual texture receive systematically different false-positive or false-negative rates under new generators.
- **Balancing trade-offs:** Balancing modality or demographic counts is insufficient if the interaction between age appearance and generator family remains unseen. The OOD protocol offers a way to test whether balancing helps beyond its observed categories.
- **Cascade fragility:** Detection, localization, and explanation form a multi-output pipeline. I should examine whether a correct top-level authenticity label coexists with unstable localization or explanation, rather than treating the first score as complete evidence.
- **Historical domain shift:** Old photographs vary in compression, restoration, film grain, and portrait conventions. The paper motivates treating these as held-out acquisition/manipulation conditions when evaluating historical-face authenticity or age-estimation reliability.

## 2. Emerging Trend

**English:**

Today’s papers shift my attention from *how much* data or context a model receives to *which evidence remains valid after the environment changes*. Historical data can become harmful when the conditional relationship has moved; multimodal context can dominate an unchanged image; and in-domain authenticity scores can fail on unseen modalities or manipulations. The common evaluation principle is counterfactual and temporal: hold the decision target fixed, perturb one source of evidence or time period, and inspect which reliability unit degrades. ## 3. Two Research Questions

### Question 1

**English:**

- **Research question:** In historical facial age estimation, does expanding the training window improve global MAE while worsening elderly-group MAE because age–appearance relationships shift across archive eras?
- **Why it matters:** This tests whether my balancing result is partly a temporal data-selection problem, rather than only an age-imbalance problem.
- **Possible data:** Portrait images, age and gender labels, archive/source or era metadata, image-quality scores, embeddings, and saved residuals from my thesis models.
- **Possible method:** Train the same regression baseline on nested historical windows; compare recent-era, broad-history, and era/source-weighted versions; estimate covariate drift in embeddings and conditional residual drift by age group.
- **Evaluation metric:** Global and elderly MAE, worst-era elderly MAE, residual-underestimation rate, age-group calibration error, temporal transfer gap, and bootstrap confidence intervals.
- **Thesis connection:** Directly tests aggregate masking, elderly degradation, balancing trade-offs, cascade fragility, and historical domain shift.
- **Risk or limitation:** Era metadata may be incomplete; digitization quality and archive source should be recorded as potential confounders rather than treated as a single temporal cause.

**中文：**

- **研究问题：** 在 historical facial age estimation 中，扩大 training window 是否会改善 global MAE，却因为 archive era 间 age–appearance relationship 的变化而恶化 elderly-group MAE？
- **为什么重要：** 这可以检验我 thesis 中的 balancing 失败是否部分来自 temporal data selection，而不只是年龄数量不平衡。
- **可能数据：** Portrait images、age/gender labels、archive/source 或 era metadata、image-quality score、embeddings 和 thesis 模型的 residuals。
- **可能方法：** 用相同 regression baseline 训练多个嵌套的 historical windows；比较 recent-era、broad-history 与 era/source-weighted training；按年龄组估计 embedding covariate drift 和 conditional residual drift。
- **评估指标：** Global/elderly MAE、worst-era elderly MAE、residual underestimation rate、age-group calibration error、temporal transfer gap 和 bootstrap confidence intervals。
- **风险：** Era metadata 可能不完整；digitization quality 与 archive source 应作为潜在 confounder 记录，不能简单归因于时间。

### Question 2

**English:**

- **Research question:** Can paired image–metadata counterfactual tests detect whether a multimodal historical-portrait system relies on archive provenance more strongly for elderly faces before its aggregate accuracy degrades?
- **Why it matters:** It turns a context-shortcut risk into a testable subgroup-reliability monitor, linking my face-domain expertise with multimodal authenticity.
- **Possible data:** Historical portraits paired with cataloguing metadata, age/gender labels, source/era fields, generated or manually controlled metadata variants, model predictions, and visual embeddings.
- **Possible method:** Hold the image fixed; swap one source, era, restoration, or provenance field at a time; measure prediction flips, confidence shifts, and disagreement between image-only and image-plus-metadata outputs by age group.
- **Evaluation metric:** Text/metadata-induced decision-shift rate, elderly-versus-non-elderly flip gap, image-only versus multimodal residual gap, calibration gap, OOD/source transfer gap, and false-confidence rate.
- **Thesis connection:** Extends aggregate masking and elderly degradation to a multimodal reliability unit, while testing conditional-routing fragility under historical source shift.
- **Risk or limitation:** Controlled metadata variants must remain plausible and non-identifying; otherwise the stress test may estimate an extreme vulnerability rather than routine deployment risk.

**中文：**

- **研究问题：** Paired image–metadata counterfactual test 能否在 aggregate accuracy 下降前，发现 multimodal historical-portrait system 对 elderly faces 更强地依赖 archive provenance？
- **为什么重要：** 这把 context shortcut risk 转成可测试的 subgroup-reliability monitor，也把我的 face-domain 经验连接到 multimodal authenticity。
- **可能数据：** Historical portraits 及其 cataloguing metadata、age/gender labels、source/era fields、生成或人工控制的 metadata variants、model predictions 和 visual embeddings。
- **可能方法：** 固定 image，每次只替换一个 source、era、restoration 或 provenance field；按 age group 测量 prediction flip、confidence change，以及 image-only 和 image-plus-metadata output 的差异。
- **评估指标：** Metadata-induced decision-shift rate、elderly/non-elderly flip gap、image-only/multimodal residual gap、calibration gap、OOD/source transfer gap 和 false-confidence rate。
- **风险：** 受控 metadata variant 必须合理且不含身份信息；否则 stress test 可能只反映极端脆弱性，而不是常规 deployment risk。
