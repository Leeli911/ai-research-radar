## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Evidence Sufficiency Under Delayed Ground Truth: Proxy Monitoring for Risk Decision Systems
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15740
Year / Venue: 2026, arXiv
Source: arXiv / Hugging Face paper metadata
Research line: label-delayed monitoring and decision-readiness under drift
Research function: Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for decision-relevant monitoring

#### English Summary

- Problem: risk decision systems in fraud detection, credit scoring, and clinical risk assessment often receive ground-truth labels days or months after a decision. During this blind period, model evidence can degrade silently, while standard drift detection does not directly say whether evidence remains sufficient for decision-making.
- Method: the paper formalizes an evidence sufficiency model with four dimensions: completeness, freshness, reliability, and representativeness. It adds a decision-readiness gate and a seven-category proxy indicator framework for estimating sufficiency degradation without labels.
- Evaluation: the framework is evaluated on the IEEE-CIS Fraud Detection dataset with controlled drift injection and blind-period simulation.
- Findings: composite proxy monitoring detects covariate and mixed drift with 100% detection rate, while pure concept drift without feature change remains undetected. The paper explicitly treats this as a structural blind spot of label-free monitoring, not just an empirical weakness.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 ground truth 延迟时如何监控模型是否仍然有足够证据支持自动决策。它不是只检测分布有没有变化，而是问当前证据是否足够 fresh、representative 和 reliable。
- 为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 里的 evaluation 问题相连。我的模型在验证集上有 MAE 和 subgroup metrics，但如果历史人像的标签质量、archive source 或 era composition 发生变化，旧的验证证据可能已经不足以支持新的推断。
- 技术直觉: 论文把 monitoring 从单个 drift alarm 转成 evidence sufficiency score。它同时提醒我，某些 concept drift 在没有标签时无法可靠发现，所以监控系统必须明确自己的 blind spots。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 这篇论文说明，一个整体 performance number 不能代表 evidence sufficiency。我的 global MAE 同样可能掩盖 elderly group 或新 archive source 下的证据不足。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly samples 可以被视为需要单独监控 evidence sufficiency 的 vulnerable subgroup。如果 elderly labels 更稀疏、图像质量更差或 archive metadata 更不完整，整体监控会低估风险。
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing 可能改善样本数量，但不一定改善 freshness、representativeness 或 label reliability。我的 thesis 中 balancing 有时伤害 elderly group，可以重新解释为没有对准真正的 evidence deficiency。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded conditional models 依赖前一阶段输出作为后续决策证据。如果前一阶段在 shift 下失准，后续 age prediction 的 evidence sufficiency 会系统性下降。
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits naturally involve stale labels, incomplete metadata, changing image quality, and era/source shift. The paper gives me a vocabulary for turning those issues into monitorable evidence dimensions.

#### Limitation

The paper is not specifically about subgroup fairness or visual models. Its value for my research is the monitoring framework and the explicit treatment of label-free blind spots.

#### Follow-up

Adapt the four evidence dimensions to historical facial age estimation: label completeness, archive-source representativeness, calibration reliability, and era/image-quality freshness.

### Paper 2

Title: FairFedMed: Benchmarking Group Fairness in Federated Medical Imaging with FairLoRA
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00873
Year / Venue: 2025, arXiv
Source: arXiv / Hugging Face paper metadata
Research line: subgroup fairness across heterogeneous medical institutions
Research function: Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor and method reference with high strategic value

#### English Summary

- Problem: federated medical imaging models are trained across heterogeneous institutions, but existing work often under-evaluates demographic group fairness and cross-institutional subgroup reliability.
- Method: the paper introduces FairFedMed, a benchmark and dataset for medical federated learning fairness. It includes FairFedMed-Oph with 2D fundus and 3D OCT ophthalmology samples plus six demographic attributes, and FairFedMed-Chest using CheXpert and MIMIC-CXR to simulate real cross-institutional federated learning. It also proposes FairLoRA, a fairness-aware low-rank adaptation method that customizes singular value matrices per demographic group while sharing singular vectors.
- Evaluation: the paper evaluates six representative federated learning methods across imaging modalities, demographic attributes, and institutional settings.
- Findings: existing federated learning models often underperform on medical images and overlook group fairness. FairLoRA improves both classification performance and fairness across diverse populations.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 medical AI 在跨机构训练时如何评估和改进 demographic group fairness。不同医院、不同成像模态和不同 subgroup 会形成真实的 deployment heterogeneity。
- 为什么重要: 这非常接近我的 thesis 方向。我的 historical portraits 也不是一个均匀数据集，而是由不同 archive source、era、age group、gender group 和 image quality 组成。平均表现好不能说明每个群体都可靠。
- 技术直觉: FairLoRA 不是简单复制一个完整 group-specific model，而是在共享表示的基础上让不同 demographic group 有自己的低秩参数调整。这比 naive balancing 更像一种可控的 subgroup-aware adaptation。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: FairFedMed 的核心动机是平均 medical image performance 可能掩盖 demographic subgroup fairness gaps。我的 thesis 中 global MAE 掩盖 elderly-group degradation 是同一类问题。
- Elderly-group degradation: 论文显式处理 demographic attributes，可以自然映射到 age-based subgroup evaluation。我的 elderly group 可以从单独报告 MAE 进一步发展为 group-aware adaptation 和 calibration。
- Balancing trade-offs: FairLoRA 提供了一个比 naive resampling 更细的干预思路。我的 balancing 失败可能说明只改变样本比例不够，模型还需要识别哪些 subgroup 需要不同的参数或校准方式。
- Cascade fragility: 这篇论文没有直接研究 cascades，但它提醒我 group-conditioned adaptation 必须被严格评估。如果 group route 或 subgroup-specific component 在 shift 下失准，它可能重复我的 cascaded gender-conditional model 的脆弱性。
- Historical domain shift: cross-institutional medical shift 可以类比 archive-source and era shift。FairFedMed 的评估逻辑可以转化为: source-aware splits, subgroup-aware metrics, and group-specific lightweight adaptation for historical portraits.

#### Limitation

The federated learning setting is more infrastructure-heavy than my core thesis. I should extract the benchmark and subgroup-adaptation logic, not move my research toward FL systems.

#### Follow-up

Test whether lightweight subgroup-conditioned calibration layers or low-rank heads improve elderly and intersectional subgroup reliability under archive-source or era holdout, compared with naive age/gender balancing.

### Paper 3

Title: Lost in the Folds: When Cross-Validation Is Not a Deep Ensemble for Uncertainty Estimation
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18329
Year / Venue: 2026, arXiv
Source: arXiv / Hugging Face paper metadata
Research line: uncertainty evaluation, calibration, and failure detection under shift
Research function: Diagnosis / Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for uncertainty-aware monitoring

#### English Summary

- Problem: medical image segmentation studies often use K-fold cross-validation ensembles but call them deep ensembles. This matters because cross-validation ensembles mix model randomness with data-exposure differences, so their disagreement may not mean the same thing as epistemic uncertainty.
- Method: the paper audits recent segmentation uncertainty studies and compares a standard 5-fold cross-validation ensemble with a 5-member deep ensemble trained on a fixed dataset with different random seeds. The comparison uses otherwise identical nnU-Net configurations.
- Evaluation: the methods are tested on three multi-rater medical segmentation datasets spanning three modalities, with evaluation for calibration, failure detection, ambiguity modeling, and robustness under distribution shift.
- Findings: deep ensembles match segmentation accuracy while improving calibration and failure detection. Cross-validation ensembles sometimes correlate more strongly with inter-rater variability, so the right ensemble construction depends on whether the goal is reliability monitoring or ambiguity modeling.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 uncertainty estimation 里的 protocol confusion。CV ensemble 和 deep ensemble 看起来都像 ensemble，但它们的不确定性来源不同，不能直接混用。
- 为什么重要: 我的 thesis 中已经用 calibration 和 residual diagnostics 观察 hidden failure。下一步如果我要做 uncertainty-aware monitoring，就必须确保 uncertainty signal 真的是 failure risk，而不是数据划分方式造成的假信号。
- 技术直觉: 如果 ensemble 成员看到的数据不同，disagreement 可能反映 annotation ambiguity 或 data exposure difference；如果成员看到相同数据但随机初始化不同，disagreement 更接近 model uncertainty 和 failure detection signal。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 论文发现 segmentation accuracy 可以相近，但 calibration 和 failure detection 不同。我的 global MAE 也可能看起来相近，但 subgroup residual variance 和 uncertainty quality 完全不同。
- Elderly-group degradation: 对 elderly portraits，我不能只问 uncertainty 是否整体有效，而要问 uncertainty 是否能识别 elderly high-error samples 和 underestimation risk。
- Balancing trade-offs: CV folds 改变训练数据暴露，会让 uncertainty 混入 sample-composition effects。我的 balancing experiments 也可能改变 uncertainty signal，需要区分模型不确定性和数据组成影响。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded conditional models 需要知道 routing 和 downstream prediction 何时不可靠。这个 paper 提醒我，failure detection signal 必须和实际 decision goal 对齐。
- Historical domain shift: 在 historical portrait shift 下，我可以比较 CV ensembles 和 deep ensembles 是否对 era/source holdout、low-quality scans、elderly samples 给出不同的 uncertainty ranking。

#### Limitation

The task is medical segmentation, not age regression. I would need to translate the uncertainty protocol into regression uncertainty, calibration, coverage-risk curves, and subgroup-specific failure detection.

#### Follow-up

Build a small uncertainty protocol study for historical age estimation: compare CV ensembles, deep ensembles, and simple bootstrap residual models under random, archive-source, and era holdout splits.
