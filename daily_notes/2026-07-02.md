## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Adaptive Calibration for Fair and Performant Facial Recognition
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04469
Year / Venue: 2026, arXiv
Source: official arXiv abstract and HTML
Research line: facial recognition calibration and subgroup fairness without demographic labels
Research function: Explanation / Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor and paper-card candidate

#### English Summary

- Problem: face-verification systems often map cosine similarity scores to match decisions as if the same embedding distance means the same match probability everywhere. The paper argues that this assumption creates calibration and fairness problems because different embedding regions can require different probability mappings.
- Method: the paper introduces Adaptive Calibration, a post-hoc calibration method that uses local embedding context and similarity scores to produce region-specific calibrated match probabilities. It aims to improve fairness without requiring demographic metadata at inference time.
- Evaluation: the method is evaluated across standard face-verification benchmarks and multiple pretrained face-recognition backbones, with metrics including AUROC, low-FPR TPR, ECE, Brier score, worst-group AUROC, and leveling-down diagnostics.
- Findings: Adaptive Calibration improves both overall performance and worst-group calibration in many settings. A key finding is that fairness improvements do not have to come from degrading better-served groups; local calibration can lift worse-served embedding regions instead.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 face verification 中 score calibration 不稳定的问题。同一个 cosine similarity 在不同 embedding region 里可能对应不同的真实 match probability，所以一个全局阈值或全局校准函数会让某些群体更容易被误判。
- 为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 很接近。我的历史人像年龄估计也依赖 learned representation。如果 elderly portraits 或某些 historical source 落在低密度、低质量或偏移的 embedding region，global MAE 和 global confidence 都可能低估局部失败。
- 技术直觉: 方法不是重新训练一个大模型，而是在 embedding space 上做 region-specific probability correction。它把 fairness 问题从 "需要 demographic label" 转成 "需要识别哪些 embedding regions 的 score-probability mapping 不可靠"。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 论文说明 global verification performance 和 global calibration 可以掩盖 worst-group AUROC、Brier score 和 low-FPR TPR 的局部失败。我的 thesis 中 global MAE 也可能掩盖 elderly group 的 residual skew 和 underestimation。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits 可能处在训练集较稀疏或视觉风格更偏移的 embedding region。Adaptive Calibration 提醒我，elderly degradation 不一定只来自样本数量少，也可能来自 score meaning 在该区域发生变化。
- Balancing trade-offs: 我的 balancing 有时 worsened elderly performance。这篇论文的 "avoid leveling down" 很关键，因为它说明 fairness intervention 应该检查是否真的 lift worst groups，而不是通过牺牲 better-served groups 得到表面公平。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded gender-conditional age model 依赖上游预测和下游条件路径。如果上游 confidence 或 embedding score 在不同 region 中含义不稳定，conditional routing 会把校准误差传递到 age regression head。
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits 的 era、source、image quality 和 pose 会改变 embedding geometry。region-specific calibration 可以被改造成 archive-source-aware 或 era-aware calibration，用于诊断 historical domain shift 下的 subgroup reliability。

#### Limitation

The task is face verification rather than age estimation. The method corrects post-hoc calibration but cannot recover information that the backbone never encoded, so it would need to be paired with representation diagnostics in my setting.

#### Follow-up

Test whether local embedding density, calibration error, or score-probability mismatch predicts elderly underestimation and high residual variance in my historical portrait models.

### Paper 2

Title: When AUC Misleads: Polarization-Aware Evaluation of Deepfake Detectors under Domain Shift
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19184
Year / Venue: 2026, arXiv
Source: official arXiv abstract and HTML
Research line: domain-mixed evaluation and score-distribution diagnostics for deepfake detection
Research function: Diagnosis / Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for decision-relevant evaluation under shift

#### English Summary

- Problem: deepfake detectors are often evaluated with AUC separately on multiple datasets, then compared using average AUC. The paper argues that this can be overoptimistic because real deployment mixes heterogeneous data sources and manipulation types.
- Method: the paper introduces Cross-dataset AUC, a metric that combines per-domain AUC with score-distribution polarization. Polarization is measured through distances between real and fake score distributions, with the main implementation using Wasserstein distance.
- Evaluation: the paper evaluates seven deepfake detectors across seven benchmark datasets, including FaceForensics++, Celeb-DF, DFD, WildDeepfake, DFDC, DFDCP, and DF40. It also studies an out-of-distribution MagicBrush scenario.
- Findings: average per-dataset AUC can rank models differently from combined-domain evaluation. Cross-AUC more closely tracks mixed-domain performance and exposes cases where high separate-domain AUC hides threshold instability under domain shift.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 AUC 在 domain shift 下误导模型选择的问题。一个 detector 在每个数据集上单独算 AUC 都不错，但把多个 domain 混合到真实部署场景时，score ordering 和 threshold 可能不再稳定。
- 为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 中 aggregate MAE masking 的逻辑一致。我的模型在某个 era/source 上表现好，不代表把多个 historical sources 混合后仍然有可靠的 age ranking、confidence 或 residual behavior。
- 技术直觉: Cross-AUC 的核心是看 score distribution 是否足够 polarized。如果真/假类别在不同 domain 中的 score 分布不稳定，单独 AUC 会给出过乐观的 generalization impression。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 论文直接说明 average AUC can mislead under domain shift。我的 global MAE 或 average subgroup MAE 也可能在 source-mixed historical portraits 中掩盖 decision threshold instability。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits 可以看作一个更容易出现 score compression 或 residual polarization 弱化的 subgroup。即使 average MAE 可接受，elderly samples 的 error scores 可能不再和真实 age ordering 对齐。
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing factor 在 Cross-AUC 中影响 metric behavior，这提醒我评估 balancing 时不能只看平均误差下降，还要看干预是否改善 source-mixed score separability and threshold stability。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded models 需要稳定的 upstream score ordering。若 gender prediction scores or age-group confidence 在不同 historical sources 中 polarization 不一致，cascade routing 会出现 domain-specific collapse。
- Historical domain shift: 这篇论文非常适合迁移到 historical portraits: separate-source performance could look good, while combined-source evaluation reveals that archive-specific score distributions are not comparable.

#### Limitation

The paper studies binary deepfake detection, not regression. I would need to adapt the idea from AUC and class-score polarization to age residual distributions, interval decisions, or ordinal age ranking.

#### Follow-up

Define a regression analogue of Cross-AUC for historical age estimation: combine source-wise residual ranking, underestimation polarization, and cross-source calibration stability.

### Paper 3

Title: Interpretable Uncertainty Routing Separating Emotion Ambiguity from Distribution Shift in Facial Expression Recognition
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22725
Year / Venue: 2026, arXiv
Source: official arXiv metadata and abstract
Research line: uncertainty decomposition and routing under facial-domain shift
Research function: Explanation / Intervention / Monitoring
Screening Score: 23/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for uncertainty-aware monitoring

#### English Summary

- Problem: facial expression recognition faces two different uncertainty sources: ambiguous in-distribution expressions and out-of-distribution inputs. A single uncertainty score can conflate these, even though the two cases require different downstream actions.
- Method: the paper decomposes uncertainty into aleatoric and epistemic components using a deep ensemble of fully fine-tuned DINOv2 models. It introduces Uncertainty-Aware Routing, an inference-time mechanism that keeps ambiguous in-distribution samples while rejecting likely shifted inputs.
- Evaluation: aleatoric uncertainty is validated against human annotator disagreement, while epistemic uncertainty is validated against corruption-induced distribution shift. The paper reports Spearman correlation for disagreement recovery and AUROC for shift detection.
- Findings: aleatoric uncertainty recovers annotator disagreement, epistemic uncertainty detects corruption-induced shift, and UAR retains about 1.8 times more ambiguous in-distribution faces than single-uncertainty routing at a matched OOD rejection rate.

#### 中文解释

- 解决的问题: 这篇论文解决的是 uncertainty 信号混在一起的问题。表情本身可能 ambiguous，但图像也可能因为 corruption 或 domain shift 而 unreliable。这两类情况不应该用同一个 rejection rule 处理。
- 为什么重要: 我的 historical portrait task 也有类似问题。某些样本本身年龄模糊，这是 aleatoric uncertainty；某些样本因为年代、画质、姿态或修复痕迹偏离训练分布，这是 epistemic uncertainty。如果二者混在一起，监控系统会误判模型失败原因。
- 技术直觉: 论文的关键不是 "uncertainty 越大越拒绝"，而是先区分 uncertainty 的来源，再决定 downstream action。ambiguous but in-distribution 的样本应该被标记为模糊，shifted samples 则应该触发 OOD warning。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 单一 uncertainty 或 global accuracy 可能掩盖两种不同失败来源。我的 global MAE 也可能把 intrinsic age ambiguity 和 domain-shift failure 混成一个平均误差。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly faces naturally carry higher age ambiguity, but historical elderly portraits may also have stronger domain shift. UAR 提醒我需要区分 "elderly age inherently ambiguous" and "elderly subgroup shifted out of training support"。
- Balancing trade-offs: 如果 balancing 增加了某些 subgroup 的 aleatoric ambiguity or epistemic shift exposure，平均样本比例改善并不等于可靠性改善。uncertainty decomposition 可以帮助解释 balancing 为什么有时伤害 elderly group。
- Cascade fragility: UAR 是一种 routing method，但它用独立信号验证 aleatoric and epistemic components。我的 cascaded conditional model 缺少这种 routing validation，因此更容易把 upstream uncertainty 传给后续 age estimator。
- Historical domain shift: corruption-induced shift 是 historical portrait degradation 的可操作 proxy。这个方法可以迁移到 archive-source shift、image restoration artifacts, blur, contrast loss, and era-specific visual style.

#### Limitation

The paper is about facial expression classification and uses deep ensembles, which may be computationally heavier than my thesis setup. It also validates shift using corruptions rather than real historical archive shifts.

#### Follow-up

Use lightweight ensembles or MC-dropout to separate age ambiguity from source/era shift, then test whether epistemic uncertainty predicts elderly underestimation before global MAE changes.
