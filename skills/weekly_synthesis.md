# Skill: Weekly Synthesis / 每周研究综合

## Purpose / 目的

**English**

This skill defines how weekly research synthesis should be performed. The weekly report should not only summarize papers. It should identify repeated themes, detect conceptual shifts, expose contradictions, rank research opportunities, update topic maps, and reflect on how my research identity is evolving.

**中文**

这个 skill 用来规定每周总结方式。周报不能只是论文摘要，而要识别重复主题、概念变化、矛盾点、最强研究机会，并反思我的 research identity 如何变化。

## Weekly Tasks / 每周任务

### 1. Identify Repeated Themes

English:

Find themes that appear across multiple papers or notes, such as subgroup degradation, representation drift, calibration failure, uncertainty under shift, or fragile interventions.

中文:

找出一周内反复出现的主题，比如 subgroup degradation、representation drift、calibration failure、uncertainty under shift、fragile interventions。

### 2. Detect Conceptual Shifts

English:

Identify whether my thinking moved from one framing to another, such as from "fairness" to "subgroup reliability" or from "accuracy" to "decision-relevant monitoring."

中文:

识别我的思考是否发生转向，比如从 fairness 转向 subgroup reliability，或者从 accuracy 转向 decision-relevant monitoring。

### 3. Identify Contradictions

English:

Look for tensions between papers or between papers and my thesis findings. For example, one method may promote balancing while my thesis shows balancing can harm elderly-group performance.

中文:

找出论文之间、论文和我的 thesis 之间的矛盾。例如某篇论文推荐 balancing，但我的 thesis 发现 balancing 可能伤害 elderly group。

### 4. Rank Research Opportunities

Rank opportunities by:

- thesis fit;
- technical feasibility;
- proposal strength;
- novelty;
- data availability;
- long-term identity fit.

中文:

研究机会排序时要看：是否贴合 thesis、技术是否可行、是否适合 proposal、是否有新意、数据是否可得、是否符合长期 research identity。

### 5. Update Topic Maps

English:

Update topic maps when a repeated pattern becomes important enough to affect my long-term direction.

中文:

如果某个主题反复出现，并且会影响长期方向，就更新 topic maps。

### 6. Reflect on Research Identity

English:

End each weekly synthesis with a short reflection: What kind of researcher am I becoming this week?

中文:

每周总结最后必须回答：这一周之后，我正在成为什么样的研究者？

## Output Requirements / 输出要求

The weekly synthesis must be:

- bilingual;
- reflective;
- technically grounded;
- concise;
- connected to my thesis findings;
- explicit about what to read, reject, and develop next.
