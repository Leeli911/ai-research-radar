# Lab and Research Blog Sources

Use this file to track technical discussions, lab posts, benchmark releases, and research framing. Lab posts are useful when they explain why a problem matters, not only when they announce a model.

- Microsoft Research Blog: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/
- Meta AI Blog: https://ai.meta.com/blog/

- Chinese judgment note:

## Signals Worth Tracking

Strong signals:

- A new benchmark for distribution shift, subgroup robustness, provenance, or monitoring
- A post that reframes evaluation for real decisions
- A tool or dataset that many researchers start using
- A governance or audit method with technical evaluation details
- A discussion about failures, limitations, or deployment risks

Weak signals:

- Pure product announcements
- Model leaderboard posts without evaluation details
- General AI opinion pieces without methods, datasets, or concrete evidence
- Safety claims that do not define evaluation criteria

## Daily Trend Selection

Each daily note should include 3 technical discussions.

Try to balance:

- 1 paper or benchmark discussion
- 1 platform or tool discussion
- 1 lab blog or governance/evaluation discussion

Do not overuse the same source for several days unless it is central to a research line.
