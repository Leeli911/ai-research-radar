## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Superclass-Guided Representation Disentanglement for Spurious Correlation Mitigation
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08570
Year / Venue: 2025 arXiv preprint, revised 2026-03-19
Source: official arXiv metadata page, verified 2026-07-12
Research line: representation disentanglement for spurious correlation mitigation and worst-group robustness
Research function: Explanation / Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 21/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference with proposal value

#### English Summary

- Problem: models trained on biased visual data often rely on spurious features that correlate with labels in training but fail under distribution shift, creating poor worst-group performance that average accuracy can hide.
- Method: the paper proposes SupER, a superclass-guided representation disentanglement method. It uses superclass information from a pretrained vision-language model to guide attention toward more stable semantic information and reduce reliance on spurious cues.
- Evaluation: the method is evaluated on standard spurious-correlation and domain-generalization settings, with attention to both average performance and robustness on challenging groups or shifted domains.
- Findings: SupER improves robustness under spurious correlation shift, especially when the model must generalize beyond the biased feature-label associations seen during training.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: 模型在训练集中可能把背景、纹理、风格或其他 shortcut 当作目标证据。一旦测试分布变化，这些 shortcut 不再稳定，某些 subgroup 或 domain 的表现会明显下降，但平均 accuracy 可能仍然不错。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 中 historical portrait age estimation 也可能存在类似问题。模型可能不是稳定地学习年龄线索，而是把图像质量、时代风格、性别相关视觉特征或拍摄条件当作年龄证据。SupER 提醒我，hidden reliability degradation 可以被理解为 target representation 和 nuisance representation 没有分开。

技术直觉: 如果类别可以放入更高层的 semantic superclass，模型可以借助更稳定的语义方向来减少对 spurious feature 的依赖。对我的研究而言，这相当于问: 年龄估计模型的 embedding 是否真的围绕 age evidence 组织，还是被 archive/source/style 这些历史域因素牵引。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: SupER directly targets cases where average performance hides worst-group degradation caused by spurious correlations. This parallels my finding that global MAE hid elderly-group failure.
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits may be more vulnerable if age evidence is entangled with historical image artifacts, gendered appearance cues, or low-quality scans.
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing age or gender counts may not remove spurious reliance. SupER suggests testing whether a balanced model still encodes source/style shortcuts that harm elderly samples.
- Cascade fragility: my gender-conditional cascade may become fragile when gender features and age evidence are entangled. A representation-disentanglement view helps explain why hard conditional routing can amplify shortcut errors.
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits contain domain-specific visual style, restoration, and acquisition artifacts. SupER provides a conceptual template for separating stable age evidence from shifted archival nuisance features.

#### Limitation

The paper focuses on classification-style spurious correlation benchmarks. I would need to adapt the idea carefully for continuous age regression and for historical portraits where superclasses and confounders are not always cleanly labeled.

#### Follow-up

Audit thesis embeddings for spurious structure: compare age, gender, source, image-quality, and era-related directions, then test whether disentanglement or nuisance-aware regularization reduces elderly underestimation without worsening other age groups.

### Paper 2

Title: CompDiff: Hierarchical Compositional Diffusion for Fair and Zero-Shot Intersectional Medical Image Generation
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16551
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint, last revised 2026-07-08
Source: official arXiv metadata page, verified 2026-07-12
Research line: fair synthetic data generation for rare and intersectional medical-image subgroups
Research function: Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor

#### English Summary

- Problem: medical image generators can reproduce or amplify demographic imbalance, especially for rare intersectional groups. Synthetic balancing can therefore fail if the generator itself is unreliable for underrepresented combinations.
- Method: CompDiff uses hierarchical compositional diffusion with demographic conditioning. Instead of treating each intersectional subgroup as an isolated label, it composes demographic attributes in a structured way to improve zero-shot generation for rare combinations.
- Evaluation: the paper evaluates generated medical images with fidelity, subgroup fairness, and downstream classification measures, including attention to unseen or rare demographic intersections.
- Findings: the method improves fair and zero-shot intersectional generation compared with less structured conditioning approaches, suggesting that synthetic-data interventions need explicit subgroup-compositional evaluation.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: synthetic data 并不天然公平。如果原始数据中某些 demographic intersection 很少，生成模型也可能生成得更差，甚至把偏差复制到增强数据中。这样一来，看似是在做 balancing，实际可能只是制造了更多低质量或不真实的 vulnerable-subgroup 样本。

为什么重要: 这和我的 thesis 里 "balancing sometimes worsened elderly-group performance" 很直接相关。我的结果说明，简单增加某些 group 的样本数不一定提高可靠性。CompDiff 进一步说明，如果补充数据的生成机制本身对 rare subgroup 不稳定，balancing 可能会把问题放大。

技术直觉: hierarchical compositional conditioning 把 demographic attributes 当作可组合结构，而不是把每个交叉 subgroup 当作完全独立类别。这有助于模型把 age、sex、race 等属性组合到未见过或稀有的 intersection 上。对我的历史人像任务而言，类似思想可以用于分析 "elderly + female + low-quality archive + old photographic style" 这类组合是否被可靠覆盖。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: a synthetic-data pipeline may improve overall performance while still generating poor-quality samples for rare intersections. This matches my concern that global MAE can hide subgroup failure.
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly portraits are likely to be a rare or visually heterogeneous subgroup. Synthetic augmentation could help only if elderly-specific and elderly-intersectional fidelity is verified.
- Balancing trade-offs: CompDiff is directly relevant to my finding that balancing sometimes worsened elderly performance. It suggests that the question is not only how many samples are added, but whether added samples preserve subgroup-relevant evidence.
- Cascade fragility: if synthetic data changes the visual distribution differently across demographic intersections, a cascaded gender-conditional model may learn sharper but less stable routes for certain age-gender combinations.
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits include age, gender, era, archive source, and image-quality interactions. A compositional view is useful for diagnosing whether historical-domain coverage is missing in rare intersections.

#### Limitation

The paper is about medical image generation, not historical portrait age estimation. I would need to treat it as a data-centric intervention reference rather than a directly reusable model.

#### Follow-up

Design a thesis-style balancing audit: compare original balancing, synthetic augmentation, and subgroup-compositional augmentation by checking elderly MAE, underestimation rate, calibration, image-quality slices, and intersectional age-gender-source reliability.

### Paper 3

Title: MedQ-Deg: A Multidimensional Benchmark for Evaluating MLLMs Across Medical Image Quality Degradations
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07769
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint, submitted 2026-03-08
Source: official arXiv metadata page, verified 2026-07-12
Research line: degradation-aware reliability and calibration evaluation for multimodal medical models
Research function: Diagnosis / Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 20/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: evaluation benchmark / method reference

#### English Summary

- Problem: multimodal medical models may answer confidently on degraded images even when image quality should make the decision uncertain. Aggregate benchmark scores can miss this degradation-specific reliability failure.
- Method: MedQ-Deg builds a multidimensional benchmark over medical image-quality degradations and evaluates multimodal large language models using performance and confidence/calibration-oriented measures.
- Evaluation: the paper tests models across multiple degradation types and severity levels, examining not only answer accuracy but also confidence behavior under visual quality shift.
- Findings: the benchmark exposes reliability and calibration weaknesses under image degradation, showing that model confidence can remain poorly aligned with actual visual evidence quality.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: 医学多模态模型在图像质量下降时，可能仍然给出很自信的回答。也就是说，问题不只是 accuracy 下降，而是模型是否知道自己在 degradation 下不可靠。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 中历史人像本身就有图像质量和域变化问题，例如扫描质量、模糊、姿态、修复痕迹、时代风格。MedQ-Deg 提醒我，historical domain shift 可以被具体拆成质量退化维度，而不是只写成抽象的 domain shift。

技术直觉: 如果模型真正对证据质量敏感，那么随着图像 degradation 变强，它的误差、uncertainty、calibration 应该有可解释变化。如果 confidence 不随证据质量变化，就会出现 hidden reliability degradation: 模型看似稳定，实际是在不可靠条件下过度自信。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: global MAE or benchmark accuracy can hide degradation-defined failure slices. A model may work on clean portraits but fail on blurred, low-contrast, or restoration-heavy historical images.
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly samples may be overrepresented in harder visual conditions or may require finer texture evidence. Image-quality degradation could therefore interact with elderly underestimation.
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing by age or gender alone may not balance image quality. MedQ-Deg suggests checking whether balancing changes quality distributions and inadvertently worsens elderly reliability.
- Cascade fragility: a cascade can fail silently when upstream gender prediction or downstream age regression remains overconfident on degraded images. Degradation-aware calibration can expose this failure path.
- Historical domain shift: the paper maps naturally to historical portraits because archival data shift often appears through quality degradation, compression, restoration, and acquisition artifacts.

#### Limitation

MedQ-Deg focuses on multimodal medical QA rather than regression or biometric evaluation. I should use it mainly as an evaluation-design reference for degradation-aware reliability, not as a direct method for age estimation.

#### Follow-up

Create a portrait-degradation stress test: apply controlled blur, contrast, compression, resolution, and restoration-style perturbations, then measure global MAE, elderly MAE, residual skew, calibration error, and cascade route instability across degradation severity.

## 3. Two Research Questions

### Question 1

English:

- Research question: Does balancing historical portrait training data by age and gender worsen elderly-group reliability when image quality and source-domain composition remain imbalanced?
- Why it matters: my thesis found that balancing sometimes worsened elderly-group performance, but the mechanism may be hidden composition shift rather than balancing itself.
- Possible data: thesis portrait images, age and gender labels, archive/source metadata, image-quality indicators, model predictions, residuals, and balanced versus unbalanced training variants.
- Possible method: compare naive age/gender balancing with composition-aware balancing that also controls image quality or source-domain proxies; analyze elderly MAE and calibration across intersectional slices.
- Evaluation metric: elderly MAE, elderly underestimation rate, worst-intersection MAE, subgroup ECE, residual variance, and model ranking stability across source-quality strata.
- Thesis connection: directly tests aggregate metric masking, elderly-group degradation, balancing trade-offs, and historical domain shift.
- Risk or limitation: source and quality metadata may be incomplete, so I may need robust proxy features from embeddings or image-quality estimators.

中文:

研究问题: 当 image quality 和 source-domain composition 仍然不平衡时，只按 age/gender 做 balancing 是否会恶化 historical portrait age estimation 中的 elderly-group reliability？

这个问题可以把我的 thesis 里的 balancing trade-off 变成一个可测试机制: balancing 失败可能不是因为 balancing 本身无效，而是因为 intervention 对错了 reliability unit。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: Can degradation-aware calibration and spurious-feature diagnostics predict elderly underestimation before aggregate MAE changes in historical facial age estimation?
- Why it matters: my thesis showed that residual diagnostics revealed hidden elderly failure; the next step is to test earlier signals based on visual degradation and representation shortcut reliance.
- Possible data: historical portrait images, controlled degradation variants, model confidence, embeddings, residuals, gender predictions, source labels, and age-group labels.
- Possible method: build a degradation stress test, compute prediction drift and calibration error across severity levels, and test whether spurious-feature indicators or representation shifts predict elderly underestimation.
- Evaluation metric: global MAE versus elderly MAE gap, subgroup ECE, degradation-slope of MAE, AUROC for high-error detection, cascade route instability, and confidence-error correlation.
- Thesis connection: extends aggregate metric masking, elderly-group degradation, cascade fragility, and historical domain shift into a monitoring framework.
- Risk or limitation: artificial degradations must be historically plausible; otherwise the test may measure unrealistic perturbation sensitivity rather than real archival shift.

中文:

研究问题: degradation-aware calibration 和 spurious-feature diagnostics 能否在 aggregate MAE 改变之前，提前预测 historical facial age estimation 中的 elderly underestimation？

这个问题适合我的背景，因为它结合了 computer vision、calibration、residual diagnostics、subgroup reliability 和 historical domain shift，不需要跳到陌生的系统工程方向。
