## 1. Today's 3 Selected Papers

### Paper 1

Title: Beyond Metadata: CAPRA: Vision-Centric Diagnosis and Mitigation of Subgroup Disparities in Medical AI
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09102
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint, submitted 2026-07-12
Source: official arXiv record and PDF, verified 2026-07-16
Research line: hidden subgroup disparity diagnosis when demographic or acquisition metadata are incomplete
Research function: Diagnosis / Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 24/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: proposal anchor and method reference

#### English Summary

- Problem: subgroup auditing in medical AI often depends on demographic, acquisition, or image-quality metadata, but those variables may be missing, noisy, or incomplete; as a result, aggregate performance can hide clinically meaningful subgroup disparities.
- Method: the paper proposes CAPRA, a calibrated proxy-axis framework that derives semantic axes from image content, calibrates those axes using a small metadata-labeled subset with patient-level cross-fitting, and uses the resulting soft subgroup structure for failure diagnosis and robust learning.
- Evaluation: the authors evaluate CAPRA on fundus, dermoscopy, and chest-radiography tasks, including settings with dataset shift and incomplete metadata.
- Findings: CAPRA improves detection of clinically meaningful subgroup disparities and supports mitigation that can outperform metadata-only slicing, image-only approaches, and latent-slice baselines, especially under shift and metadata scarcity.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: 很多 subgroup auditing 假设我有可靠的 demographic、acquisition 或 image-quality metadata，但真实数据里这些标签可能缺失、不完整，或者和视觉失败机制不完全一致。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 使用 age 和 gender 做 subgroup evaluation，但 historical portraits 里真正的 failure 可能还和 archive source、era、scan quality、pose、restoration style 相关。CAPRA 提醒我，显式 metadata 只是 subgroup reliability 的一部分，视觉内容本身也可以定义 proxy reliability axes。

技术直觉: CAPRA 不把 subgroup 当成固定标签，而是从图像中学习可校准的 proxy axes。少量 metadata 可以用来校准这些轴，随后这些轴就能在更多无标签样本上定位潜在失败区域。这个思路比只按 age/gender 切片更接近 hidden reliability degradation。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: CAPRA直接针对平均性能掩盖 subgroup disparity 的问题。它把隐藏失败从全局指标中拆出来，与我的 global MAE 掩盖 elderly-group degradation 完全一致。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly failure 可能只是一个可见切片，背后还可能混合 source、quality、historical style 和 facial visibility。CAPRA 的 proxy axes 可以帮助我从视觉证据中寻找比 age label 更细的 vulnerable slice。
- Balancing trade-offs: 如果 balancing 只基于 age/gender metadata，就可能错过真正导致 error 的视觉轴。CAPRA 提供一种更细的 intervention target，解释为什么我 thesis 里的 balancing 有时会伤害 elderly group。
- Cascade fragility: 我的 gender-conditional cascade 依赖显式 gender route。CAPRA 让我重新思考: 如果路由变量不是主要 failure axis，conditional routing 可能把错误集中到被错误定义的路径上。
- Historical domain shift: historical portraits 的 metadata 可能缺失或不可比，视觉风格却强烈变化。CAPRA 的 calibrated proxy-axis 设计自然适合 source、era、scan quality 和 portrait style shift 的诊断。

#### Limitation

CAPRA主要在医学影像分类任务中验证。我的 age estimation 是连续回归任务，需要把 subgroup disparity 从 accuracy/AUC 扩展到 MAE、underestimation rate、residual skew、prediction interval coverage 和 calibration error。

#### Follow-up

Prototype a CAPRA-style diagnostic for my thesis embeddings: use a small set of known age/gender/source/quality proxies to calibrate visual axes, then test whether those axes reveal high-residual elderly or historical-style slices that global MAE hides.

### Paper 2

Title: RISE: Interactive Visual Diagnosis of Machine Learning Fairness Using Residuals
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04339
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint, submitted 2026-02-05
Source: official arXiv record and PDF, verified 2026-07-16
Research line: residual-based fairness diagnosis under group and domain differences
Research function: Diagnosis / Explanation / Monitoring / Evaluation
Screening Score: 21/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for residual diagnostics and visual monitoring

#### English Summary

- Problem: common fairness diagnostics often depend on a small set of predefined metrics and can make it hard to inspect how residual errors vary across protected attributes, domains, and interaction effects.
- Method: the paper introduces RISE, an interactive visual analytics system that centers fairness diagnosis on residuals, supporting residual distribution comparison, subgroup exploration, and iterative inspection of model errors.
- Evaluation: the authors describe RISE through fairness-analysis workflows and case studies that compare residual behavior across groups and domains rather than relying only on aggregate accuracy.
- Findings: residual-centered visualization can reveal distributional patterns, group disparities, and domain-specific error structures that are not visible from single-number fairness or performance metrics.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: fairness evaluation 经常被压缩成几个指标，但真正有用的信息常常在 residual distribution 里，尤其是误差的方向、尾部、方差和 subgroup interaction。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 里 residual diagnostics 是最能说明 elderly underestimation 的证据之一。RISE 把 residual 作为 fairness diagnosis 的核心对象，正好支持我把 "看 residual plot" 上升为一个更系统的 evaluation method。

技术直觉: 如果 subgroup failure 是不均匀发生的，那么只看平均误差会丢掉 residual 的形状信息。RISE 的思路是把 residual distribution、group slices 和 domain differences 放在同一个交互式分析流程中，让隐藏的误差结构更容易被发现。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: RISE 把 residual distribution 作为核心诊断对象，直接回应我 thesis 中 global MAE 无法显示 elderly underestimation 和高方差的问题。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly group 的失败不仅是 MAE 更高，也可能表现为系统性低估、残差偏斜和尾部风险。RISE 的 residual-first analysis 能更清楚地表达这种 failure pattern。
- Balancing trade-offs: balancing 后如果 global MAE 或 group mean 改善，residual shape 仍可能恶化。RISE 提醒我比较 balancing 前后的 residual skew、tail error 和 group-domain interaction，而不是只比较平均指标。
- Cascade fragility: cascaded conditional models 可能在某个 route 内产生特殊 residual pattern。RISE 的诊断方式可以帮助我检查 gender route 是否改变了 elderly residual distribution。
- Historical domain shift: historical portrait domains 可以按 archive、era、image quality 或 restoration status 分层。RISE 的 domain-aware residual inspection 可以直接适配 historical domain shift 分析。

#### Limitation

RISE更像诊断和可视化系统，不是自动纠错方法。它对我的研究价值在于把 residual diagnostics 形式化，而不是直接提供新的 robust training algorithm。

#### Follow-up

Use RISE as a design reference for a thesis reliability dashboard: compare residual distributions by age group, gender, source, image quality, and model family; explicitly track underestimation rate and tail residuals under shift.

### Paper 3

Title: FairSelect: A Systematic Evaluation of Intervention Selection Across Intersectional Subgroups
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08953
Year / Venue: 2026 arXiv preprint, submitted 2026-07-12
Source: official arXiv record and PDF, verified 2026-07-16
Research line: choosing fairness interventions across intersectional clinical subgroups
Research function: Explanation / Intervention / Evaluation
Screening Score: 22/25 using `skills/paper_screening.md`
Usefulness: method reference for balancing and intervention trade-off analysis

#### English Summary

- Problem: no single fairness intervention reliably works for every dataset, metric, model, or subgroup, especially when subgroup definitions are intersectional and evaluation is clinically constrained.
- Method: the paper proposes FairSelect, a systematic protocol for evaluating and selecting fairness interventions across intersectional subgroups, using both performance and fairness criteria instead of assuming one mitigation method is universally best.
- Evaluation: the authors evaluate multiple fairness-intervention strategies on structured clinical prediction tasks, with attention to subgroup intersections and metric trade-offs.
- Findings: intervention effectiveness varies substantially across datasets, subgroup definitions, and metrics; systematic intervention selection is necessary because a method that improves one subgroup or metric can fail or degrade another.

#### 中文解释

这篇论文解决的问题是: fairness intervention 不能默认 "选一个常用方法就好"。在 intersectional subgroup 下，不同 intervention 可能带来完全不同的 performance-fairness trade-off。

为什么重要: 我的 thesis 发现 balancing 有时会让 elderly group 更差，这正是 intervention selection 的问题。FairSelect 让我把这个观察从 "一次实验的意外结果" 重新理解为: fairness intervention 需要按 subgroup、metric、domain 和 downstream decision context 系统选择。

技术直觉: FairSelect 的核心不是提出一个更复杂的模型，而是把 intervention choice 本身变成 evaluation object。它强调在多个 subgroup intersection 和多个指标下比较 intervention，而不是只报告一个总体 fairness improvement。

#### Connection to My Thesis

- Aggregate metric masking: 如果只看 global MAE 或 global fairness score，可能看不到某个 intervention 对 elderly subgroup 的负面影响。FairSelect 强调跨 intersectional subgroups 的系统评估。
- Elderly-group degradation: elderly group 可以进一步拆成 elderly-female、elderly-male、elderly-low-quality、elderly-specific-source 等切片。FairSelect 支持我评估哪些切片从 balancing 中受益，哪些切片受损。
- Balancing trade-offs: 这篇论文和我的 balancing finding 直接相关。它提供一个 intervention-selection 视角: balancing 不是天然公平，而是一种需要和重加权、阈值、校准或模型选择一起比较的策略。
- Cascade fragility: conditional routing 也是一种 intervention。FairSelect 让我把 cascade 当成被比较的 intervention，而不是默认更精细的 architecture；需要检查它是否只在某些 intersectional slices 上有效。
- Historical domain shift: historical portrait data 的 subgroup structure 会随 source、era 和 image quality 改变。FairSelect 的系统选择框架可以扩展到 source-shifted 或 time-shifted validation sets。

#### Limitation

FairSelect主要基于结构化临床预测任务。我的视觉回归场景需要重新定义公平和可靠性指标，例如 worst-slice MAE、underestimation rate、prediction interval coverage 和 subgroup-specific calibration。

#### Follow-up

Use FairSelect as a template for comparing unbalanced training, age/gender balancing, reweighting, calibration, and cascade routing in my thesis setting under the same subgroup and historical-shift evaluation grid.

## 3. Two Research Questions

### Question 1

English:

- Research question: Can calibrated visual proxy axes identify hidden high-residual elderly/source/style slices in historical facial age estimation when explicit metadata are incomplete?
- Why it matters: my thesis showed elderly-group degradation, but age alone may not be the true reliability axis; historical source, image quality, restoration style, and facial visibility may define hidden slices.
- Possible data: thesis portrait images, age labels, gender labels, archive/source metadata if available, image-quality proxies, model embeddings, and prediction residuals.
- Possible method: learn or extract visual proxy axes from embeddings, calibrate them using the small set of available metadata, form soft slices, and compare residual distributions across these slices under source or historical-period shift.
- Evaluation metric: global MAE, elderly MAE, worst-slice MAE, residual skew, underestimation rate, slice stability across model seeds, and overlap between proxy slices and available metadata.
- Thesis connection: directly extends aggregate metric masking, elderly-group degradation, and historical domain shift into a hidden subgroup discovery problem.
- Risk or limitation: proxy axes may be difficult to interpret if visual factors are entangled or if metadata coverage is too sparse for calibration.

中文:

研究问题: 在 explicit metadata 不完整的 historical facial age estimation 中，calibrated visual proxy axes 能否识别隐藏的 high-residual elderly/source/style slices？

这个问题可测试，因为我已经有 embeddings、residuals、age/gender labels 和可能的 source/quality proxies。它把我的 thesis 从 age-group evaluation 推进到 hidden reliability-axis discovery。

### Question 2

English:

- Research question: Under historical portrait domain shift, when do balancing and conditional-routing interventions reduce worst-slice residual error, and when do they worsen residual skew or subgroup calibration?
- Why it matters: my thesis found that balancing sometimes worsened elderly performance and cascaded gender-conditional models were fragile; this question tests those findings under a systematic intervention-selection protocol.
- Possible data: original and balanced thesis datasets, regression models, multi-task models, cascaded conditional models, subgroup labels, proxy slices, prediction residuals, and calibration outputs.
- Possible method: compare intervention families using the same subgroup grid: unbalanced training, age/gender balancing, sample reweighting, post-hoc calibration, and conditional routing; evaluate each under source/style/time splits.
- Evaluation metric: elderly MAE gap, worst-slice MAE, residual skew, underestimation rate, subgroup calibration error, route instability, and intervention harm rate where a method improves aggregate MAE but worsens a vulnerable slice.
- Thesis connection: directly tests balancing trade-offs, cascade fragility, aggregate metric masking, elderly degradation, and historical domain shift.
- Risk or limitation: intervention comparisons may be sensitive to small subgroup sizes, so uncertainty intervals or repeated splits are needed before claiming robust effects.

中文:

研究问题: 在 historical portrait domain shift 下，balancing 和 conditional-routing 什么时候降低 worst-slice residual error，什么时候反而恶化 residual skew 或 subgroup calibration？

这个问题把我的 thesis 里最关键的两个负面发现系统化: balancing 和 cascade 都不能只看总体 MAE，而要看它们对 vulnerable slices 的 residual 和 calibration 后果。
